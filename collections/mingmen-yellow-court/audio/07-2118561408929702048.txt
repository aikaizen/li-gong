[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Waking Mingmen and the kidneys to form the Yellow Court: a step-by-step method. A post from November 6. [break] For the next ten days, during the Jia-Shen decade (Jia-Shen to Gui-Si), it is a good time to activate the kidneys and Mingmen, guided by the Ding-Hai Jade Maiden.

Here, step by step, is an integrated practice method that awakens Mingmen (the Former Heaven original qi, kundalini) and draws out the Former Heaven qi of the kidneys (kidney fire, the energy of the hun soul) to unite them in the Yellow Court.

Step 1. Preparation:

Opening the palm (Laogong) and pre-warming the kidney and small-intestine switch: This is the preliminary work, before you start drawing breath in through the bones, to drain the cold and open the kidney circuit.

Opening Laogong with thumb and index finger touching (vibration contemplation): Lightly touch the tips of the thumb (sympathetic) and index finger (parasympathetic) of both hands, rest them on your knees, and feel your heartbeat.

Let the whole palm throb, "thum, thum, thum," as the cold (cold wind) of the organs drains out between the fingers, and let a warm ball of energy the size of a ping-pong ball form over the center of the palm (Laogong).

Bending the 3rd, 4th and 5th fingers and the sound "bi—eup" (direct stimulation of the kidneys and small intestine):: To first wake the blocked energy of the kidneys and small intestine, slowly bend the middle, ring and little fingers (fingers 3, 4, 5) while voicing "bi—eup." This vibration first shakes awake the karmic imprints and cold in the small intestine, the trapezius and both kidneys.

[break] Step 2.

First method: the double intake of the in-breath through Laogong (the bones) and the skin of the whole body (charging the Mingmen battery)

This is the process in which "on the in-breath, breath comes in through Laogong and the bones and gathers in Mingmen." It is the core stage in which Later Heaven qi is used as kindling to set fire to the Former Heaven original qi (kundalini) of Mingmen.

In-breath (Laogong to arm bones to vertebrae to Mingmen, together with whole-body skin breathing):

1) Laogong-into-the-bone intake: As you breathe in, suck the energy ball in through the opening of Laogong in the palm as if snatching it, and draw it down in one go through the wrist bones to forearm bones to shoulder blades to Dazhui (T 1), then along the core of the vertebrae to Mingmen (L 2 to L 3).

2) Skin-to-spine intake: At the same time as you draw energy in through Laogong, let the skin of the whole body draw the energy of the outer space (Later Heaven qi) into the center of the spine, as if tightening toward the spinal column, and join it at Mingmen with the energy from Laogong.

Out-breath (settling at Mingmen with the sound "Om," or returning to Laogong):

Early stage (while purifying the spine): On the out-breath, send the energy back from Mingmen through the spine to Dazhui to arm bones to both palms (Laogong), opening the channel inside the bones (microtubules, periosteum) back and forth.

[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Advanced stage (when charging Mingmen intensively): On the out-breath, voice "Om—" so that it resonates in the center of the chest, and watch as the energy gathered on the in-breath resonates strongly at Mingmen (and at the four points: lower dantian, Lingtai and the center) and is amplified into a hot mass the size of a basketball. (At this point the marrow temperature rises, and the cold of the bladder and hip joints drains out through the lower body and between the toes.)

[break] Step 3.

Second method: back-and-forth breathing between the lower dantian and Mingmen, and activating the kidneys (forming the Yellow Court). Once Mingmen has caught fire, this is the process in which energy is moved back and forth through the slanted three-cun tube (cave) between the lower dantian at the front and Mingmen at the back, fully waking the kidneys on either side.

[break] It proceeds as follows, depending on your stage of practice and how you use the heels and abdominal pressure.

Led by awareness and a physical switch (the trajectory "in-breath: lower dantian, then Mingmen / out-breath: Mingmen to lower dantian")

In-breath (condensing from the lower dantian to Mingmen): As you breathe in, push the energy and abdominal pressure that came into the lower belly (lower dantian) back toward the spine (using the pull of the crura of the diaphragm on L 2 to L 3, linked with a switch of pressing the heels gently into the floor), compressing and concentrating the energy deep from the lower dantian into Mingmen. At this point the left and right kidneys and adrenal glands on either side of Mingmen are strongly pressed and stimulated.

Out-breath (spreading from Mingmen to lower dantian): As you breathe out (with the sound "Om" or a soundless micro-breath, plus a switch of giving the heels a little slack), let the Former Heaven original qi that was tightly compressed in Mingmen push out warmly through the three-cun channel from Mingmen to the lower dantian at the front, soaking the whole abdominal cavity (the enteric nervous system).

[break] Step 4.

The final fruit: purification of kundalini and its union with the kidneys' Former Heaven qi (kidney fire) to the birth of the Yellow Court

These are the physiological and energetic changes in the kidneys that occur as the energy travels back and forth between the lower dantian and Mingmen.

Purifying the coarse heat: The kundalini that first wakes in Mingmen (high sympathetic heat) is rough and hot, but as it moves back and forth between the lower dantian (acetylcholine, parasympathetic energy) and Mingmen while connected to the stillness of the center of the chest (no-mind Zen, no-thought Zen), its natural frequency calms, and it is refined into soft, gentle energy with no sense of anything foreign.

Extracting the kidneys' Former Heaven qi (the energy of the hun soul, the fire within water): The moment the kundalini of Mingmen settles quietly and softly, the "Former Heaven qi of the kidneys" (kidney fire) contained in the left and right kidneys on either side of L 2 to L 3 (the clusters of dense mitochondria in the tubules) is stimulated as if drawn by a magnet and comes out to the middle.

[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Gathering of the Yellow Court (the 11th stage): At the exact midpoint of the line connecting the lower dantian and Mingmen (in front of L 2 to L 3), "the purified kundalini of Mingmen and the One Thing of the lower dantian and the Former Heaven qi of the left and right kidneys" join into one, and the blazing brazier-fire of the Yellow Court is formed.

One-minute checklist for actual practice (summary)

1. Preparation: touch thumb and index finger and feel the pulse in the palms to after draining the cold from the fingers, make a warm ball in the palms.

2. In-breath (charging): breathe in simultaneously through Laogong in the palms (the arm bone to vertebra route) and the skin of the whole body, compressing the energy deep from the lower dantian into Mingmen at the back (L 2 to L 3).

3. Out-breath (circulating): with the sound "Om—" (or a fine out-breath), watch the warm energy of Mingmen and both kidneys push out through the tube to the lower dantian (keep the center of the chest down to the lower dantian quiet, as an "empty jar").

4. Condensing: when the back-and-forth movement becomes smooth, contemplate the energy of both kidneys gathering at the midpoint between the lower dantian and Mingmen (the Yellow Court) and a hot brazier-fire forming there.
