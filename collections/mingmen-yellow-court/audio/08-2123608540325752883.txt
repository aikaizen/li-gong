[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The in-breath that makes kindling for Mingmen. A post from November 20. [break] In the half-body small heavenly circuit and bone-purification system, the in-breath is not simply a time for drawing in air. It is a time for gathering the outer Later Heaven qi inward and making the "kindling" that wakes the Former Heaven original qi of Mingmen (L 2 to L 3) at the back.

The Former Heaven original qi is activated better together with the Later Heaven qi, the energy of the stars, than by waking on its own.

The core of the method that concentrates Later Heaven qi fastest is to join the following two flows together within a single in-breath.

Laogong to Mingmen (from the center of the palm to the axis of the spine): with the in-breath, draw the mass of energy gathered in both palms (Laogong) all at once through the arm bones, the shoulders and Dazhui, along the core of the spine to Mingmen.

Skin to spine and Mingmen (from the outer boundary of the body to the central pillar): at the same time, taking the spine as a single central pillar, let the outside energy tighten inward as if the skin were breathing, gathering into the core of the spine and Mingmen.

When the concentrated energy rising from the palms and the energy that wraps the whole surface of the skin and converges on the spine join at Mingmen within one breath, the Former Heaven original qi of Mingmen wakes dramatically, and the spark for the next stage, rib purification and the half-body small heavenly circuit, comes alive.

Rather than pushing it in with force, savor the sensation of both hands and the whole skin quietly gathering into a single point, Mingmen, at the place where the in-breath's expansion and convergence meet.
