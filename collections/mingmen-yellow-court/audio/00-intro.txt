[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Mingmen, Kidney Fire and the Yellow Court. [break] This is his map of the lower body, mostly following Guseon's method. Forcing qi into the lower belly over-drives the parasympathetic and can turn the gathered energy into yin-fire, cold that freezes the organs; detonating kundalini (his Former Heaven original qi) from below before the upper channels and the center are set can run wild. So the order is: Om and the organs first, then a still center in the chest, then the bones. Later Heaven qi is drawn in through the palms (Laogong), the arm bones and the whole skin to Mingmen at L 2 to L 3 as kindling. Breathing back and forth through the three-cun passage between the lower dantian and Mingmen wakes the kidneys' fire within water. Where Mingmen's purified kundalini, the One Thing of the lower dantian, kidney fire and the heart-fire that sank from the chest fuse, the Yellow Court forms: a brazier on which emotions melt like snowflakes. Once the circuits are open, absorption comes by itself. [break] 15 posts by Marici, translated from the Korean.
