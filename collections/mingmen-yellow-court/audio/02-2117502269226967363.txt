[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The danger of practice built only on relaxation and accumulating qi. A post from November 3. [break] In ordinary dantian breathing the lower dantian is treated as a vague pouch of energy. But if you forcibly push energy into the lower belly to accumulate qi, the parasympathetic nervous system becomes abnormally over-activated.

In that state, if the mind has not been trained, and in daily life emotions such as anger, stress or refusal flare up, or past trauma (karmic imprints) lodged in the large intestine is touched, then the over-concentrated cholinergic parasympathetic energy cannot produce an electric spark. Instead it abruptly undergoes a phase transition into a "pushing force" (yin qi).

As a result, all the Later Heaven qi you had gathered in the lower belly suddenly turns into a huge mass of cold, freezing the organs and sinking down to settle in the kidneys, the bladder and the Yongquan point. This is the yin-fire phenomenon, the "fire entering, demons possessing" that breathing practitioners so often suffer.
