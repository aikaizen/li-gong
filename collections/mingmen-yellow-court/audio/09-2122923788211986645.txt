[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Making the passage between the lower dantian and Mingmen. A post from November 18. [break] On the in-breath, raise the energy gathered in the lower dantian below the navel to Mingmen at L 2 to L 3; on the out-breath, send the energy out from Mingmen to the lower dantian. In this way you make a passage along which the dragon (the energy) comes and goes between Mingmen and the lower dantian.

As the energy comes and goes like this, it stimulates the kidneys, kidney-fire energy comes out of the kidneys, and, uniting with the Former Heaven qi of Mingmen, it forms the Yellow Court, where kundalini becomes active. It is the place in front of Mingmen, also called the true-qi point.
