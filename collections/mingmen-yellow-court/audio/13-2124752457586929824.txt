[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Energy practice: the fastest highway into absorption. A post from November 23. [break] If you try to get rid of thoughts or force yourself to contemplate when you meditate, you soon get exhausted. In the end you are trying to catch thoughts with thoughts, so only the frontal lobe tenses.

The biggest advantage of energy practice (the bottom-up approach) is that once you learn the flow of energy, you enter absorption by itself.

If you keep the body's energy circuits open, the lower dantian, Mingmen and the Yellow Court, the autonomic nerves descend into a deep parasympathetic stability and the power of the brain's distraction circuit (the DMN) switches off on its own. It is not pressing the waves down by hand; it is putting the wind itself to sleep.

Only, as the energy grows stronger, don't lose the "unbothered ground" that watches the flow calmly, so that you don't get drunk on the energy sensations or carried away.

In the end, the flow of energy is the fastest highway that goes straight to absorption without wrestling with distracting thoughts.
