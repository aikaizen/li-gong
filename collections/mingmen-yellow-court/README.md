*A collection of 15 posts by 마리지천 摩利支天 ([@z9_s8_y_9](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9)), Nov 3 – Nov 23, 2026 (Korea time), translated from the Korean.*

## What this cluster teaches

This is his map of the lower body, mostly following Guseon's method. Forcing qi into the lower belly over-drives the parasympathetic and can turn the gathered energy into yin-fire, cold that freezes the organs; detonating kundalini (his Former Heaven original qi) from below before the upper channels and the center are set can run wild. So the order is: Om and the organs first, then a still center in the chest, then the bones. Later Heaven qi is drawn in through the palms (Laogong), the arm bones and the whole skin to Mingmen at L2–L3 as kindling. Breathing back and forth through the three-cun passage between the lower dantian and Mingmen wakes the kidneys' fire within water. Where Mingmen's purified kundalini, the One Thing of the lower dantian, kidney fire and the heart-fire that sank from the chest fuse, the Yellow Court forms: a brazier on which emotions melt like snowflakes. Once the circuits are open, absorption comes by itself.
