[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The Membrane Path and the Finger Switches. [break] Where Daoist internal alchemy inflates the meridians, the Buddhist line from Bodhidharma's Sinew-Changing and Marrow-Washing Classic trains the membrane between bone and muscle as its main channel, because the membrane is where the body's microtubules are bundled most densely, and because the thin boundary between two structures is itself the physical image of the form of quiescent extinction. Breath, vocal resonance and original qi make the rib membrane swell without the old Shaolin sticks. The fingers are the switches: slowly bending the index finger (the parasympathetic and listening pathway) tugs a line up to the ear, straightens the spine through the vestibulospinal tract and drops the diaphragm; flexing the thumb toward the little finger reaches the chest and lungs; joining thumb and index finger balances the two branches.
