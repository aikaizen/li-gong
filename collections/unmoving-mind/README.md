# The Unmoving Mind: Autonomic Spillover and the Iron Wall
### 부동심 不動心 · 자율신경 범람

*A collection of 7 posts by 마리지천 摩利支天 ([@z9_s8_y_9](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9)), Nov 21 – Nov 27, 2026 (Korea time), translated from the Korean.*

## What this cluster teaches

The unmoving mind is not reached by calming down. The arousal system (sympathetic) and the quiescence system (parasympathetic) normally move like a seesaw, but pushed past a critical point, either one spills over into the other and both fire at full strength together. In the chest this spillover is felt first as the taut iron wall of the third dhyāna, the strong energy of Mingmen meeting the release of rib purification and Om. When the standoff passes its limit the wall collapses into emptiness and the bright nature of the fourth dhyāna. So he faults the wellness world's 'vagus hacks' as half the work: the dregs of emotion are lodged in the sympathetic chain beside the spine, especially at T1, and must be touched, allowed to heat up, and washed through with the seeing mind, with the forehead shining, the right atrium supporting from the front, and the back released. Zhongji, the inner face of the spine at T5, is the switch for the fourth dhyāna.
