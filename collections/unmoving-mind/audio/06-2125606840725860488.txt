[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The Iron Wall: training for the wind (English). A post from November 26. [break] Western meditation sells you “calmness”—feeling peaceful in a quiet room.

Traditional Korean somatic training aims for something much harder: the “Iron Wall”.

It has 3 neurological stages:

1. Calmness (quiet room) 2. Detachment (mild stress) 3. The Iron Wall (standing unshaken inside a blizzard)

You don’t build an Iron Wall by fighting the storm. You build it by anchoring your nervous system in your chest and spine until external chaos can’t penetrate your baseline.

[break] Stop training for quiet rooms. Train for the wind.
