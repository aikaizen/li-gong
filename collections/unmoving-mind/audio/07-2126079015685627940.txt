[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Zhongji: the switch for the fourth dhyāna. A post from November 27. [break] In traditional Buddhism the fourth dhyāna is the stage of absolute peace (purity of mindfulness through equanimity) in which the in-breath and out-breath stop (breath abiding) and even pleasure and pain are entirely cut off. Physiologically, in terms of practice, Zhongji (the ventral face of the spinal cord at T 5) sits at the switch that creates this state.
