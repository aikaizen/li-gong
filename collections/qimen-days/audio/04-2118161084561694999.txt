In other words, it is the decisive inflection point at which the potential of "Shen-You metal qi", compressed in a vacuum (void) for the past ten days, is unsealed in an instant and erupts into reality (leaving the void).

So powerful metal qi is set off, and events arise in which Shen and You are stirred, such as the Yin-Shen clash or the Mao-You clash.
