[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The Ding-You Jade Maiden and the breath of the chest. A post from November 7. [break] Today is the first day of the Ji-Hai month (Start of Winter) and a day related to the Ding-You Jade Maiden.

The Ding-You Jade Maiden has the image of "true metal refined by fire" (the purest gold and gems refined out by fire), where the starlight of heaven (Ding fire) meets the pure gem of earth (You metal).

The Ding-You Jade Maiden's three sacred titles, "Tongxuan Daode Zhenjun", "Zang Wengong, Shumi / Renjing" and "Weishen, Congkui," all contain the secret of internal alchemy: "through deep breathing of the chest (Danzhong and the lungs), draw in the profound mysterious qi of the universe and preserve the vitality and the immortal elixir within you" (Ding-You preserving life).

Danzhong and the lungs, which the Ding-You Jade Maiden influences, receive the clear breath of heaven (heavenly qi), govern the qi of the whole body (the lungs govern qi), and are the organs that hold the po soul, the body's actual life-support system (autonomic nerves, immunity, bodily sensation).
