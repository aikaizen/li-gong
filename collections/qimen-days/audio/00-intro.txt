[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Days of the Jade Maidens: Reading the Qimen Calendar in the Body. [break] Through early November he read each day's stem-branch through Qimen Dunjia and the Six Jia: which decade it belongs to, which Jade Maiden is on duty, which branches clash, combine or fall void, and how to turn a collision into a high-voltage spark by pairing the Six Ding Jade Maidens (yin, function) with the Six Jia Generals (yang, substance). He maps the palaces onto the abdomen and gives hand seals for the hours. These posts run from the Geng-Chen day through the change of decade on the Jia-Shen day to the Ding-Hai day, when the decade's own Jade Maiden descends, with asides on aged Pu'er chosen to match the day and a reading of Trump's chart on the Ren-Wu day.
