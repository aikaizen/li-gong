[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The method for opening the bright nature of the chest. A post from November 17. [break] The method for opening the bright nature of the chest. The energy of Mingmen comes to the chest, the still center. Since energy gathers where it finds stability, the chest must be still.

[break] The energy gathered this way travels on the in-breath along the breastbone to Lingtai at T 5.

[break] The energy that went to Lingtai goes on the out-breath in a straight line from Lingtai to the center.

In this way you let the energy of the front and back of the body communicate and purify the karmic imprints in the breastbone.

[break] When Lingtai is activated there is pain.
