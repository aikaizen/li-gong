[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] A reply from November 24. [break] Rather than trying to control the breath, it would be good to be aware of the breath happening and notice the movement of the ribcage. If you try to control the breath intentionally, carbon dioxide rises and a fight-or-flight response (sympathetic) can come out at the brainstem level. Rather than the breath, it's better to notice the ribcage widening and that force gathering at T 5 in the back.
