[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] A practice the Dragon Spirit is said to have taught: rib purification. A post from November 18. [break] Usually, when emotion surges, people say, "Breathe in deeply and send it down to the lower belly." But if you force the breath down while the chest (the middle dantian and the ribs) and the organs have not been purified, side effects arise.

The passion lodged in the chest (heat, sympathetic arousal) is pressed hard from top to bottom without ever being resolved, so instead the belly gets bloated, or the pressure rebounds and shoots up into the head: the rising-qi phenomenon.

As a result, the emotional wave that arose in the front of the chest easily gets trapped as it is in the intercostal nerves and rib fascia on both sides, and stiffens into a dull ache.

[break] Rather than sending it down, try breastbone purification.

Instead of stamping emotion down, you open a horizontal rotating circuit that crosses the cylinder of the ribcage from the front (the center) to the back (Lingtai), and refine the energy.

The congestion, the density of passion, lodged in the front of the chest (the center) rides the in-breath as the ribcage widens, travels along the outer edges of the ribs on both sides, and is scattered to Lingtai at the back (around the 5th thoracic vertebra).

Because this spreads emotional energy that was clumped in one spot and about to explode across the broad surface of the left and right ribs, and drains it out through the spinal line at the back, the chest opens up refreshingly at once, without suppression.

On the out-breath, the qi gathered at Lingtai at the back (around T 5, where sympathetic ganglia are dense and passion is stored) is drawn back into the center through the straight midline passage, together with the out-breath (and the sounding of "Om").

As this round trip repeats, the emotional turbid qi wedged in the ribs and the coarse heat of the sympathetic system are washed out, and a firm stillness that outside stimuli cannot shake (the iron wall of no-mind to breaking through to the arising of heart-fire) settles in the center.

When the front-to-back rotation of the ribcage (rib purification) is complete in this way, the ground of the chest is emptied like the void and a warm heart-fire arises. Then, without any straining to send it down, that soft energy flows down by itself, like the law of gravity, to the lower belly (the lower dantian, the Yellow Court).
