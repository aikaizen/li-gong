[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Rib Purification: Opening the Chest from Front to Back. [break] When emotion surges, the usual advice is to breathe it down into the belly. He teaches the reverse order: if the chest (the middle dantian and the ribs) has not been purified, pushing the breath down only presses the trapped heat lower, bloats the belly or sends qi rushing to the head. Rib purification opens a horizontal circuit across the cylinder of the ribcage instead. On the in-breath, the density gathered at the center of the chest spreads out along both rib arcs to Lingtai at T 5 on the back. On the out-breath, with Om, it returns in a straight line to the center. T 5 is where the cardiopulmonary sympathetic outflow ends and the visceral outflow begins, so widening and vibrating the rib muscles sends a 'safe now' signal back to that main switch. When the chest has emptied like open sky, warm heart-fire sinks by itself to the lower belly and the Yellow Court. Only then does abdominal breathing come.
