[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The misunderstanding of abdominal breathing. A post from November 21. [break] The misunderstanding of abdominal breathing: when people keep hearing "don't lift the upper chest," it is easy to freeze the whole ribcage like ice. But the only thing to forbid is "the vertical movement of shrugging the shoulders and hauling upward."

The middle ribs opening softly horizontally and backward is the ideal breathing mechanics, widening the diaphragm's range of motion and draining tension out to Lingtai at the back.
