[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] The Heart-Soul: Heartbeat, the Right Atrium and the Self. [break] The heart is treated as a little brain of its own and an endocrine organ of calm. Every heartbeat sends a pulse up to the brain that refreshes the sense of 'I'; when the beat slows and softens, the boundary between subject and object thins into no-mind. Systole is when the self forms and the body's intuitive warnings fire; the pulse can be felt between the touching thumb and index finger, where the self is seen being born and dying away. The practice is not to stare at the heart but to lay down ease in the center of the chest and add a warm longing, like a hen on her eggs. In the breath, open the front ribs and, resting for a moment at the end of the in-breath, feel venous blood fill the right atrium just right of the sternum. That passive filling triggers the vagal brake, ANP and oxytocin, and he calls it the awakening of the heart-soul. [break] 8 posts by Marici, translated from the Korean.
