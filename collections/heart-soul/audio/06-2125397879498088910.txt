[break] People with a busy mind and a chest that's all blocked up, please, just once tonight, take one breath into the "right side of your chest" (seriously, your head goes empty, it's a new world.)

This is something neurocardiology and classical practice both say: not the thumping heartbeat (on the left), but put your awareness 1 to 2 centimeters outside the right border of the sternum (the right atrium), and as you breathe in, just ride the feeling of warm blood filling up smoothly.

The ultra-sensitive sensors in the right atrial wall that catch even 1 to 2 millimeters of mercury of pressure (stretch receptors) switch on, then the vagal brake and the relief hormones (ANP, oxytocin) are released at the same time, then the distraction circuit (the DMN) is forced to shut down. That's the principle, apparently.

No need to force the belly to inflate or take big breaths: just drop the tension in the shoulders and tongue, open the front ribs a little, and rest for 1 to 2 seconds at the end of the in-breath. That's it.

Don't think of it as drinking air; feel it as warm water filling an open vessel. Seriously, try it, the chest settles right down and you go still instantly, it's insane
