[calm, unhurried, contemplative teacher reading a meditation text] Every heartbeat refreshes the self. A post from November 14. [break] According to brain science (Tallon-Baudry's team and others), the brain receives the heartbeat-evoked potential (HEP) that comes up with every beat of the heart in the ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) and the precuneus, and renews (refreshes) every second the first-person sense of self (selfhood): "ah, the 'I' who feels and thinks right now is here."

[break] With every beat, the heart sends the information called "I" and updates it.

When the heartbeat slows and softens, the impact of this "self-refresh pulse" thins, and without straining to empty yourself, you slide naturally into no-mind, where the boundary between subject (I) and object blurs.
