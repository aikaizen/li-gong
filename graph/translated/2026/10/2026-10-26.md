## Lower trigram Former Heaven, upper trigram Later Heaven
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2114491191849034064)

In internal alchemy, a Yijing hexagram is built with the lower part as Former Heaven (선천 先天) and the upper part as a Later Heaven (후천 後天) trigram.

Fire-Lake, Opposition (화택규 火澤睽 ䷥) has below the Former-Heaven Dui trigram (number 7 in Later Heaven) and above the Later-Heaven Li trigram (number 9).
