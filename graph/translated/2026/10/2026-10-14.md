## La and Yang: life force and the field of prosperity
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110273293987258770))

Tibetan proverbs and medical texts say: "When the body's *la* (life force) leaks away, the household's *yang* (wealth luck) scatters with it."

Sudden illness (*zané*), fraud, gossip and scandal, or big losses from a momentary error of judgment (tilt in trading, large stop-outs and liquidations) are all phenomena of a hole opening in the energy field and fortune leaking out.

In the traditional economic and spiritual ideas of Tibetans, the energy that gathers wealth is called "yang" (gYang, the magnetic field of luck and prosperity), and it is held to move as one body with "la" (Bla), the life-protecting membrane of the human body.

In traditional Tibetan cosmology and tantric economics, "yang" (gYang, གཡང་) is not merely a number in a bank account or money itself. Yang is "a high-density magnetic field that makes wealth, luck, and opportunity gather and stay by themselves" (Magnetic Field of Prosperity), and the "living essence of abundance" (Essence of Wealth) residing in material things.

In Tibetan shamanism (Bön) and tantra, yang is described as a very sensitive, fluid energy. Like steam or clear dew, it pools "only when the vessel is whole and sealed."

"Yang-gug" (gYang-'gugs, the ritual of summoning fortune 초복의례 招福儀禮) literally means "hooking the scattered yang (fortune) and pulling it back to lock it in tight." Practitioners and families capture this energy by combining two symbolic implements with one vessel of breath and body.

The bodily gesture that implements this instantly, in daily life and at the trading desk, is "the vajra fist: press the root of the ring finger (third joint) with the thumb and wrap it with the four fingers."

Drawing the market's opportunities and clear judgment into the lower belly (the treasure vase) with the in-breath and then closing the ring finger's gate by forming the vajra fist is itself a micro yang-gug ritual that locks in both my "la" (life force) and "yang" (assets, fortune) at once.

Form the mudra like this and ask for the blessing of the goddess Pema Shawari.

Of Pema Shawari's four manifestation colors, the form appearing as "brilliant gold (yellow)" is in charge of "increasing" (Gye-pa 增長: increasing and expanding lifespan, merit, wealth, and energy) among the four tantric activities (pacifying, increasing, magnetizing, subjugating).

The primary wealth blessing of Pema Shawari and the vajra-armor mantra (OM PEMA SHAWARI PHAT!) lies in "a perfect defensive wall against unexpected loss of assets and bankruptcy" (hedging). It connects to the principle that protecting the money you've earned is itself making money.

No matter how much profit (liquidity) comes in by Jambhala's blessing, if the practitioner's own nervous system (the white channel 백맥) is unstable and there's a hole in the "la" (life-protecting membrane), then in front of a single sharp market move (the appearance of Rahula) he'll get excited or panic and hand back to the market at a stroke all the "yang" (fortune) he's gathered.

Tibetan teachers insist that before doing the wealth practices of Yellow Jambhala or Vaishravana, one must first complete the "vajra armor" (Pema Shawari) or a protective boundary.

## Za Rahula, the black swan, and the healing goddess Pema Shawari
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110273293987258770) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110254262450278809))

Za Rahula (gZa' Rāhula, or Zachen Rahula) is "the uncontrolled anomaly of the cosmos, sudden violence, and the cold shadow that paralyzes life energy at a stroke."

He causes volatility like solar and lunar eclipses.
Like sharp swings in the economy.

That's because Rahula is "the incarnation of extreme volatility and the black swan," who appears without warning to swallow the sun and moon (eclipses) and in an instant paralyzes the nervous system or plunges it into madness.

To purify the sudden changes Za Rahula causes, one must receive the blessing of Pema Shawari.

Pema Shawari (Sanskrit *Parṇaśavarī*; Chinese 엽의불모 葉衣佛母, "Leaf-Clad Buddha Mother") is "the supreme healing goddess of epidemics, nerve toxins, and disturbances of the channels," presiding over the medicinal herbs and vitality of the deep primeval forest.

An indigenous shamanic goddess of the Śavara, an ancient Indian forest hunting tribe, sublimated into a tantric wisdom dakini (공행모), she symbolizes the purest, wildest natural immunity outside secular civilization.

If, when reciting the mantra, you also visualize her distinctive iconography (form) and the color of her light, then beyond mere sound vibration a powerful imagery healing occurs, to which the cranial nerves and endocrine system respond immediately.

Pema Shawari is visualized not as a gentle bodhisattva but as a strong, dynamic forest goddess with a slightly wrathful expression (semi-wrathful). Each ornament on her body and each implement in her hands symbolizes a healing mechanism of Tibetan medicine.

Mantra: OM PEMA SHAWARI PHAT (옴 페마 샤와리 펫)

## Za Rahula: the cosmic shadow who swallows the inner sun and moon
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110254262450278809) (quotes [the Oct 9 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2108340006981152989))

Za Rahula (gZa' Rāhula, or Zachen Rahula) functions beyond a mere protector: he is the most violent and deadly cosmic spiritual being, disturbing and controlling the energies of heaven and earth.

Just as the ancient Indian asura "Rahu" swallows the sun and moon and causes eclipses (식 蝕), Tibetan shamanism held that Rahula instantly swallows the inner sun and moon of the body (the left and right channels ida and pingala, or the life energy *lung*), cutting off qi and blood.

As in the ancient myth where Rahula (Rahu) swallows the sun and moon of the sky to cause eclipses, when Rahula's wave penetrates the crossing points (chakras) where the breath and qi of the left channel (moon / white channel) and right channel (sun / red channel) meet, an "inner eclipse" occurs.

That is, the energy exchange between the nervous system (white channel) and the vascular system (red channel) blacks out in an instant.

Rahula always holds a drawn bow and arrow. From the shamanic standpoint, if someone suddenly suffered a stroke, facial paralysis, hemiplegia, an epileptic seizure, or sudden madness, it was interpreted as being hit by Rahula's invisible poisoned arrow, or as having met the gaze of one of the thousand eyes in his body (the evil eye) and having one's life force frozen.

In Tibetan shamanic astrology (Nag-rtsis and dKar-rtsis), Rahula is a key variable governing the baleful influences (흉살 凶殺) of time and space.

On certain days it is strictly forbidden to dig the ground, cut down a big tree, or hold major events like weddings and funerals facing the direction where Rahula's gaze rests. It was believed that violating this exposed one to the poison (toxic breath) spewing from the mouths and eyes all over Rahula's body, bringing disaster on the family.

In modern terms, you'd say a market crash is also because Rahula's arrows rained down. ^^

He is "the uncontrolled anomaly of the cosmos, sudden violence, and the cold shadow that paralyzes life energy at a stroke."

Where Tibetan shamanism and traditional medicine (Sowa Rigpa) meet, Rahula's role appears as the most concrete, physical terror. Tibetans called acute nervous-system disorders of unknown cause "zané" (gZa'-nad, planetary illness) or "za-ab" (gZa'-babs, Rahula's strike / descent).

"Rahula's strike" (zané) in Tibet is a concept that captures, in highly symbolic yet precise physiological language, the phenomenon in which sudden electromagnetic and climatic changes in the outer cosmos (planetary energy) mesh with imbalances of the inner autonomic and vascular systems (reverse flow of lung and tripa) and shut down the neural network (white channel).

The greatness of Tibetan tantra lies in not excluding such a dangerous and capricious shamanic calamity-god, but enlisting him as the gatekeeper guarding the highest teaching (Dzogchen). Paradoxically, maintaining the purest, stillest primordial awareness (Rigpa) required the fiercest, lightning-like energy able to cut down outer interference and inner slackness instantly.

## The legend of Za Rahula, tamed by Padmasambhava
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110184559195336775) (quotes [the Oct 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2106518721544241484))

Rahula (Za Rahula) is not an object of popular devotion that ordinary Buddhists worship to pray for blessings. His nature carries an extremely dangerous and fierce energy that strikes the nervous system and causes eclipses.

Instead, he has been strictly honored as a "supreme protector" (Dharmapala) who guards the purity of the teaching and smashes practitioners' demonic obstacles in Nyingma, the oldest school of Tibetan Buddhism, and in its highest practice system, Dzogchen (the Great Perfection).

A very tragic and intense Tibetan local legend lies behind how the Indian Rahu, coming into Tibetan tantra, was transformed into the terrible, fierce wrathful deity "Za Rahula" seen in the image earlier.

In ancient Tibet (or northern India) there was a great yogi who devoted himself only to practice deep in the mountains. He was deeply respected by the queen (or a patron) of a kingdom and received offerings from her. But jealous courtiers falsely accused him to the king: "That practitioner is having an affair with the queen."

The enraged king, without investigating, seized the yogi, tortured him terribly, and burned him alive (or threw him into a pit of vipers). At the moment of death, the yogi, who had practiced purely his whole life, was seized by extreme rage at human folly and the injustice done to him.

"I will become the being who brings the most terrible calamities upon this foolish world!"

His fierce resentment combined with his amplified practice energy, and right after death he was reborn as "Za Rahula," a terrible demon king with nine heads, thousands of eyes, and a serpent's lower body. Carrying a bow and arrows, he flew through the sky shooting arrows that caused strokes, epilepsy, and terrible epidemics, and he became an object of terror who even blotted out the sun and moon.

When cosmic calamity broke out, the great teacher Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche), founder of Tibetan tantra, or Vajrapani, the incarnation of wrath, stepped in.

Padmasambhava did not destroy Rahula.

Instead, after subduing (조복) his vast energy of rage and karma with the power of the Dharma, he showed him the ultimate truth of the universe and awakened his original clear Buddha-nature.

Realizing that his rage came from dualistic illusion, Rahula knelt before Padmasambhava and vowed: "From now on I will use this mighty power not to destroy the Dharma but to crush the obstacles and enemies who obstruct the deepest teaching of tantra (Dzogchen)."

After that, Za Rahula became one of the three most powerful protectors of the Dzogchen tradition. The arrows he shoots are no longer calamities that sicken the innocent, but a weapon of compassion that pierces and kills exactly the "tangles of energy" and the life of the "ego" that obstruct a practitioner's awakening.

These stories show metaphorically that the energy Rahula governs is "suppressed fierce karma" and an "explosion of bioelectricity" that strikes the nervous system. Out of control, that powerful destructive force becomes stroke or paralysis (Rahula's arrows), but tuned by wisdom (Padmasambhava) it becomes the most powerful driving force to open the dimensional door of awakening. The essence of tantra is contained whole in this story.

Rahula mantra: Om Za Rahula Sarva Vighnan Shatrum Maraya Hum Phat!
