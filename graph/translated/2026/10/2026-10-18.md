## Green Jambhala and Great Exceeding ䷛
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111755777740595398) (quotes [the Oct 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111253190553559456))

This is the "Green Jambhala" (녹색 잠발라, green wealth deity), who brings wealth and business success at the speed of wind (the wind wheel 풍륜 風輪).

Not wealth you sit and wait for: this is the deity who resonates most powerfully with "work that must spread fast on the wind (networks and word of mouth, Xun ☴) and generate great liquidity (Dui ☱)," like a project I've planned, market distribution, a platform, or a token economy.

In Yijing terms, this is the deity whose frequency best matches *Lake-Wind, Great Exceeding* (택풍대과 澤風大過 ䷛: left hand Xun ☴, Fourth-Center Tanlang 사중탐랑 / right hand Dui ☱, Eighth-Center Bobi 팔중보필).

"Great Exceeding" (대과 大過) literally means "to exceed greatly, to overwhelmingly surpass ordinary standards."

The nickname of "Xun (巽 ☴)" itself in the Yijing and feng shui: "Xun is near profit, threefold in the market" (손위근리시삼배 巽爲近利市三倍): the wealth palace that triples market profit!

"Xun is wood, is wind, is entering… it goes to the market and gains profit nearby, multiplying it threefold (巽… 爲近利市三倍)."

In the southeast (position 4) sits the Xun trigram (巽 ☴, wind, eldest daughter, the breath's taking-in 부입).

The inner flesh (Former-Heaven Fuxi bagua): if you overlay the Former-Heaven bagua on that same southeast position, the trigram hidden at its base (root) is "Former-Heaven Dui (兌 ☱, lake, youngest daughter, primal joy and treasure)."

That is, activating the Xun palace affects the Former-Heaven Dui palace within it, and *Great Exceeding* (택풍대과 澤風大過 ䷛) is formed.

Would you try right now gently forming the Xun trigram (☴: index and middle touching the thumb) and taking one deep breath in?

An energy that activates wealth fortune forms.

## Za Rahula, the secret of the black sun
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111697807958823302) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110254262450278809))

[Za Rahula, the secret of the black sun]

Freshly made new-season tea is biased to one side, strong in green wood qi (목기 木氣) or sharp fire qi (화기 火氣). But old tea that has ripened well through thirty years of wind and frost has a tea nature (차성 茶性) that is neither cold nor hot: the perfect "central harmony (중화 中和), the energy of the spring and autumn equinoxes."

The equinoxes are also connected with solar and lunar eclipses.

In Tibetan tantra (especially vase breathing 보병기, tummo, and the secret teachings of Za Rahula), the core aim of practice is to stop the rough winds (karmic winds 업풍 業風) that flow divided in the left channel (moon, yin) and right channel (sun, yang), and push them into the central channel (avadhuti) in the very middle.

The moment the right-hand Dui trigram (☱), symbolizing Zuofu and Youbi (좌보우필, the sun and moon), meets the left-hand Zhen trigram (☳), symbolizing Za Rahula, and the left and right channels overlap at the center of the central channel (an inner solar and lunar eclipse), dualistic tension and afflictions vanish in an instant, and an inexpressible bliss bursts out: "great bliss" (대락 大樂, vast rapture) and "emptiness" (공성 空性, a stillness empty yet full) at once.

This sensation, meshed with the stillness after rain stops, is the experience of "bliss and emptiness are not two" (락공불이 樂空不二). This is the energy of Rahula, and the energy of the Celestial Worthy of the Nine Heavens, Responding to the Origin, Thunder-Sound, Universal Transformation (구천응원뇌성보화천존 九天應元雷聲普化天尊).

Gathering into the central channel like this is called *Lake-Thunder, Following* (택뢰수 澤雷隨 ䷐) in the Yijing.

The Later-Heaven position of thunder (Zhen) is originally, in Former Heaven, where the "sun (Li-fire ☲)" rises (the sun rising from Fusang 일출부상).

The Later-Heaven position of the lake (Dui) is originally, in Former Heaven, where the "moon (Kan-water ☵)" waxes (the moon rising over the western hills 월출서산).

Beneath the Later-Heaven Zhen palace (3) hides the Former-Heaven "sun (Li ☲)," and beneath the Later-Heaven Dui palace (7) hides the Former-Heaven "moon (Kan ☵)"!

So the moment you form the Zhen trigram (☳) with the left hand and the Dui trigram (☱) with the right to make *Following* (䷐), outwardly thunder and lake (Zhen and Dui = spring and autumn equinox) unite. But in the inner Former-Heaven body, the "sun (Li ☲)" of due east and the "moon (Kan ☵)" of due west are drawn into the very center (palace 5, the central palace, the central channel) and overlap face to face: "sun and moon joined like a jade disc" (일월합벽 日月合璧), a total solar and lunar eclipse!

In all the internal-alchemy classics, including the *Zhouyi cantong qi* (주역참동계), the *Wuzhen pian* (오진편), and the *Yonghobigyeol* (용호비결), when circulating the abdominal orbit, passing the eastern Mao position (palace 3 Zhen, spring equinox) and the western You position (palace 7 Dui, autumn equinox) is called "bathing" (목욕 沐浴) and treated as most important.

The hot yang qi rising after the fire is kindled at the winter solstice (palace 1 Kan) (advancing the fire 진화 進火) reaches its mildest climate at the eastern Zhen palace (3, Mao, spring equinox), and after passing the summer solstice (palace 9 Li) and reaching the western Dui palace (7, You, autumn equinox), it becomes clear and cool like an autumn sky.
The classics teach: "At the positions of the spring equinox (Zhen) and autumn equinox (Dui), don't force the breath (the bellows) tight or push it. Empty the mind and let the qi wrap around and wash itself (bathing and warm nurturing 목욕온양)."

In terms of seasons, the perfect mildness of the spring and autumn equinoxes, neither cold nor hot;

astronomically, Za Rahula's solar and lunar eclipse, sun (Former-Heaven Li) and moon (Former-Heaven Kan) becoming one at the node (opening of the central channel);

in five-phase terms, the Chen-You combination into metal (진유합금, the condensation of Vajrasattva), in which eastern wood (Zhen, liver-gallbladder) and western metal (Dui, lungs-large intestine) lock together at the navel center (central palace 5).

Since all happen at once, the nervous system responds immediately and blossoms into rapture (great bliss 대락 大樂), the entrance to deep samadhi.

## Zhen and Xun: the only two switches we can grasp
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111646439344636170) (quotes [the Oct 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111253190553559456))

When you overlay the Möbius flow of the Former-Heaven bagua (Qian 1 → Dui 2 → Li 3 → Zhen 4 ↔ Xun 5 → Kan 6 → Gen 7 → Kun 8) and the Möbius flow of the Later-Heaven nine palaces (Kan 1 → Kun 2 → Zhen 3 → Xun 4 ↔ [central palace 5] ↔ Qian 6 → Dui 7 → Gen 8 → Li 9), the only codes that guard "the junction of dimensional transition" (just before and after the central crossing point) on both tracks are Zhen (震 ☳, thunder) and Xun (巽 ☴, wind).

Of the eight energies of the bagua, the only tools of the phenomenal world that a practitioner can grasp directly with waking awareness and use to start the engine are Zhen and Xun.

Breath (the bellows): in internal alchemy, Xun is "the wind of the breath" (the wind wheel 풍륜 風輪) entering and leaving through the nose and diaphragm. I can't touch the fire (Li) or water (Kan) of the dantian directly with my hands, but the bellows of breath (Xun-wind ☴) I can regulate by will.

Zhen (震 ☳, thunder 雷) = the vibration of mantra and the pulse of single yang (the spark plug):
Zhen is "the vibration of the first yang qi" (일양시생 一陽始生) waking within the still dantian, and "the spark of sound and wave (thunder-sound 뇌성)" that rings out when you recite seed syllables and mantras.

This is the principle the *Shuogua* commentary of the *Book of Changes* states: "thunder and wind clash and stir each other" (雷風相薄).

When the practitioner joins the wind of breath (Xun ☴) and the wave of awareness and mantra (Zhen ☳) into one and blows them into the center of the lower belly (central palace 5), the stalled gears of Former and Later Heaven mesh, and "true breathing" (진식 眞息, real inner automatic breathing) awakens.

In the Zhen-Xun (thunder and wind) code, "Za Rahula" of Tibetan tantra and the highest deity of Daoist thunder rites (뇌법 雷法), the "Celestial Worthy of the Nine Heavens, Responding to the Origin, Thunder-Sound, Universal Transformation" (구천응원뇌성보화천존 九天應元雷聲普化天尊), are important.

Between Former Heaven (the source energy of the universe) and Later Heaven (the body and the real world) lies an invisible, thick dimensional membrane (ignorance 무명 無明 / turbid qi).

When the yin and yang of heaven (Qian ☰) and earth (Kun ☷) stand off taut, the physical phenomenon that cuts between them like a flash and links heaven and earth into one is the thunderbolt (뇌전 雷電).

"Thunder is not only in the sky; it is the Former-Heaven one qi (일기 一氣) in my belly (the dantian nine palaces) moving. Yin and yang rub, thunder strikes (Zhen 3), the wind of breath helps it (Xun 4), the five phases of the central palace fuse into one, and the Thunder-Sound Celestial Worthy manifests within my body."

Only when thunder (Rahula, the Celestial Worthy) first shakes heaven and earth awake (Zhen 3, Xun 4) does a golden sweet rain (the nectar of Jambhala and Vajrasattva) finally pour from the sky.

## The Tea Waker cup and "draining fire to generate metal"
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111611575358673156)

When you drink Pu-erh from a Tea Waker, the floral and fruity notes blooming from the earth grow stronger. The sweetness lingering on the tongue grows stronger, as intense as an apple's.

Because it wakes the tea's hidden qi, it's called not a mug but a "waker." If you drink it well, you may occasionally see a "golden rain" (sweet dew 감로수).

That's because it produces the action of "draining fire to generate metal" (설화생금 洩火生金), softly absorbing fire's stinging heat and toxicity (fire toxin 화독 火毒).

The moment the fiery heat passes through the waker's Chen-earth (辰) and combines (합 合) with You-metal (酉), the heat that wanted to disperse is highly compressed and cooled, hardens (is stored) into the purest crystal, "true gold (진금 眞金) and diamond (vajra 금강 金剛)," and pours back down into the Dharma-source palace (생법궁 生法宮).
