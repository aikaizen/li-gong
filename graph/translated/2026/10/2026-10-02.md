## Drunvalo Melchizedek's "Sacred Space of the Heart"
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2105877679019057578) (quotes [the Sep 23 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2102488171858866283))

"The Sacred/Tiny Space of the Heart" — Drunvalo Melchizedek

Around the heart there is a biomagnetic field about 55–65 feet across, and inside the heart two magnetic fields overlap: a doughnut-shaped (not spherical) "outer torus" and an "inner torus" within it.

The ancient *Chandogya Upanishad* explains that the tiny space within the heart is as vast as the outer universe, and that heaven and earth, sun and moon, and the constellations are all contained in it.

The center point (bindu point) where the toroidal field rolls into the center of the heart chakra is a "singularity" in which polarity (male and female, yang and yin, etc.) disappears,

and through it one can connect instantly to everything in the universe without the constraint of time (in zero time).

That is, it's like creating the world from emptiness.

[Method]

Step 1 (posture and breath): Sit comfortably with the spine straight, close the eyes, and relax the body. Breathe in through the nose and out through the mouth, keeping the in- and out-breath the same length to maintain a rhythmic breath.

Step 2 (moving from the brain to the heart): Since in most people the spirit (awareness) resides in the pineal gland (brain), first bring awareness to the head, then feel or visualize it leaving the brain, going down the neck, passing the lungs, and moving to the heart in the center of the chest (slightly left).

Step 3 (entering the torus and stopping): Enter the heart and pass through the outer torus field, then declare to yourself, "I will move through the inner torus field into the tiny space of the heart, and where I stop is that place," and feel awareness moving.

After moving for about 1–2 minutes, the point where movement stops completely and becomes still is your own "tiny space."

Step 4 (asking for light and staying): If the space where you stopped feels dark, ask "Let there be light" and look at the surroundings (a cave, stars, a waterside, different for each person), and follow any water there or wherever intuition leads.

Stay in that space and listen to the endlessly sounding "Om."

Step 5 (closing): When ending the meditation, don't bring awareness back to the head (the outer body). Leave the spirit in the tiny space of the heart and the center of the heart chakra, and slowly open your eyes.
