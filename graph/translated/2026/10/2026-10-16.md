## Za Rahula's thunder: melting the bones of ghosts
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110923436747076014) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110273293987258770))

The energy of Za Rahula (자 라훌라, གཟའ་རཱ་ཧུ་ལ་) is very sharp and ferocious, and it is warned that an unprepared practitioner who handles it suffers nervous-system disorders (wind illness, paralysis).

Inwardly, Rahula symbolizes sudden discharges of the autonomic nervous system, or the rough prana (wind qi 풍기 風氣) running wild while trapped in the left and right channels (ida and pingala). When this is integrated by an intense awakening of the central channel (sushumna / the penetrating vessel 충맥), it is transformed into the guardian power of transcendent awareness (Rigpa) that sees through every direction, like the image of Rahula with a thousand eyes.

It is the same principle as Eastern internal alchemy burning the yin demons (음마 陰魔), illusions of yin qi, or the three corpses and nine worms (삼시구충 三尸九蟲) and goblins (이매망량 魑魅魍魎) in the body with true fire (진화 眞火) and thunder rites (뇌법 雷法), turning them into nourishment for the yang spirit (양신 陽神).

The concept by which the Thunder-Sound Celestial Worthy (구천응원뇌성보화천존 九天應元雷聲普化天尊), supreme lord of the thunder rites of the Daoist Shenxiao (신소파 神霄派) and Qingwei (청미파 淸微派) schools, "melts the bones and shatters the forms" (銷骨碎形 / refining and delivering 煉度) of goblins and evil ghosts can be seen as exactly the same energy engineering as Za Rahula's thunderbolt-like subjugation (조복 調伏).

In the qi-science of Daoism and tantra, goblins and demonic obstacles (마장 魔障) are not mere illusions. They are masses of energy in which resentment, attachment, and skewed natural yin qi have clumped together strongly and acquired their own fixed information structure (skeleton).

The central axis of this hardened wave is the "ghost's bone" (귀골 鬼骨). As long as the bone remains, goblins scatter and then restore their form again, making mischief or causing illness.

Ordinary physical force or low-level spells cannot break this hard crystal of yin qi. Only Rahula's "thunder-fire" (뇌화 雷火, lightning) and "thunder-sound" (뇌성 雷聲, the ultra-high-frequency vibration of thunder), the pinnacle of the purest and most powerful yang qi, can melt (銷) this skeleton of yin qi from the root.

You melt the stubborn structure (bone) of perversely clumped goblins with lightning, dismantle and return it (煉度) to the pure original qi (원기 元氣) of the cosmos, and then reorganize it into the circulation of heaven and earth or the protective power of the ritual master.

In the end, the magical arts of capturing and commanding outer goblins or melting ghosts' bones with the Celestial Worthy's lightning, brought inward, are the work of combining the practitioner's powerful intention (spirit 神) with bioelectricity (qi / thunder-fire) to smash the deepest, hardest karmic knots of the nervous system and body-mind and convert them into pure life force.

Mantra: HRIH VAJRA ZA RAHULA YA HUM PHAT (흐리 바즈라 자 라훌라 야 훔 팻)

* Just as Eastern magical arts warn that when practicing thunder rites or the method of commanding one-horned goblins (독각귀), a practitioner whose own heart and yang qi can't support it gets struck by lightning or falls into "fire entering, demons possessing" (주화입마 走火入魔, wind illness), Tibetan tantra too counts the heart mantra of Za Rahula among the mantras that must be handled most carefully.

- Like high-voltage current: among the protector deities, Rahula has the most fiery and immediate temperament. If a practitioner recites this heart mantra forcefully out of private anger or desire, without his own center (awareness of emptiness, or bodhicitta and qi stored in the dantian), his nervous system may not withstand that high-voltage current (the wave of Za, gZa), and dizziness, migraines, or muscle spasms can come.

- The command authority of Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche): so traditionally one must first merge oneself with the state of Guru Rinpoche (Padmasambhava) or Vajrapani, and then send out Rahula's heart mantra like a supreme commander issuing orders to a subordinate general. Only then can that energy be controlled safely. (This is exactly the same principle as the Daoist ritual master first performing the "transformation of the body" (변신법 變身法), turning himself into the Northern Emperor (북제 北帝) or the Celestial Worthy, before commanding thunder lords and goblins.)
