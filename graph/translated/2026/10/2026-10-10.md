## Two ways practice goes wrong
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2108706892994457730) (quotes [the Oct 9 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2108340006981152989))

When problems arise in practice, it's usually that uncontrolled heat causes hallucinations and other abnormal phenomena, or that, out of greed for light, one becomes a vampire coveting others' light and energy.

Light that comes from your future self must become the energy of metal. This is "fire overcoming, generating metal" (화극생금), and it is Vajrasattva.
