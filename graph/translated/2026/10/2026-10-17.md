## Why yellow Jambhala is practiced without Vasudhara's empowerment
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111400471831454207)

In tantric iconography, the male main deity yellow Jambhala appears to exist alone, but that's partly because his key implements and mandala structure already contain the female principle (Vasudhara's attributes).

And as far as I know, it's less that Vasudhara's empowerment is strictly required (difficult to get), and more that she can be practiced without a separate heavy empowerment (wang), or is integrated into Tara.

## Vasudhara, the earth womb, and Return ䷗
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111367902029074641) (quotes [the Oct 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111358313141297548))

In the mandalas and mythic lineage of tantra, the eternal consort (명비 明妃 / dakini) of Jambhala (the male wealth deity), the earth goddess who supplies him with the source of abundance, is "Vasudhara" (바수다라).

For Jambhala's blessing (가피) to activate, Vasudhara's activity must begin first.

The meaning of Vasudhara:

Vasu: treasure, gold, goods, the seed of vitality
Dhara: to hold, to sustain, the earth (womb), a flowing river

So Vasudhara literally means "the womb of the earth holding the seed of treasure" (the earth-mother goddess 지모신 地母神); Chinese sutras translate her as "Bodhisattva Who Upholds the World" (지세보살 持世菩薩) or "Heavenly Maiden of the Source of Wealth" (재원천녀 財源天女). It calls to mind Demeter.

If Jambhala is the father (skillful means) who distributes treasure to the world, the mother (the source of wealth 財源) who actually conceives and raises that treasure in her womb is Vasudhara.

When we saw earlier that "for the male deity (Jambhala) to activate, he must meet the counterclockwise womb," the personal embodiment of that womb is the goddess Vasudhara.

Vasudhara's blessing, as a Yijing hexagram, can be called *Earth-Thunder, Return* (지뢰복 地雷復 ䷗).

Because it is the golden seed (single yang 一陽) conceived in Vasudhara's womb (Kun ☷).

Looking at the structure of Return (䷗), above (outside) is Kun (坤 ☷, earth, womb), and at the very bottom (first line) a single yang line (일양, Zhen ☳) has just been born.

In the dark earth of the winter solstice (동지 冬至) when the whole world is frozen solid (Kun-earth ☷ = Vasudhara's deep womb), the moment the first flame of life (single yang = Jambhala's seed syllable "Jam") finally begins to pulse with a "thump!" is Return.

It is the first life force of spring (일양시생) breaking up through the dark earth as Zhen-wood (震木 / Mao-wood 卯木).

In the embrace of the womb, Jambhala's seed (the syllable "Jam ཛཾ," or Vasudhara's seed syllable "Vas / Vam") takes the mother's nourishment and awakens as "single yang," then shoots up vigorously along the 3 o'clock direction (the position of eastern Mao-wood 卯木).

So to receive Vasudhara's blessing, take the hand seal of *Return* (지뢰복 ䷗).

## Jambhala's hidden femininity
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111358313141297548) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142))

The Tibetan tantric god of abundance, Jambhala (the yellow wealth deity), is depicted outwardly as a bearded, sturdily built male main deity (buddha or bodhisattva). But look deeply into his inner symbols and attributes and "the femininity of the mother who holds and raises abundance (great earth mother, womb)" is steeped in him more strongly than anyone.

Ordinary male protector deities (for example Vajrapani or Vaishravana) show a linear, aggressive masculinity (yang 陽), muscles hard and angular or armored. Jambhala's body, by contrast, is all soft, full curves (yin 陰).

The "wisdom of equality" (평등성지 平等性智) that embraces without discrimination: the wisdom of Ratnasambhava, "the wisdom of equality," means a mother's unconditional receptivity (hidden virtue 음덕 陰德) that embraces and raises all life equally, regardless of whether someone is impressive or lowly.

In Indian, Nepali, and Tibetan tradition, Jambhala's eternal partner (consort goddess) is Vasudhara, goddess of abundance and the earth (Heavenly Maiden of the Source of Wealth / Upholder of the World).

The outer form of the Later-Heaven bagua is the protective shell of a dependable father (skillful means) who guards the practitioner from poverty and obstacles; energetically it rotates clockwise.

Hidden within that Later-Heaven bagua is the Former-Heaven bagua: the womb of the mother (wisdom, earth), which sets up a counterclockwise womb rotation in the lower belly (the Dharma-source palace 생법궁), conceives wealth and vitality, and sends down nectar (golden rain) that never runs dry.

## The Dharma-source palace: male deity outside, womb rotation inside
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111253190553559456) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142))

In Tibetan tantra, the triangular (or vase-shaped) secret space in the lower-dantian chakra is called the "Dharma-source palace" (생법궁 生法宮; Sanskrit *Dharmadhatu-garbha* or *Dharmodaya*).

"Dharmodaya" literally means "the primordial womb (*bhaga* / *yoni*) from which all dharmas (truths and phenomena) are conceived and come forth."

In all the energy laws of the universe there is an absolute principle: "solitary yang (a male deity) can never give birth or manifest by itself (고양불생 孤陽不生), and solitary yin (the feminine) cannot grow (독음부장 獨陰不長)."

So the male deity Rahula must receive the help of the female principle. That's why, in the Dharma-source palace, the qi flows counterclockwise (the womb, the female principle) while Rahula's mantra is inscribed around the outside. Inside is female, outside is male.

Seen from the front, the vortex of the vertical circle rolling in counterclockwise (left-turning 좌선 左旋) on the in-breath is the "amniotic fluid of the womb" (yin 陰 / the wisdom of prajna), which sucks the male deity's seed deep into itself, wraps it, adds flesh and blood, and raises it.

When the male deity's mantra stands at the outer edge of the Dharma-source palace and a circle turns counterclockwise (the womb's direction) inside it, it outwardly looks like a solo practice of the male deity, but energetically the male deity (skillful means) has entered the vortex of the womb (the dakini's wisdom, emptiness) and achieved perfect sexual and spiritual union (Yab-Yum). That's why, the moment this counterclockwise rotation occurs, the male deity's power finally activates explosively.
