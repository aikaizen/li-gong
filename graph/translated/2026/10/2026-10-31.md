## A few ways to drink Pu-erh tea
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2116420949147038166) (quotes [the Oct 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2115604968447320119))

A few ways to drink Pu-erh tea (보이차):

1. Don't drink the first steeping right away; pour it out. This is called "washing the tea" (세차). The caffeine, which dissolves quickly in water, comes out at this stage.

2. Don't swallow at once. Wet the whole mouth thoroughly, let it soak into the teeth and gums and mix well with saliva, then swallow in portions.

If you just gulp it down, in the body it [is treated] as water…

## Day of Wu-Yin (戊寅日): settling the tiger
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2116327858205577314)

Oct 31, 2026, a Wu-Yin day (무인일 戊寅日). A tiger (Yin 寅) butts upward from beneath the mountain (Wu 戊), so the energies naturally clash and it is an easy day to grow impatient.

But with the attunement method of Body-Qimen (신기문 身奇門), this rough impulse becomes the best chance of the day to fill the dantian.

- Left hand Xun ☴ + right hand Qian ☰ (Heaven-Wind, Gou ䷫ · "Dragon Escape" 용둔): wrap around the sharp-edged energy.
- Left hand Xun ☴ + right hand Gen ☶ (Mountain-Wind, Gu ䷑ · "Tiger Escape" 호둔): seat the tiger in its home palace.
- Finally, upper trigram Qian ☰ + lower trigram Gen ☶: lock it in heavily.

The tiger's drive, which wanted to leap outward, goes quiet and condenses into solid inner power (내공 內功) in the lower dantian. An unshakable mind (부동심 不動心). Today's attunement is complete.

## Controlling the killing edge of Geng-metal by joining with it
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2116194241827467497) (quotes [the Oct 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2115678145655583097))

One way to control the killing energy (살기 殺氣) of Geng-metal (경금 庚金) is simply to join with it (합 合).

From the Body-Qimen view, a flow in which Xun (巽 ☴) meets Qian (乾 ☰) (Heaven-Wind, Gou ䷫ · Dragon Escape), and Kan (坎 ☵ · the Rest Gate 휴문) is then added, interlocks precisely with two principles. The first is the yin-yang union of yin-wood and metal-qi (陰木 + 金氣), as in the Yi-Geng combination (을경합 乙庚合) and the Chen-You combination (진유합 辰酉合). The second is the generating cycle "metal generates water, water generates wood."

Qian ☰ symbolizes strong yang-metal (the very body of Geng 庚). Xun ☴ symbolizes soft yin-wood (Yi-wood 乙). Normally metal and wood collide ("metal overcomes wood"). But when yang-metal (Qian ☰ / Geng) meets yin-wood (Xun ☴ / Yi), they do not fight. They embrace like husband and wife: the yin-yang harmony of the Yi-Geng combination.

So you form the Xun seal with the left hand and the Qian seal with the right. In the Book of Changes, the hexagram where Qian ☰ meets Xun ☴, *Gou* (天風姤 ䷫), means "meeting by chance, yin and yang uniting" (음양상구 陰陽相遘)…
