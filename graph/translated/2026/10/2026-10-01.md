## The Merkaba: chariot of light
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2105613281876652442) (quotes [the Sep 20 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2101526624823026082))

"Merkaba" is a word rooted in ancient Egyptian and Hebrew, combining "Mer" (light), "Ka" (spirit), and "Ba" (body): "a chariot (vehicle) of light enveloping spirit and body."

Geometrically it takes the form of a three-dimensional Star of David, two tetrahedra (pyramids) interpenetrating up and down: the "star tetrahedron."

The upward pyramid symbolizes the masculine (heaven, yang) and the downward pyramid the feminine (earth, yin); when these two energies are in perfect balance, the light body is said to awaken.

Vividly visualize the upper and lower pyramids beginning to rotate in opposite directions.

As this rotation speeds up enormously, close to the speed of light, feel a huge disc-shaped field of light (a toroidal field) extending around the physical body.

This is the moment the crystal light body (Merkaba) awakens.
