## Rahu and Ketu in the body: the black sun of the central channel
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2109906927144841532) (quotes [the Oct 13 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2109850838260998290))

Just as Rahu and Ketu are the points in the sky where the sun's path and the moon's path meet, inside the body the points where the left (ida) and right (pingala) channels spiral up around the sushumna at the center of the spine (in a caduceus shape) and cross are the "chakras."

Pingala nadi (the right channel, the sun's path): flows along the right side of the spine; a yang (陽) energy governing the sympathetic nervous system, heat, logic, the flow of time (future orientation), and exhalation.

Ida nadi (the left channel, the moon's path): flows along the left side of the spine; a yin (陰) energy governing the parasympathetic nervous system, coolness, intuition, memory (past karma), and inhalation.

In the ordinary state, human life force (prana) and awareness can't enter the central channel (sushumna); they alternate between the sun and moon channels and live trapped in the waves of duality (subject and object, attachment and aversion).

The key crossing points where these two energies tangle and mesh most strongly are called "granthis" (spiritual knots) in yoga, and the Rahu-Ketu axis connects directly to both ends of the sushumna and its key knots.

In the sky the sun and moon normally go their own ways, but when they align exactly in a straight line (new or full moon) at the position of the nodes (Rahu-Ketu), solar and lunar eclipses occur. Rahu swallows the sun and moon.

The dualistic breath and thoughts that flowed in the left and right channels (ida, pingala) stop. Tantric scriptures express this as "Rahula swallows and kills the sun and moon."

To the ancients, the motion of the sun and moon was "time" (Kala) itself. When Rahula swallows the sun and moon, inner psychological time (past regret and future anxiety) stops in an instant.

At that moment the Black Sun manifests in the sushumna.

The "black sun" of a total eclipse is not mere darkness.

It symbolizes the "emptiness of no-self" (무아의 공성 無我 空性), revealed only when the dualistic illusion of sensory light (the sun) and reflected light (the moon) is swallowed by Rahula, and fundamental pure awareness (Dzogchen's rigpa, the Daoist "one opening of the mysterious pass" 현관일규 玄關一竅).

Light, when it stays merely light, just scatters.
Only when it meets the moon and becomes black light does it settle in the practitioner's body.

## The black sun, Doumu, and the hidden eighth and ninth stars
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2109850838260998290) (quotes [the Oct 9 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2108340006981152989))

When the yin and yang of ida and pingala gather into the sushumna, it is said an inner eclipse occurs. This is the black sun.

In the Daoist Big Dipper tradition, this phenomenon in which Zuofu and Youbi (좌보 우필) open their eyes is described as "invisible stars appear in the void," and it connects with the "black sun" (the opening of the one aperture of the mysterious pass 현관일규 玄關一竅) that Rahula reveals by swallowing the sun and moon in Indian tantra.

It is Doumu Yuanjun (두모원군 斗母元君), the Daoist mother goddess of the Big Dipper, who is the Buddhist tantric "Marici" (마리지천 摩利支天).

In Daoist iconography fused with tantra, the attendants guarding Doumu Yuanjun (Marici) on left and right, and the key driving power that moves the seven stars, are precisely "Lord Rahu" (나후성군) and "Lord Ketu" (계도성군).

An ordinary person lives ruled only by the Big Dipper (the seven senses and seven emotions), but when a practitioner unites the energies of yin and yang through internal alchemy (sun and moon joined 일월합벽), the 8th star (Zuofu) and 9th star (Youbi), invisible until then, finally awaken at the center of the brain and begin to shine.

Daoism calls this opening of the eyes of Zuofu and Youbi "invisible stars appear in the void," and it is exactly the same physiological and spiritual state as the "black sun" (opening of the mysterious pass) that Rahula reveals by swallowing the sun and moon in Indian tantra.

*(Translator's note: the author's handle, 마리지천 摩利支天, is the name of this goddess, Marici.)*
