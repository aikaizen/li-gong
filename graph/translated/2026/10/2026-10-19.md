## Guimei ䷵: the hexagram that opens the heart chakra
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112101275261419888) (quotes [the Oct 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2106518721544241484))

For activating the heart chakra, the suitable internal-alchemy Yijing hexagram is *Thunder-Lake, The Marrying Maiden* (뇌택귀매 雷澤歸妹).

If you treat the Marrying Maiden simply as a fortune-reading hexagram:

• "Guimei" (歸妹) means a younger sister goes off to marry. It is the image of lightning striking and thunder rolling above a lake, a metaphor for a young woman marrying in a hurry, somewhat impulsively or without following proper procedure.

Fortune reading: a period when feelings run ahead and it's easy to break order or propriety. It may look glamorous outside but lack substance inside, so it's safest to avoid impulsive decisions and overreach and go with the natural order.

That's how it's interpreted.

But… seen through internal alchemy, which reads the flow of qi, it carries completely different energy: it is the hexagram (energy) in which the chest chakra opens and a sun rises in the chest.

Looking at the Marrying Maiden (䷵), of the six lines, the middle lines 3 and 4 meet as yin and yang.

• Line 6: ⚋
• Line 5: ⚋
• ★ Line 4: ⚊ (the first line of Zhen ☳ = the single yang fires!)
• ★ Line 3: ⚋ (the last line of Dui ☱ = the open top of Dui!)
• Line 2: ⚊
• Line 1: ⚊

The lower line 3 (six in the third place ⚋) is "Dui's open top" (태상절 兌上缺, the place where the top of the lake is softly open): the energy pushed up by lines 1 (yang ⚊) and 2 (yang ⚊), filling the lower belly (the earth position), is unbolted and softly opens at line 3 (yin ⚋: the lower chest, the solar plexus).

Right above it, line 4 (nine in the fourth place ⚊) is "Zhen's joined bottom" (진하련 震下連, the place where thunder's first yang line explodes): directly above the open space of line 3 (yin ⚋), line 4, the master of the upper trigram Zhen (☳), ignites with yang qi (⚊) as fierce as thunder and soars into the empty sky of lines 5 (⚋) and 6 (⚋) above!

Conclusion (where yin and yang meet and burst): with the lower belly (the two yang lines 1–2 ⚊⚊) supporting and the upper chest (the two yin lines 5–6 ⚋⚋) clear and empty, line 3 (yin ⚋) and line 4 (yang ⚊) touch exactly at the center of the chest and spark yin-yang union. So the structure is one in which the chest chakra cannot help opening wide in every direction without any tightness!

If you draw the "nuclear trigrams" (호괘 互卦: the inner hidden trigrams), the Yijing secret of looking into a hexagram's flesh (the central lines 2–5), you get a spine-tingling explanation of why the heart chakra and eyes brightened.

Inner lower nuclear trigram (lines 2, 3, 4) = ⚊ ⚋ ⚊ ➔ "Li-fire (離火 ☲: sun · heart · eyes · chest chakra)"!

Inner upper nuclear trigram (lines 3, 4, 5) = ⚋ ⚊ ⚋ ➔ "Kan-water (坎水 ☵: moon · kidney water · clear essence-qi)"!

Look! In that very chest position where line 3 (⚋) and line 4 (⚊) of the Marrying Maiden (䷵) meet, none other than "Li (離 ☲, sun, heart's flame)" and "Kan (坎 ☵, clear water)" were hidden as nuclear trigrams, precisely interlocked!

You just felt that "a sun hidden inside Zhen (☳) opens the chest and brightens the eyes," and indeed, connecting lines 2, 3, 4 of the Marrying Maiden, a perfect "Li-fire (離 ☲: ⚊⚋⚊, sun, eyes, heart)" trigram rises right in the middle of the chest!

That is, line 3 (yin ⚋) becomes the empty center of Li (☲: "Li is empty in the middle" 이중허), the core of the chest chakra, and line 2 (yang ⚊) below and line 4 (yang ⚊) above make the upper and lower walls of that heart flame, lighting a brilliant sun (Li ☲) at the center of the chest!

So if you hold the left hand as Dui and the right hand as Zhen, the heart chakra opens. You feel happiness.

## Spirit and qi answering each other: Inner Truth ䷼
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112036566436806775) (quotes [the Oct 19 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111979623475241105))

Internal alchemy has the phrase "spirit and qi answering each other" (신기상응 神氣相應). It means the spirit (神) of the upper dantian and the qi (氣) of the lower dantian respond to each other like magnets in the central palace (중궁) and are bound into one.

*Wind-Lake, Inner Truth* (풍택중부 ䷼, Dui + Xun) is the ultimate "civil fire" (문화 文火): the precious sweet dew and true qi pooled in the lake of the lower belly (Dui ☱) are only gently stroked from above by the soft wind of the breath (Xun ☴), quietly ripened as if brooding an egg.

For the line text of nine in the second place of Inner Truth, which Confucius himself explained in the *Xici* commentary of the *Book of Changes*, it says: "A crane calls from the shade, and its young answer." That's how important this hexagram is.

The reason is that it is "the central axis of true intention" (진의 眞意), where the sweet dew of the left hand (Dui ☱, Former-Heaven Kan ☵) and the soft breath of the right hand (Xun ☴) meet, quieting all anxiety and stray thoughts and ripening true qi (진기 眞氣) most purely.

## Vasudhara, the seed syllable Vam, and Inner Truth ䷼
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111979623475241105) (quotes [the Oct 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111755777740595398))

If the green Jambhala is the frequency that expands wealth luck through active circulation, then this relates to Jambhala's consort, the female earth and abundance goddess "Vasudhara" (바수다라), and the seed syllable "Vam" (밤, བཾ).

In the Yijing this is expressed as *Inner Truth* (풍택중부 ䷼).

In Sanskrit and Tibetan tantra, the syllable "Vam" is the seed syllable of "water" (the water element 水大, Apas) among the five great elements of the universe. It symbolizes the "water of life and nectar (Amrita)" that wells up unfailingly from the treasure vase, and the compassionate energy of female buddhas and bodhisattvas (Vasudhara; the water element within the wisdom-fist mudra of Vairocana of the Diamond Realm).

What lies hidden beneath (the Former-Heaven body of) the Later-Heaven Dui palace (☱, the lake) held in the left hand is precisely the "Former-Heaven Kan palace (☵, water of the deep source)"! The moment you take the Dui palace (middle + ring) with the left hand, its root, Former-Heaven Kan-water (water = the "Vam" seed), quietly fills up from the floor of the lower belly (palace 1 Kan ➔ palace 2 Kun, Vasudhara's earth). That's why the goddess Vasudhara, holding golden jeweled ears of rice and a vase of nectar, and the seed syllable "Vam" came to mind intuitively.

Look at the six lines of Inner Truth (䷼) (⚊⚊⚋⚋⚊⚊).

Top and bottom are closed by solid golden walls (yang lines ⚊⚊), and the belly in the middle (lines 3–4) is roundly empty (yin lines ⚋⚋): the shape of a jar. It is the very image of the "vase of abundance" (the jar of the Mysterious Female 현빈) held by Vasudhara, filled with the nectar of the syllable "Vam"!

It is useful when you want to drink tea quietly and empty your inner center (the central palace) so that the world's opportunities, wealth, and people's trust answer and gather by themselves without effort ("a crane calls in the shade and its young answer" 명학재음 기자화지), or when designing the "credit and continuity" ("sincerity that binds" 유부연여) of a currency, brand, or policy.

The mudra is: left hand Dui (middle and ring touching the thumb) / right hand Xun (index and middle touching the thumb).
