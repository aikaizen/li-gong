## Wind and thunder strengthening each other: Yi ䷩
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2115241006765584484) (quotes [the Oct 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111755777740595398))

Wind and thunder press on each other (풍뢰상박 風雷相薄): wind and thunder help each other and double their power.

The *Tuan* and *Great Image* commentaries of the *Book of Changes* define Wind-Thunder, Increase (䷩) as "the image of wind (Xun ☴) and thunder (Zhen ☳) meeting and adding to each other's force" (풍뢰익 風雷益).

Among the 64 hexagrams it is a representative greatly auspicious (대길 大吉) hexagram, with the character "increase" (익 益: profit, gain, help) in its very name.

"There is gain to be had: it is beneficial to have somewhere to go, and beneficial to cross the great river" (利有攸往 利涉大川).

It shows its best energy when you expand the scale of a plan or business, when you draw in the help of a benefactor (the grace of "diminishing above to increase below" 손상익하), or when you want to overturn a stagnant situation and take real gains through fast word of mouth (wind 風) and powerful execution (thunder 雷).

Structure and two-hand seals: "outer trigram, Later-Heaven Xun (☴) × inner trigram, Former-Heaven Zhen (☳)"

Composition: outer trigram (upper trigram · right hand, Later-Heaven Earth Plate 지반): Xun (巽 ☴ · Fourth-Center Tanlang 사중탐랑), palace 4 of the southeast. Anatomically it lies beneath the liver (간 肝), where blood qi flows out (혈출 血出) and organ qi comes in anew (부입 府入): the "key transfer station of the abdominal Möbius (∞) flow."

Inner trigram (lower trigram · left hand, Former-Heaven Heaven Plate 천반): Zhen (震 ☳ · Seventh-Ha Wuqu 칠하무곡): thunder, vibration, the explosive movement (동 動) of the first yang qi.

The two-hand seals and how the fingertip terminals work:

Left hand (Zhen ☳): press only the ring finger (output · Triple Burner) flat against the thumb (control · lungs), one to one, and extend the index and middle fingers.

Right hand (Xun ☴): touch the index finger (input) and middle finger (control) to the thumb, and lift only the ring finger (output).

The exquisite meshing of the terminals: the left hand closes only the output (ring finger) to compress, while the right hand closes input (index) and control (middle) and opens only the output (ring) to radiate. It is an asymmetric yin-yang pump.
