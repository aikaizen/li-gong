## Sudden market moves and Rahu
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110624523683078168) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110273293987258770))

The market's sudden moves remind me of the Tibetan Rahu (라후).

Rahu, the ancient Indian asura who causes solar and lunar eclipses, is a great guardian absorbed into Vajrayana (especially the Dzogchen tradition of the Nyingma school). With nine heads and a thousand eyes studded all over his body and the lower body of a serpent, Rahula is the sovereign who commands the malevolent energy of all the planets (요성 曜星), the eight classes of spirits (팔부신중), and above all the violent lightning and nervous-system waves of the "Za (gZa)" class, which cause strokes and paralysis.

Among the protector deities, Rahula has the most fiery and immediate temperament.

If a practitioner recites this heart mantra forcefully out of private anger or desire, without his own center (awareness of emptiness, or bodhicitta and qi stored in the dantian), his nervous system may not withstand that high-voltage current (the wave of Za, gZa), and dizziness, migraines, or muscle spasms can come.

One must merge oneself with the state of Vajrapani and then send out Rahula's heart mantra like a supreme commander issuing orders to a subordinate general. Only then can that energy be controlled safely. (This is exactly the same principle as the Daoist ritual master first performing the "transformation of the body" (변신법 變身法), turning himself into the Northern Emperor or the Celestial Worthy, before commanding thunder lords and goblins.)

## Geng-Wu spirit qi: holding the center while goblins run wild
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110556460547793046) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142))

"Geng-Wu spirit qi" (경오영기 庚午靈氣), which lets you hold your center even when petty ghosts run wild

In stem-branch (간지 干支) study, Geng-Wu (庚午) is "the structure of true metal refined by fire (화련진금 火煉眞金): taking the flames of the fiercest furnace (Wu-fire 午火) with the whole body, forging itself into a fine sword and a bell (종 鐘), and conceiving cold water of life (Ren-water 壬水) within."

In Geng-Wu, the heavenly stem holds Geng-metal (경금 庚金), hard and rough raw ore and cast iron, and the earthly branch holds Wu-fire (오화 午火, Direct Officer 정관), the noon blaze in which yang qi and heat reach their peak among the twelve branches.

Of the five phases, only Geng-metal is not afraid of fire (Ding-fire, Wu-fire) but welcomes it. An unrefined lump of cast iron can only be reborn as a sharp treasured sword (보검 寶劍) or a great bell (대종 大鐘) whose sound carries far by passing through the furnace flames ("split Jia to draw Ding" 벽갑인정 劈甲引丁, and true metal refined by fire 화련진금).

This matches the process in Tibetan tantra in which the fierce fire of the lower-belly dantian (tummo, the fire-accompanying wind 화평 룽) rises along the spine and channels and forges the rough neural network to high density, like "meteorite iron fallen from the sky" (namchak, the vajra armor).

Wu-fire (午), the peak of the hottest fire, is paradoxically the place where cold water (Ren-water 壬水) is conceived (胎), by the principle "when yang reaches its extreme, yin stirs" (극양즉음 極陽則陰).

At the same time, the heavenly stem Geng-metal (庚) sitting on Wu-fire is in the "Bathing" (목욕 沐浴: washing and tempering) stage of the twelve life stages. That is, outwardly it seems wrapped in hot flames, but behind that, Geng-metal itself generates water (metal generates water 金生水), washing and cooling the heated body in a tempering action.

Inside Wu-fire's hidden stems (지장간) are Bing-fire (丙, Seven Killings 편관), Ji-earth (己, Direct Resource 정인), and Ding-fire (丁, Direct Officer 정관).

Had there been only the flames of Bing and Ding, Geng-metal would have melted. But moist earth, Ji-earth (기토 己土), sits in the very middle, and through fire generating earth (火生土) → earth generating metal (土生金) it absorbs the fierce fire qi and acts as "insulation and crucible (the treasure vase)" protecting Geng-metal.

That's why Geng-Wu spirit qi holds the energy of thunder and lake (진택 震澤).

## Fire melting metal: Pema Shawari's mantra against panic trading
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110550106269532439) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142))

In the five phases, fire (火) overcomes metal (金) (fire overcomes metal 화극금 火剋金). Moderate fire refines raw ore (Geng-metal 庚金) into a fine sword or jewel (Xin-metal 辛金), but uncontrolled fierce flames instead melt the metal and evaporate it without a trace (too much fire melts metal, 화다금용). And if wind, wood (木, lung / volatility), joins in, wood generates fire (木生火) and the flames grow beyond control.

When sudden market swings (wood) fan a trader's impatience, greed, and fear (fire), heat shoots up to the head (rising-qi syndrome 상기증), and cool judgment and the neural network (metal) are paralyzed. As a result, my principal and profits, already finished like jewels (Xin-metal), melt away in the hot flames (liquidation, losses).

When fire qi surges is when Za Rahula's energy is active. Against this polarized volatility of Rahula,

the mantra [OM PEMA SHAWARI PHAT], which links to the frequency of the medicine goddess Pema Shawari,

acts as a shield that "hard-brakes (resets)" the amygdala running wild with fear and greed, preventing losses.

At this time, with the thumb of both hands (or the left hand) press gently on the root of the ring finger (the third joint), wrap it with the four fingers into a vajra fist (금강권), and rest it on the thigh.
