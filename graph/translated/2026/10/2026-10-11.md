## Before the true law: chaos as the release of grievances
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2109058460012982441) (quotes [the Oct 9 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2108340006981152989))

Before the true law (진법 眞法) is established, a stage must first pass in which all kinds of distorted, confused energies (the disordered law 난법 亂法) run rampant and each releases its grievance.

Chaos is not regression, but the process of a pendulum swinging violently to the opposite extreme to rebalance a long-standing asymmetric weight.

In a history of "suppressing yin and honoring yang" (억음존양 抑陰尊陽) and mutual overcoming (상극 相克), lecturing "From now on let's all be equal and harmonious" while leaving the knots that built up untouched is like winding bandages around a wound about to burst with pus.

It opened the stage, telling all the wronged, "Pour out every resentment and desire you held, to your heart's content." This is the release of grievances (해원 解冤).

The conflicts erupting everywhere and the outpouring of hidden resentments can be seen less as signs of collapse than as an unavoidable "spiritual and social healing crisis (a turn-for-the-better reaction)" on the way to a new point of balance.
