## Small Taming ䷈: dense clouds, no rain
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112414787615572179) (quotes [the Oct 20 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112349557434786024))

Bright light simply scatters and disappears if left alone. What matters is gathering that light. That is, what matters is Vairocana Buddha (비로자나불) becoming Vajrasattva (금강살타).

Wind-Heaven, Small Taming (풍천소축 風天小畜 ䷈) is the 9th of the 64 hexagrams. Taken literally it means "wind (風 ☴) blows above heaven (天 ☰), gathering and nurturing (畜) on a small scale (小)."

The most famous line explaining Small Taming in the main text of the *Book of Changes* (the judgment) is "密雲不雨 自我西郊": "dense clouds gather from the western fields until their density peaks, yet they do not yet pour down as rain."

If the clouds (qi) gather and pour out as rain (liquid, semen), the energy discharges and it's over.

"Dense clouds, no rain" is the state in which the qi doesn't leak outward or pour out as liquid (leaking essence, 누정 漏精), but is compressed and charged taut as a high-density energy cloud in the heavens (inside the body).

It is the hexagram in which palace 6 Qian (great capital, authority) meets the wind of palace 4 Xun (market profit, commercial sense) and ripens solidly inward.

It means that, rather than spending or earning showily on the outside, when you consolidate the inner substance (the Gate of Life 명문, the core) and store energy, the great explosive power that follows (Fire-Heaven, Great Possession ䷍) is prepared.

## Ligong: why not to pump energy into the lower dantian (taking from Kan to fill Li)
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112365901437919440) (quotes [the Oct 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2111253190553559456))

Pumping energy into the lower dantian is not effective. Leaking essence (누정) easily occurs, and since only the energy gets stronger, conceit easily arises.

When people who claim to be practicing have rotten or broken characters, it's because they practice centered on the lower dantian below the navel.

Water and qi flow from high pressure (where it's full) to low pressure (where it's empty).

If at first you press only the lower dantian, the Kan palace (감궁), the passage above isn't open, so the pressure bursts out downward (perineum, genitals). That's why people who practice centered below the navel become sexually sensitive.

On the other hand, if you first activate palace 9 Li (이궁 ☲: index and ring fingers touching, the middle finger spread wide and empty), a powerful "vacuum suction (negative pressure)" forms in the very center of the Li palace!

In that state, the moment you connect palace 1 Kan (감궁 ☵: middle finger touching), the single strand of true yang (⚊) trapped at the bottom of the Kan palace doesn't leak downward but is sucked up like an arrow toward the empty seat of the Li palace (☲) above (the open passage of the middle finger) and unites with it: taking from Kan to fill Li (취감전리 取坎填離)!

## The Earth Dragon, leaking essence, and charging the Qian battery directly
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2112349557434786024) (quotes [the Oct 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2110356759156265142))

[Internal alchemy and wealth notes] The Earth Dragon's (토룡 土龍) vigor and leaking essence (누정 漏精), and the secret of charging the "Qian-palace (☰) battery" directly

When wealth luck (재운 財運) leaks away, the principle is exactly the same as when qi leaks away. When cash and liquidity (downstream: Kan-water, Dui-metal) have drained out right now and the balance is empty, if you rush to plug only the cash hole in front of you, money keeps leaking like a jar with no bottom.

Whether qi or wealth, when the downstream leaks, you don't touch the bottom. You must first grasp and charge "palace 6 Qian (☰)," the upstream source power plant that produces that wealth and energy and the core of credit and authority, and lift it upward ("raise the origin, secure against collapse" 거원고탈 擧元固脫). Then the leak stops immediately.

1. The Earth Dragon leaves its cave (토룡출굴 土龍出窟): the single yang awakened in the earth, and an unexpected leak

After you turn the qi at the floor of the lower belly through practice (palace 1 Kan ➔ palace 2 Kun ➔ palace 3 Zhen), a cozy warmth spreads through the whole body, and at night you dream of "an earthworm wriggling up out of the ground."

In internal alchemy the earthworm is the "Earth Dragon" (토룡 土龍, the young dragon in the soil), and the earth dragon (지룡 地龍) that bores through blocked meridians. It is a sign that the first stirring of single yang (일양초동 一陽初動) of *Earth-Thunder, Return* (지뢰복 地雷復 ䷗), the first yang qi (Zhen ☳) waking and shooting up from the bottom of the great earth (Kun ☷), has been ignited exactly in the unconscious and the deep layers of the body.

But sometimes this awakened yang qi fails to be vaporized upward (refining essence into qi, 연정화기 煉精化氣) and, taking advantage of the relaxed gap during sleep, pours out through the lower reproductive gate (palace 1 Kan): "running leak" (주루), leaking essence. At this point the most interesting phenomenon of the study of change is found.

The accident (leak) clearly happened at palace 1 Kan (☵, water), yet when you wake in the morning, the place that actually feels empty and hollow is palace 6 Qian (☰, heaven, pure yang).

2. The core secret: when Kan (☵) leaks, don't touch Kan. Charge Qian (☰), the source battery!

Why is Qian discharged when Kan leaked?

Because, by the principle of the *Zhouyi cantong qi* (주역참동계 周易參同契), the true yang (⚊) lodged in the center of palace 1 Kan (☵) is originally a "Former-Heaven original-yang battery" dispatched from palace 6 Qian (☰). In five-phase terms too, palace 6 Qian-metal (the mother) is the upstream power plant that gives birth to palace 1 Kan-water (the child): metal generates water (금생수 金生水).

When the downstream dam gate (Kan) opens and the water gushes out, not only is the yang battery inside lost along with it, but Qian, the upstream source power plant, is wholly discharged trying to fill the gap (the mother's qi is damaged, 모기손상).

Here is where the decisive secret divides. If you think, "Yesterday qi gathered in Kan and leaked, so today I must fill Kan again (middle finger alone)" and stimulate the bottom, you lose a hundred battles out of a hundred.

With the lower gate (the essence gate 정관) already open and loose, if you keep applying water pressure to the bottom, it's like pouring water into a bottomless jar: the qi sinks further downward (sinking of middle qi 중기하함) and only the fatigue increases.

When wealth luck leaks away, the principle is exactly the same.

When cash and liquidity (downstream: Kan-water, Dui-metal) have drained out right now and the balance is empty, if you rush to plug only the cash hole in front of you, money keeps leaking like a jar with no bottom. Whether qi or wealth, when the downstream leaks, you don't touch the bottom. You must first grasp and charge "palace 6 Qian (☰)," the upstream source power plant and core of credit and authority, and lift it upward (거원고탈 擧元固脫). Then the leak stops immediately.

In Korean medicine and Daoist medicine, when treating "symptoms of qi and fluids pouring downward," like uterine bleeding or diarrhea, there is a taboo never broken:

"directly stimulating the lower part (kidneys, bladder, genitals) or using herbs that send things downward."

When the bottom leaks, you must lift from above (the central palace, the Qian palace).

If you touch the bottom when water leaks from it, the qi sinks further down (중기하함). The orthodox treatment used here is "tonify the middle and boost the qi" (보중익기 補中益氣) and "raise the origin, secure against collapse" (거원고탈 擧元固脫: boost the original qi of the middle and upper body and lift the downward-pouring qi sharply up and hold it).

3. Rapid charging of the Qian battery: *Wind-Heaven, Small Taming* (풍천소축 風天小畜 ䷈)

The hand seal that restores a discharged palace 6 Qian fastest is Small Taming (䷈).

• Seal: left hand Qian (☰: index, middle, and ring all touching) / right hand Xun (☴: index and middle touching)

• The outer (Later-Heaven) action: touching all three fingers of the left hand seals palace 6 Qian (the pure-yang battery of the lower dantian) tight like a pressure cooker. In that state, the bellows of right-hand palace 4 Xun (the wind of the breath, market profit multiplied threefold) is driven back inward, piercing the central-palace core and charging the Qian battery directly. The moment you hold it, a weighty strength and pleasant heat fill the belly that was hollow.

• The inner (Former-Heaven) secret: more remarkable still is the overlay of Former and Later Heaven. Under the flesh of Later-Heaven Qian (☰) is "Former-Heaven Gen (☶: mountain, gatekeeper, stopping)," and under Later-Heaven Xun (☴) is "Former-Heaven Dui (☱: lake, fluids, cash)."

So the moment you hold Small Taming, *Lake-Mountain, Influence* (䷞) and *Mountain-Lake, Decrease* (䷨) work simultaneously inside, replenishing the fluids and wealth qi (Dui ☱) that drained out while locking the loosened lower belly and the strongbox door (Gen ☶) like a boulder!

4. Final sealing (봉고 封固): lock the filled qi and wealth with the mountain (Gen 艮 ☶)

Once you've filled the Qian battery with vitality and the driving force of wealth, then before sleeping at night or at the end of practice you must finish with "sealing" (봉고 封固), fully locking the lower gate so the energy doesn't leak downward again.

• Mountain-Lake, Decrease (산택손 山澤損 ䷨): left hand Dui (☱ middle + ring) / right hand Gen (☶ index)
➔ Cover the sensory joy and wealth gathered in palace 7 Dui with the heavy boulder of palace 8 Gen (mountain, the Jumen-star storehouse 거문성), fixing it so it doesn't shake.
• Earth-Mountain, Modesty (지산겸 地山謙 ䷎): left hand Gen (☶ index) / right hand Kun (☷ all extended)
➔ Don't leave the awakened Earth Dragon (yang qi) lying at the bottom; hide it deep in the wide, calm earth (palace 2 Kun) and let it sleep.

⚡ The core formula: "When the downstream (1 Kan, liquidity) leaks, never touch the bottom. Charge and lift the upstream source battery (6 Qian, authority and core) with *Small Taming* (䷈), and lock it firmly with the Gen mountain (☶) of *Decrease* (䷨)." The internal elixir (내단 內丹) that protects the body's original qi and the "wealth elixir" (재단 財丹) that protects real-world riches run on a single principle.
