## From Qian-Kun to Li-Kan: water falling, fire flaring
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2114204012811792440) (quotes [the Oct 24 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2113802659836891366))

At birth, humans lose the perfect Former-Heaven (선천 先天) harmony of Qian and Kun (건곤 乾坤) and live in the Later-Heaven (후천 後天) yin-yang imbalance of Li (이 離 ☲) and Kan (감 坎 ☵).

Above (the chest, the heart), fire (Li ☲) blazes and dries out, and below (the lower dantian, the genitals), water (Kan ☵) pools and grows cold. So we walk the path of birth, aging, sickness, and death called "water falling, fire flaring" (수락화염 水落火炎)…

## The pelvic floor comes before a stable lower dantian
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2114167289893564558)

The pelvic floor (골반기저) means the pelvic floor muscles. Not the anus side: the muscles running from the perineum (회음) toward the front of the body matter more.

Only after those pelvic floor muscles are activated does qi form stably in the lower dantian.

## Anapanasati is more than counting breaths
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2114164601877352806) (quotes [the Oct 24 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2113981110162190844))

People think *anapanasati* (안반수의 安般守意) just means counting the breath. That is only its surface image.

On the in-breath there is a flower that blooms, turning clockwise from inside to outside; on the out-breath, the breath leaves, but there is a flow that winds back from outside to inside.

This harmony (the mean, 중용 中庸) of inside and outside is anapanasati.
