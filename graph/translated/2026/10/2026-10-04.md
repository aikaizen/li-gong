## Why the law of attraction backfires, and why to descend into the heart
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2106518721544241484) (quotes [the Sep 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2104834510693322802))

Why the "law of attraction" (The Secret) we commonly know produces side effects for so many people or ends in half-success, and why one must descend into the chest (heart chakra), lies in this structure of "duality versus unity."

The brain is not an organ for higher thought but an organ for survival and reproduction. It is an old toolbox and dualistic tool, evolved and designed for survival to distinguish and calculate "me and others," "good and evil," "gain and loss."

When you pull hard with the head, "I want to be rich" or "I want to be healthy," the premise and fear "I'm lacking now (poor / sick)" inevitably cling to the bottom of the brain's dualistic structure like the back of a coin.

Just as pushing on a wall makes the wall push back with the same force, the more strongly you want light with a split consciousness (the brain), the more an exactly equal amount of darkness (the counter-effect) is cast behind your back (in the unconscious and unexpected reality) where your sight doesn't reach.

This is called the principle of "neither increasing nor decreasing" (부증불감 不增不減). Seen as a whole, it's a zero-sum game, neither growing nor shrinking.

By contrast, the "lower heart chakra" (the tiny space within the heart) at the center of the sternum is the one zero point where duality doesn't operate: a structure of unity (Oneness).

A furnace where upper and lower energies melt into one: when you draw in the two energies of heaven (yang, the upper triangle) and earth (yin, the lower triangle) through the breath and unite them in the heart chakra, a primordial state of unity opens in which the very opposition of yin and yang disappears (the Eastern "Supreme Pole" 황극 皇極, the Western singularity at the center of the torus).

In the space of the heart you know intuitively that all things are already connected as one and everything is already complete. So creation happens not through the ego's craving, "I lack, so I must have," but by naturally projecting images of already-complete joy, gratitude, and unconditional love from inside (the heart) to outside (the hologram of reality).

In the end, only the creative act of emptiness takes place, and you understand that the world is the dance of God.

So the secret of true attraction and creation can be summed up as: "Stop trying to manipulate reality with thought (the brain); send awareness down into the center of the chest (the lower heart chakra) and dream from the frequency of Oneness, which has no counter-effect."
