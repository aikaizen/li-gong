1. The intercourse of Zeir Anpin and Nukva

In Lurianic Kabbalah the two most important divine personae (Partzufim) are "Zeir Anpin" (the Small Face, the masculine principle) and "Nukva" (the feminine principle).

The genital at the tip of Zeir Anpin is Yesod, and Nukva, the cosmic womb that receives it fully, is Malkuth (Kingdom) at the very bottom. 2. Mayim Nukvin: the fierce flame of the dakini (the letter Shin, ש)

Here lies the most spine-chilling insight of Lurianic Kabbalah. God's seed falling from above (Mayim Dukhrin, the masculine waters), however merciful, never descends first.

Only when the Nukva below (yoni / Malkuth) first kindles a huge energy of longing and heat and strikes upward is the masculine principle stimulated. Luria called this fierce energy surging up from the bottom "Mayim Nukvin" (the feminine waters).

This is the identity of the letter "Shin" (ש, fire) of path 20 that we pointed out.

Look at the shape of this letter. Three tongues gathering into one base: a perfect "vessel," resembling the anatomical structure of the yoni with the spine and pelvic cavity opened.

In Kabbalah the letter Shin (ש) symbolizes "fierce fire." This matches exactly how in tantra the dakini's yoni is depicted not as merely an empty, quiet space but as a dynamic red inverted-triangle furnace (altar) in which the flames of tummo boil up in the Dharma-source palace (생법궁).

When you as a practitioner become the dakini and receive God's seed (Yod), the great furnace that burns that immortal seed upward is this burning yoni (ש) of path 20.

The practitioner, taking the position of the empty yoni (dakini), fiercely kindles the flame of the lower dantian (sexual energy) with bahya kumbhaka and spinal bellows, that is, by shooting up "Mayim Nukvin," forcibly begins the divine intercourse.

3. The cosmic seed pouring through the birth canal of Tav (ת)

When the flame of the letter Shin (ש) shoots up from the lower dantian and pierces Zeir Anpin (the masculine principle), only then does the holy masculine principle of the higher dimension pour out, in ecstasy, the spiritual seed (God's seed, Yod).

This immortal seed passes through Yesod (the phallus) and finally pierces path 21, "Tav" (ת), reality's closed gate and birth canal, striking like lightning into the womb of Malkuth.

That is, the true Mekubbal (Kabbalist practitioner) of Lurianic Kabbalah was not a scholar who simply memorized scripture. He was the most provocative and intense sexual alchemist, becoming himself the empty space of the cosmic dakini (Malkuth / Nukva), burning his inner fierce sexual energy (Mayim Nukvin), and drawing the divine seed of the higher dimension down into the womb of reality.
