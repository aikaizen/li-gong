## Opening the central channel while alive, so consciousness exits upward
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094991896984891456) (quotes [the Sep 1 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094442108530962913))

So that at death consciousness doesn't leave through the lower chakras and be reborn in the realms of hungry ghosts, hell, animals, and so on,

it's necessary to overcome duality and activate the central channel while alive.

Opening that central channel is precisely the method of "afflictions are bodhi," and the fastest way is to use the mechanism of death originally present in the cosmos,

and to use the structure of death while alive, one overcomes duality using sexual energy.

When sexual energy doesn't flow outward but flows into the sushumna, the subtle tube, the blocked central channel loosens and one reaches bliss-emptiness.

The key is the flame that rouses the central channel,
and also making it not be consumed outward for gene replication at the level of survival and reproduction,

but flow into the sushumna tube.

In this process the dakini's blessing
and guidance are needed.

## The dakinis' rescue operation in the bardo
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094960062355902678) (quotes [the Sep 2 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094955836582060042))

To use fast-acting sexual energy to achieve emptiness and great bliss without falling into hell, the dakini's protection and blessing are needed.

When you receive the dakini's blessing, countless dakinis holding red hooks or nooses come directly into the bardo to meet you.

They snatch the practitioner's consciousness (clear light) as it's about to be swept away by the karmic winds and pull it up like lightning into the dakini heaven.

This is closer to the dakini's characteristic rough, dynamic "rescue operation" than to gentle consolation.

They make you dwell in cosmic orgasm in the dakini heaven, the space of boiling great bliss (Mahasukha).

## Sexual energy: more efficient than hwadu or vipassana, and more dangerous
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094955836582060042) (quotes [the Sep 1 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094756484399951979))

Using sexual energy, the mechanism of the cosmos, is far more efficient than hwadu or vipassana for realizing "afflictions are Buddha-nature," but the danger is great too. Tantric history says that misusing sexual energy ends in falling into hell.

But it isn't that there are no countermeasures. And so following the natural flow itself brings about liberation.

## A seed in front of the spine, behind the heart, bright as the sun
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094921054288544221)

*(Translator's note: written in casual speech, as a first-person practice report.)*

After asking Akshobhya Vajra for blessing,
I meditated on the FARAON mantra
in the vajra indestructible bright drop.

Something like a very tiny seed
formed in front of the spine, behind the heart,
and grew brighter and brighter until it was like the sun.

Then the spine at the back, around thoracic vertebrae 4 and 5, straightened, and it felt as if the body were floating upward.
