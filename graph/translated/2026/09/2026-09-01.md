## Samael Aun Weor: those who despise sex fall into Lilith's realm
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094792065637024209) (quotes [the Sep 1 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094442108530962913))

According to a very distinctive and provocative claim in Samael Aun Weor's philosophy, those who regard sex as dirty, despise it, and repress it fall into the realm of Lilith.

He held that ascetic monks, fanatics who treat sex as sin, and deformed sexual deviants who go against life energy belong to this realm.

He saw sexual energy as a manifestation of the "Holy Spirit" and the sacred fire that builds the universe.

So he regarded despising sex itself as an ignorant act of blaspheming and rejecting God's most powerful tool of creation.

So rather than becoming a heavenly being without afflictions and delusions at death, having a human body [is better for] the Buddha-nature of emptiness and non-duality…

## The sacred and profane are not separate
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094640275549475078) (quotes [the Aug 31 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094278172099637438))

The sacred and profane are not separate. Holy history comes with pleasure.

萬物負陰而抱陽 沖氣以為和: guarding yin and embracing yang, all things achieve harmony through the blending qi.

There must be a separation of bliss and emptiness (락공분리 樂空分離) for wisdom and method to arise. Wisdom is yin; method is yang.

Awakening without pleasure is merely empty.

*(Translator's note: the classical line is from the Daodejing, chapter 42; standard translation: "All things carry yin and embrace yang; through the blending of qi they reach harmony.")*
