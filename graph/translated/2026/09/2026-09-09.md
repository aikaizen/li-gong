## The dakini is not the god's secretary
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097513432413257960) (quotes [the Sep 8 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097056479555846544))

In Highest Yoga Tantra the dakini is not a secretary assisting the male deity.

If the male deity is "the moving power" (method / great bliss), the female deity is "the vacuum womb that holds it and makes it explode" (wisdom / emptiness).

Without yab-yum (sexual union), the meshing of these two gears, no engine in the universe turns for even one second.

Highest Yoga Tantra is the master of great bliss (대락 大樂) and passion, which does not suppress greed and intense passion but rather detonates them and sublimates them into wisdom.

It fuses the extreme pleasure (great bliss) and empty wisdom (emptiness) that arise when you kindle tummo (inner fire) in the lower dantian, melt down the white bodhicitta at the crown, and burn the heart's firm knot like a furnace.
