[Kabbalah path 18, for walking alone (독행)]

Energy that has risen past the heart chakra reaches the throat chakra. At the throat chakra the feminine principle (the womb) raises the child.

Western alchemy often misunderstands this: the union of man and woman does not mean an actual physical union. Tibetan tantra was wary of that case, held that only masters could do it, and saw it as doing more harm than good.

The union of yin and yang is the union of the feminine and masculine principles within one's own body. When one can unite oneself, he (she) becomes one who walks alone. And this is efficient when using the feminine principle.

- Archangels: Kamael (5) → Tzaphkiel (3)

- Bodily: between the throat chakra (5th) and the third-eye center (6th).

- Effects 1 (pathworking): transcending the limits of time and space and deepening astral travel, union with the whole collective, breaking through walls of limitation, increased precognition and channeling.

- Effects 2 (tarot symbolism): tough outside and soft inside, protection, defense, challenge, conquest, cooperation, collaboration, unity, drive.

## The union of feminine and masculine: Kabbalah path 19 and the birth of Horus
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100378777981468972) (quotes [the Sep 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100075901820322301))

[The union of the feminine and masculine principles, Kabbalah path 19]

The birth of Horus, from the union of yin and yang

1. Set's usurpation and the mutilation of Osiris

Osiris, god of fertility and king of Egypt, was killed through the jealousy and scheming of his brother Set. Set cut Osiris's body into 14 pieces and scattered them throughout Egypt.

2. Isis's search and magical restoration

Isis, Osiris's wife and the great goddess of magic, set out to find her husband's scattered body. She succeeded in finding and assembling all the pieces except one part, the genitals (eaten by a Nile fish called the oxyrhynchus). So Isis made magical genitals of gold (or clay), attached them to Osiris's body, and wrapped him as a mummy, restoring his body whole.

3. The breath of life and conception

Isis turned into a kite (or falcon) and circled over Osiris's body. With powerful wingbeats she raised a magic wind and breathed the breath of life into Osiris temporarily. Uniting with the revived Osiris, Isis at last succeeded in receiving his semen (seed), and thus conceived Horus.

4. Hiding and the birth of Horus

Osiris then left to become king of the underworld (the afterlife), and Isis fled from Set's eyes. She hid in Khemmis, the papyrus marshes of the Nile delta, and bore Horus alone. Isis protected her son by magic from threats such as the vipers and scorpions Set sent, and raised Horus in secret.
