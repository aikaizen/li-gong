## Changing your luck means being reborn
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2101526624823026082) (quotes [the Sep 10 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097901142474953117))

There may be many ways to change one's luck, but one must be reborn and the Supreme Pole (황극 皇極) must take shape.

It isn't simply wearing colors that suit you or anything like that.

The Korean philosophical text *Cheonbu-gyeong* (천부경 天符經) calls this "three and four form a ring; five, seven, one" (삼사성환 오칠일 三四成環 五七一).

Your luck changes only when you ride the Merkaba, the chariot of God.
