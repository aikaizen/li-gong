## When fire reaches its extreme, metal is born: Kabbalah path 22
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099840764876657132) (quotes [the Sep 15 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099699744566325712))

[When light reaches its extreme, metal is born, Kabbalah path 22]

What matters in spiritual pilgrimage is the process of handling fire to make metal. If you only let the fire blaze, you fall into the illusions of "fire entering, demons possessing" or into self-intoxication. When fire reaches its extreme, it must become metal. This is called the principle of "fire at its extreme generates metal" (화극생금 火極生金).

In the East, metal energy is the qi of austere killing (숙살지기 肅殺之氣), because it is an energy that kills and removes things. The energy of Geng-Shen (경신 庚申), in which that metal energy works properly, defeats other perverse metal energies. This is justice.

What matters most in practice is handling metal energy well. If you can't handle metal energy, you waste a lot of time just grasping at floating clouds.

- Sephiroth traveled: Tiphereth (6) → Geburah (5)

- Hebrew letter: visualize ל Lamed shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Eloah va-Daath (6) → Elohim Gibor (5)

- Archangels: Raphael (6) → Kamael (5)

- Planet / sign / element: Libra (10.31–11.23)

- Bodily: between the heart chakra (4th) and the throat chakra (5th).

- Effects: following one's true calling, stripping away aspects that don't serve the best, expressing the true will centered in the heart (Tiphereth), restoring cosmic balance, improving balanced judgment.

Photo: *Critique of Judgment*, Kant

*(Translator's note: in Korean, the same word 화극 can be read as "fire overcoming" (火剋) or "fire at its extreme" (火極); here he writes the latter.)*

## Death and rebirth: Kabbalah path 23
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099699744566325712) (quotes [the Sep 15 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099649307406377421))

[Kabbalah path 23, of death and rebirth]

The water that appears here is cosmic water. Just as one is reborn on going into water and coming out again, the ego dies hung upside down on the cross and a new consciousness is born. It's the road from Mercury of water to Mars of fire; there must be water for the sun to be born.

From the alchemical perspective, the role of Haejoeum Gwaneum (해조음관음, the Avalokiteshvara of the sound of the tide) is to give birth to the vajra body through water.

- Sephiroth traveled: Hod (8) → Geburah (5)

- Hebrew letter: visualize מ Mem shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: Elohim Tzabaoth (8) → Elohim Gibor (5)

- Archangels: Michael (8) → Kamael (5)

- Planet / sign / element: Venus / Libra (10.31–11.23) / Water

- Bodily: between the solar plexus chakra (3rd) and the throat chakra (5th).

- Effects: recognizing the convergence and discharge of energy, overcoming resistance through deep breath, the art of non-manifestation and gaining objectivity, acquiring healing ability.

## Scorpio of reversal: Kabbalah path 24
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099649307406377421) (quotes [the Sep 15 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099622714093027701))

[Scorpio of reversal, Kabbalah path 24]

Kabbalah path 24, moving from Netzach of victory to Tiphereth of the chest, is Scorpio. 🦂 The scorpion is taken as death because of its deadly poison. At the same time, it is the death needed to be born as the phoenix, the immortal bird. So it is called rising from the brink of death (기사회생). There must be death for there to be resurrection.

Receiving the blessing of path 24, you come to use difficult situations in reverse.

- Sephiroth traveled: Netzach (7) → Tiphereth (6)

- Hebrew letter: visualize נ Nun shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Tzabaoth (7) → YHVH Eloah va-Daath (6)

- Archangels: Haniel (7) → Raphael (6)

- Planet / sign / element: Mars / Scorpio (11.24–11.29)

- Bodily: between the solar plexus chakra (3rd) and the heart chakra (4th).

- Effects: forming a sense of cosmic oneness, that you are part of everything and everything is part of you; imagination and regeneration that turn misfortune into blessing.

## Your own nature awakens: Kabbalah path 25
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099622714093027701) (quotes [the Sep 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099394474099212683))

[Kabbalah path 25, where your own nature awakens]

At last, path 25, where you meet your own nature. It builds the inner strength to live as yourself, and it is the path where you first meet your true self hidden in the chest. A self-sufficient joy forms, often experienced as the energy of bliss that arises when yin and yang unite.

- Sephiroth traveled: Yesod (9) → Tiphereth (6)

- Hebrew letter: visualize ס Samekh shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: Shaddai El Chai (9) → YHVH Eloah va-Daath (6)

- Archangels: Gabriel (9) → Raphael (6)

- Planet / sign / element: Jupiter / Sagittarius (11.30–01.20) / Fire

- Bodily: between the sacral chakra (2nd) and the heart chakra (4th).

- Effects: trusting the inner guide, overcoming the spiritual ego, connecting an alchemical channel to higher dimensions, developing self-responsibility, improving the ability to heal others.
