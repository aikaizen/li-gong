## Practice begins with other-power: turning the light around and forming the true seed
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2103638631894405434) (quotes [the Sep 25 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2103326036431974658))

A common misunderstanding among those practicing Seondo is that by gathering qi in the lower dantian through breathing you can become an immortal by your own power.

Practice does not succeed by the physical technique of dantian breathing (self-power); it begins only when it meets "other-power" (타력 他力), the entry of the fundamental light of the cosmos (the Holy Spirit, the nature-light of empty spirit 허령성광, the light of Vairocana Buddha).

When the light of a higher dimension (the sun) shines on the sea of a lower dimension (the moon, the kidneys), the two wheels rotate, generating light and heat and producing interference light.

This is "turning the light around to shine back" (회광반조 廻光返照), far more important than breathing.

When light crosses and interferes, the primordial seed-information of the cosmos (the original spirit) first floats in the void as a 3D "hologram" (영 影, shadow-image). Metallic energy (qi 氣) extracted from the physical body sticks to this form of light (principle 理) and is fixed, and it is made into a concrete elixir (건금 乾金, Qian-metal).

The "true seed" (진종자, the elixir) that forms in our body (the lower dantian, the one opening of the mysterious female 현빈일규) through turning the light around is not mere energy, but a condensation of the virtual world (the hologram, the shadow-image) containing all the information of the primordial cosmos (the original spirit).

"A world hidden in a grain of millet" (일립속중장세계 一粒粟中藏世界) speaks of this.

The true seed (진종자 眞種子), the root seed of the cosmos, is formed.

Left as is, the true seed formed through turning the light around (the primordial egg, the hologram) scatters (leaks).

To prevent this, a sealing work is needed that applies light (光) and heat (火) to harden it into a solid, and the state in which the seed is kept without scattering is called "the end of leaking" (누진 漏盡).

So, looking at King Wen's Later Heaven nine-palace diagram, the direction in which the true seed of the cosmos (Qian-yang-metal 건양금) is generated is the northwest, "6 White Metal Star" (6백금성).

The essence of practice is not artificial breathing methods. The real beginning is "turning the light around" (turning the light to shine back), shining the light (光) in the fire of the heart (the Li trigram) onto the water of the kidneys (the Kan trigram, the one opening of the mysterious female) to generate heat (energy).

The light of the cosmos (the Holy Spirit) can never be received with the selfish greed (the Lesser Vehicle) of wanting to live forever alone. True light descends only when there is the heart of a "bodhisattva" (the Great Vehicle) ready to sacrifice oneself for others.

Otherwise, if you just circulate energy with artificial breathing and greed, you get engulfed in false fire (illusion) and fall into "fire entering, demons possessing" (주화입마, devils).

The hologram grain grows more and more refined and becomes precise in the structure of an "eye" (the original ring-image 원환영). This interaction of light ascends from the physical body → astral body → causal body, unlocking the seal of the true self sleeping at each layer, and finally awakens the spirit-embryo infant (신태영아 神胎嬰兒, the light body).

Li (離 ☲), the fire trigram in the chest (heart), and Kan (坎 ☵), the water trigram in the lower dantian (kidneys), interact. The inside of Li (the chest) is empty (yin), and the inside of Kan (the dantian) is full (true yang). As the full yang qi of the dantian (the yang line) rises to fill the empty space of the chest (the yin line), Qian (乾 ☰, Qian-yang-metal), pure yang energy, is completed.

In that process, human consciousness also deepens and expands from consciousness dominated by the reptilian brain (the serpent's egg), to the consciousness of the limbic brain (the phoenix's egg), and on to the egg of light (the third eyeball).

## Refining essence into qi: what dantian breathing really is
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2103530544894263774) (quotes [the Sep 21 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2101958492945047932))

*(Translator's note: this post and the one at 01:30 are written in a playful dialect register; this is the later posting, with one typo fixed.)*

Many people misunderstand dantian breathing as absorbing and storing outside air or qi (氣).

But true refining of essence into qi is not outside qi; it's the process of vaporizing (기화 氣化) the essence (精) already stored in my body, using water and fire.

You release the energy bound up in certain thoughts and emotions and use it as movable energy.

Breathing only plays a supporting role, using wind (風) to move and push this vaporized energy into a particular womb (the dantian).

Also important here: holding the breath a long time doesn't make refining essence into qi happen.

What matters is shutting the gate so the vaporized energy (qi) is locked in the particular space called the womb and can't leak out, and even more important is that the end of leaking (누진 漏盡) not fail.

Ordinary practitioners focus only on the "microcosmic orbit" that circulates the conception and governing vessels, but the most central goal of the hundred days of building the foundation (백일축기) is opening the "central channel" (the reflective line 반사선) that runs through the exact center of the body.

People don't know that. The central channel road is the essential framework in which the immortal's child (the spirit-embryo infant 신태영아) grows and moves, an invisible line inside the spine, and the place where the practitioner's womb forms.

I'll tell you the knack for forming the central channel, so listen well.

On the in-breath, outer breath comes in, air comes in, but the energy rises from below upward. Picture a small bright jade bead. That bead rises.

On the out-breath, the bead comes back down.

Again, raise it a little more on the in-breath and lower it on the out-breath.

That's how the central channel gradually forms. It's called the reverse-gathering method (역취법).
