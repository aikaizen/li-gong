## Korean Seondo is not quite Daoism
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2105264271496204526)

Rather than Daoism, there is a Seondo (선도 仙道) culture. It's a little different from Daoism. For example, rather than activating Daoist gods or rituals, Seondo has the meaning of self-cultivation and self-development.
