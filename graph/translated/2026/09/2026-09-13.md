## Kabbalah path 32: the power to hide yourself
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099069249251930557) (quotes [the Sep 13 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2098947508487794790))

In the Kabbalah system, path 32 moves from Malkuth, meaning the earth, to Yesod, the low astral plane. One of the effects granted in this process is the power to hide oneself. That is, you gain the power to go your own way without straining to show yourself in tangled human relationships.
