## Waking and dreaming are both illusions; turiya
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2096397843007516881) (quotes [the Sep 2 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2095095620751274233))

The real world we live in and the dreams during sleep are both illusions with a double-matrix structure made by the mind. That is, they're empty.

Passing through the four states of consciousness in Hindu philosophy (waking, dream, deep sleep, turiya) and ultimately reaching the state of "turiya" (clear light), "form is emptiness, emptiness is form": that is the true meaning of "waking and sleeping as one" (오매일여 寤寐一如).
