## Elijah's chariot: the Merkaba as shield and weapon
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2101958492945047932) (quotes [the Sep 13 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2098911575352758606))

*(Translator's note: written in casual speech.)*

According to Kabbalistic tradition, the Merkaba in which Elijah is said to have ascended is special.

The Merkaba (chariot) is a perfect shield and weapon that confines within geometric order, and channels, the fierce cosmic energy that could burn up the body's nervous system.

In Kabbalah the chariot is an important symbol implying the same concept as the Tree of Life.
