## Receiving the seed of Buddha-nature: Kabbalah path 20
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100075901820322301) (quotes [the Sep 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099981511747645786))

[Kabbalah path 20, receiving the seed of Buddha-nature]

Someone outside can guide growth to some degree, but in the end you must go by yourself. Kabbalah path 20 is Virgo and also signifies the seed of Buddha-nature. The seed of Buddha-nature is "י Yod."

It happens to be Virgo season now, so it's a good time to receive the seed of Buddha-nature and to raise it.

Visualizing Yod is known as a path by which wisdom arises.
Wisdom lies in making a womb yourself and raising a sprout in that womb.

"Maeum" (마음, mind) is "ma-um," meaning a true sprout is budding.

- Sephiroth traveled: Tiphereth (6) → Chesed (4)

- Hebrew letter: visualize י Yod shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Eloah va-Daath (6) → El (4)

- Archangels: Raphael (6) → Tzadkiel (4)

- Related mythic figures: Chiron, Hermes (Mercury), Moses

- Planet / sign / element: Virgo (09.17–10.30)

- Bodily: between the heart chakra (4th) and the throat chakra (5th).

- Effects 1 (pathworking): practicing a life of serving and purifying others, avoiding excess in life, expressing a heart of compassion, embodying and teaching humility.

- Effects 2 (tarot symbolism): composure, reflection, analysis, criticism, inquiry, awakening, practicality, wisdom.

## Escaping the wheel of fortune and growing luck: Kabbalah path 21
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099981511747645786) (quotes [the Sep 15 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2099840764876657132))

[How to escape the wheel of fortune and grow good luck, Kabbalah path 21]

Freud misinterpreted the libido with the term "Oedipus complex." Oedipus's journey is not a journey to possess the father's woman, the mother.

It was realizing that one discovers the femininity within oneself and grows Buddha-nature on the basis of that femininity.

When you master the principle by which the yin and yang of the cosmos meet, that is, learn it with the body, you can escape the wheel of fortune and realize Jupiter's energy of growth. That is good luck.

- Sephiroth traveled: Netzach (7) → Chesed (4)

- Hebrew letter: visualize כ Kaph shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Tzabaoth (7) → El (4)

- Archangels: Haniel (7) → Tzadkiel (4)

- Planet / sign / element: Jupiter

- Bodily: between the solar plexus chakra (3rd) and the throat chakra (5th).

- Effects: breaking old patterns, purifying the soul, understanding the cycle of life and death, the capacity to bear risk, developing intuition, enhancing superhuman hearing and dream interpretation.
