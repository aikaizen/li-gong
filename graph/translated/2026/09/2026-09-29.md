## The ego and the po soul are to be integrated, not destroyed
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2104834510693322802) (quotes [the Sep 25 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2103284279937794192))

The "dissolution of the self" that most spiritual teachers proclaim is only a phenomenon that occurs at the ultimate stage; for those who haven't reached that process to attempt it can be a wrong approach and even arrogance.

The ego and its root, the "po" (백 魄), are not things to be forcibly extinguished, but things to be trained, purified, and integrated with the original self (the original spirit 원신 元神)…

## The Big Dipper's seven stars as the body's seven chakras
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2104747349226684738) (quotes [the Sep 24 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2102918871770620091))

The Big Dipper (북두칠성 北斗七星) is an asterism of seven bright stars forming a ladle shape in the night sky, located around the hips and tail of Ursa Major.

Korean alchemy sees the seven stars of the Big Dipper (Tanlang, Jumen, Lucun, Wenqu, Lianzhen, Wuqu, Pojun) as corresponding to the seven "chakras" of the human body.
