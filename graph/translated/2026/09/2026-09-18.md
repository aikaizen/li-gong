## The Zohar's cosmic sexual union
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100912717536305381) (quotes [the Sep 13 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2098911575352758606))

*(Translator's note: written in casual speech.)*

The *Zohar*, the most classic Kabbalistic text, is itself full of symbols of great sexual union.

The Zohar saw the restoration of the cosmos (Tikkun) as accomplished through the cosmic sex (union) of central "Tiphereth" (the Holy One, the masculine principle) and the lowest "Malkuth" (Shekhinah, the feminine principle).

In particular, the 9th Sephirah, Yesod (Foundation), symbolizes precisely the "genitals" in cosmic anatomy.

The awakened one called the righteous (Tzadik) was described as one who gathers his pure energy in this Yesod, pours the light of the higher dimension into the material world, and shoots it back upward.

## The secret magic of reality creation: Kabbalah path 14, feminine energy
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100857736561041840) (quotes [the Sep 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100838186985701870))

[The secret magic of reality creation: feminine energy, Kabbalah path 14]

Path 14 is where the feminine and masculine principles combine, and its associated planet is Venus. The feminine principle means Understanding. The masculine principle means the seed of information.

When the feminine principle receives and understands the masculine principle, reality can take on a concrete form and be born.

Venus has the power to give form to formless fire energy and make it real.

- Sephiroth traveled: Binah (3) → Chokmah (2)

- Hebrew letter: visualize ד Daleth shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Elohim (3) → Yah / YHVH (2)

- Archangels: Tzaphkiel (3) → Raziel (2)

- Related mythic figures: Aphrodite, Freya

- Planet / sign / element: Venus

- Bodily: the left-right connection within the third-eye center (6th chakra) (the pituitary line).

- Effects 1 (pathworking): catching chances to grow beyond limits, creative power that breathes life into dreams (conception), insight into wholeness, cosmic orgasm and the creation of harmony.

- Effects 2 (tarot symbolism): romance, love, family, abundance, vitality, libido, creation, childbirth, stability.

## Help from a noble woman: Kabbalah path 17
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100745689471099336) (quotes [the Sep 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2100478816103866596))

[Kabbalah path 17, with help from a noble woman]

In Indian myth, Lakshmi signifies a noble woman who helps. When Kabbalah path 17 is activated, there are good romantic connections, and in particular you meet a precious woman.

Interestingly, a sword often appears in the images for path 17. It can be the sword that protects oneself and the community, and also the sword with which a mother protects her family.

- Sephiroth traveled: Tiphereth (6) → Binah (3)

- Hebrew letter: visualize ז Zain shining brightly.

- Divine-name mantra: YHVH Eloah va-Daath (6) → YHVH Elohim (3)

- Archangels: Raphael (6) → Tzaphkiel (3)

- Related mythic figures: sacrificed gods (Osiris, Krishna, the Buddha, Jesus)

- Planet / sign / element: Mercury / Gemini (06.22–07.20)

- Bodily: between the heart chakra (4th) and the third-eye center (6th).

- Effects 1 (pathworking): heightened physical and psychic senses, union with cosmic consciousness, inner stillness, understanding the principle of opposition and difference, finding a soul friend.

- Effects 2 (tarot symbolism): opportunity, encounter, love, marriage, temptation, absorption, obsession, passion, help from a noble person.
