## How the third eye opens: the link between heart and eyes
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097294792409649444) (quotes [the Sep 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2095782977406181482))

How the third eye opens: the connection between heart and eyes

The third eye is not a trivial organ for seeing ghosts or reading auras.

It is because it's the ultimate point of union where the sun (emptiness) and moon (great bliss) are folded into one (bliss-emptiness 대락공성).

An arrow shot from the zero point of the heart pierces the target at the forehead, and is completed as a sacred lens that intuits the entire cosmos "as it is."

The fundamental clear light that bursts out as the heart chakra's indestructible bright drop opens is not a cold, stagnant empty space.

It holds the energy of the fierce red spark (red bodhicitta) ignited in the inner yoni.

This is the "morning sun" of awareness (the sun, pingala) that tears the darkness of ignorance and surges up the central channel.

This vast life force rises, turning discriminating delusions to ash.

At the next stage, at the point between the brows (ajna chakra) and the crown, the white dew, the "moon" (white bodhicitta, the energy of the pituitary / pineal), resides.

This is the masculine principle, symbolizing great bliss (Mahāsukha) and compassion.

When the red morning sun surges up fiercely, this cold, still moonlight energy doesn't flee but prepares to meet the flame.

The moment the surging morning sun (wisdom / emptiness) reaches the point between the brows, the white moon (method / great bliss) embraces the red sun.

In this collision of extreme energies, fire boiling water and water enveloping fire as they fuse, the world of duality (sun and moon, left and right channels, subject and object) finally collapses.

## Tögal: seeing with the heart for liberation, and the kati channel
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097209574013178067) (quotes [the Sep 2 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094991896984891456))

[How to see with the heart for liberation]

Tögal means "leaping over the skull."

It isn't a road of piling up concepts, but a road on which the heart's luminosity bursts out through the eyes along a special energy pathway and directly shows its own nature in space.

If liberation is the goal, don't cling to this phenomenon as a mystery; let everything that appears go as the play of your own awareness.

The special pathway from the heart to the eyes is called the kati channel.

Kati is the passage through which that liberation bursts directly from the heart through the eyes. It's a different layer altogether from the ordinary tantric nadis (left, right, central channels).

It's a channel of luminosity that comes fully to light only in Dzogchen, especially in Tögal.

Starting from the "lamp of the flesh" (citta lamp of the flesh) in the very center of the heart, two strands transparent like hollow crystal tubes, about one-eighth the thickness of a horsehair, branch like the horns of a wild yak, go around behind the ears, and end at the pupils of both eyes.

See with the heart.

Mystifying this channel as a "special energy passage" and hiding it, or veiling it as "only for the qualified," is itself another attachment.

The real thing is to open your eyes, gaze into space, and directly confirm the moment the heart flows out through the eyes.

If you try to understand it as a concept, the brain blocks it again.

Just look.

From the heart, the eyes open.

## The third eye as the ultimate cosmic eclipse
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2097203735277773295) (quotes [the Sep 5 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2096063664487068130))

The third eye (ajna chakra) is not a cheap magic trick that opens out of nowhere on the forehead. It is the ultimate cosmic eclipse (일식 日食), produced when fundamental energy ignited in the deepest place of the heart, the indestructible drop, surges up the spine (central channel) and collides with the energy of the head.
