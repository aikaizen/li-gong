## How qi rises: from the governing vessel to the central channel
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2104379310438224115)

When energy rises from below, at first it goes up the governing vessel (독맥) along the spine; as practice progresses further, it rises through the central channel (중맥). A sensation of the central channel develops.

In opening the central channel, reverse-gathering breathing (역취법) is important: you learn the sensation that while air comes in with outer breathing, the energy rises up through the middle of the body.
