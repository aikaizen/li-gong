## Receiving Vajrasattva's seed: a dakini visualization
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2096159877274255653) (quotes [the Sep 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2095782977406181482))

We who live in the world below form cannot perceive light itself. Vajrasattva is the light of Mahavairocana (대일여래) taking on form.

Following the yin-yang principle of the cosmos, Vajrasattva's light is planted as a seed in the body of a practitioner whose subtle body has been made a goddess. Because sentient beings are the Buddha-mother (불모) who gives birth to the Buddha.

The visualization for this forms a bond with the dakini.

1. The seed syllable HRIH (흐릿, also 후라후), symbolizing the dakini, shines red.

2. That seed shines red in the practitioner's 2nd chakra.

3. Next, visualize the seed syllable BAM, symbolizing the womb. Again visualize it red.

4. In that state the 2nd chakra is activated and a distinctive pleasant feeling arises.

5. In that state, visualize the blue seed syllable HUM, symbolizing the seed of light, being planted on top of the BAM seed.

6. After doing this, slowly open your eyes, become aware of the space between the brows, and become aware of the whole face. This is the process of activating the trigeminal nerve and returning to reality.

In this way, the Tibetan esoteric teaching is a system that practices by using mechanisms found in nature.

## Vajrasattva's hundred-syllable mantra
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2096004623341637908) (quotes [the Sep 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2095782977406181482))

Vajrasattva's blessing descends like nectar and extinguishes karmic obstructions. When karmic obstructions are extinguished, it's pictured as if they melt and flow out. And the indestructible bright drop forms in the heart chakra.

The Hundred-Syllable Mantra (백자진언 百字眞言) for receiving Vajrasattva's blessing is as follows.

In tantric practice it's recited while visualizing each and every wave of the sound as clear nectar (liquid light) running down the spine.

Looking at a picture of Vajrasattva, connect the HUM seed in Vajrasattva's chest with the HUM seed in your own chest and recite the hundred-syllable mantra below.

Hundred-syllable mantra:

Om Vajrasattva Samayam Anupalaya
Vajrasattva Tvenopatishtha
Dridho Me Bhava
Sutoshayo Me Bhava
Suposhayo Me Bhava
Anurakto Me Bhava
Sarva Siddhim Me Prayaccha
Sarva Karma Su Chame
Cittam Shriyam Kuru Hum
Ha Ha Ha Ha Ho
Bhagavan Sarva Tathagata
Vajra Ma Me Munca
Vajri Bhava
Maha Samaya Sattva
Ah Hum Phat

Short heart mantra:
Om Vajrasattva Hum
