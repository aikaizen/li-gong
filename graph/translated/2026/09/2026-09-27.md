## Why we age and die: the original spirit and the knowing spirit

## Why we are born human
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2104034769764057288) (quotes [the Sep 23 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2102641624434331707))

From the Seondo perspective, the purpose of being born human is to become an "immortal" (신선 神仙). A human body is needed to become that immortal, so being born human in this life is seen as a great opportunity in the cycle of rebirth.
