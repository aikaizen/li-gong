## The secret in the Cundi mantra
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088850443434934696) (quotes [the Aug 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088792079673790854))

The bodhisattva who embraces our gate of arising and ceasing, to guide us to the Dharma-body server for a hard reset of karmic obstruction, is Cundi Bodhisattva.

So the Cundi mantra holds a secret.

Om Cale Cule Cundi Svaha, *Burim*

"Om" means setting up the altar.

In "Jarye" (자례, Cale),
"Ja" is the infinitely expanding place of no-thought.
"Rye" means the microcosmic orbit and the continuation of qi.

It expands from the limited individual
to infinite Dharma consciousness.

In "Jurye" (주례, Cule),
"Ju" connects this infinite place of no-thought with the place of no-mind at the base of the chest. In "Ju," the vowel "u" plays that role.

When the place of no-thought and the place of no-mind are connected, you're in a state where you can receive the Buddha's empowerment and prediction (관정수기).

"*Burim*" at the end of the mantra means a special breathing method and lets you turn the fire wheel among the five great elements of space, wind, water, fire, and earth.

Turning the fire wheel burns karmic consciousness.

[Om Cale Cule Cundi Svaha, *Burim*]

If you do the vocalization of the mantra "properly,"
the 8th consciousness comes alive and you receive the Dharma's blessing.

## The tea ceremony as *charye* (차례)
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088818823747215364)

Yes~~ it's performing a rite (*ye/rye*, 예/례) through the tea ceremony. That's why it's called *charye* (차례, "tea rite," also ancestral rite). ^^

"Ch" is a strong pronunciation, energy that repels evil qi;

"r" circulates the qi in the microcosmic orbit (down the front, up the back), like tea water convecting;

"ye" connects the energy at the body's center with the surface energy.

This is contained in the tea ceremony of drinking tea. ^^

## Merit: waking and caring for the soul-consciousness within
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088806380056789187) (quotes [the Aug 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088675597199560755))

Even if we have Buddha-nature, becoming a Buddha is another matter. That's because merit must be accumulated.

The reason many disciples of the Buddha practice through cycles of rebirth is also that one life isn't enough time to accumulate merit.

Among merits, the core merit is the merit of great love and great compassion. And the first of these is waking and caring for the soul-consciousness that cohabits within you.

When rapport builds with your soul-consciousness, the soul-consciousness's abilities, what are called supernormal powers, come into play.

For example, seeing your own thoughts as images, or coming to know other people's states.

When your own soul wakes, you can use what saju calls the useful god (용신 🐉).

*(Translator's note: 용신 means "useful element" in saju; written with 用 it reads literally "using the god," and it sounds like 龍神 "dragon god," hence the emoji.)*

## The 8th consciousness and Cundi, who opens the source
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088792079673790854) (quotes [the Aug 15 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088453846528082168))

The reason we can hard-reset our karmic consciousness through synchronization with the Dharma body, despite our individual selves' many karmic impressions, is that we already have within us the 8th consciousness that communicates with the Dharma body.

There is a primordial god that gives birth to arising and ceasing (the Deer God of *Princess Mononoke*), and this primordial god only gives birth to arising and ceasing and has no interest in the suffering of beings.

But this primordial god and all the beings within it also have the 8th consciousness, and that 8th consciousness becomes a path that communicates with Amitabha Buddha.

Tantra's identical-Dharma-realm practice, that is, practice of synchronizing with Amitabha Buddha, builds the environment that activates that source. And the bodhisattva who opens that source while protecting us from the demonic realm and guiding us is Cundi Bodhisattva.

In tantra, all we have to do is build the environment for Cundi Bodhisattva to act, and long for Cundi Bodhisattva.

## Using the god: the useful element in saju
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088675597199560755)

Useful god (용신): people often talk about the useful element in saju, but it isn't using a god formulaically and mechanically.

Waking the soul within you through a certain method, interacting with that soul, and manifesting supernormal powers together with it: that is *yongsin*, that is, using the god as an agent.

That is, you need merit to be able to use the useful god. That merit is the merit of great love and great compassion.
