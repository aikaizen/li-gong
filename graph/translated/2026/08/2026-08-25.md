## Reality Transurfing, excess potential, and the vow from the zero point

In Reality Transurfing, the reason wishes don't come true and instead produce a counter-reaction (resistance) is that when you assign "importance" to a particular object and hold desire or attachment based on lack, "excess potential" arises.

To eliminate this excess energy, the universe applies "balancing forces" that obstruct the wish's fulfillment.

That's why the more you do things like attraction, the more your life becomes worn out in fantasy.

From the viewpoint of tantric physiology, the root cause producing this excess energy can be seen as exactly the "karmic winds" that flow ceaselessly through the subtle channels of our body, stirring up discrimination, attachment, and fear (ego).

And as presented in *The Tibetan Book of the Dead*, upon reaching the clear light, the Dharma body, the obstacles of the karmic consciousness we create pause, and in that state, the zero point, one makes a vow.

In the state of the Dharma body, the image of "I (Ego)" has collapsed, so there is no lack, no attachment, no fear. Since there is no lack, the very "importance" assigned to any goal becomes completely zero (0).

Therefore the "vow" raised in this empty state of emptiness (great bliss) is not the ego's anxious "wish," but the universe's own "pure intention," which generates no balancing force at all.

Since there is no excess energy, that vow itself slides smoothly into shape as physical reality (the emanation body, Nirmanakaya) without any counter-reaction.

In Tibetan tantra, the special state for that zero point is created through the body..
