## The origin of the White-Robed Great Being dharani
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2084279563757240612)

The origin of the White-Robed Great Being dharani (백의대사신주 白衣大士神呪)

This mantra doesn't come from the orthodox Buddhist canon (정장 正藏), composed in India and translated into Chinese; it's a "people's dharani," born spontaneously and polished in the desperate lives of East Asian common people as it passed through China.

According to the best-known Chinese tale, long ago, when someone faced execution under a false accusation, or wandered at death's door with an incurable illness, the compassionate Avalokiteshvara (the White-Robed Great Being), dressed in white from head to toe, appeared in a dream.

The bodhisattva taught them a short incantation and said, "If you recite this incantation 10,000 times (or 12,000 times), you'll surely live." When they woke and recited it day and night in prison (or on their sickbed), they were miraculously pardoned or the illness was healed as if washed away.

[The bodhisattva] was indigenized as "the most familiar and compassionate mother, who doesn't stay in temples but comes down to the marketplace in white clothes and directly soothes the suffering of ordinary people."

Over thousands of years, countless people at the crossroads of life and death, or in desperate crises, have recited this mantra with the same sound ("Tadyatha Om Garabada…") and an earnest heart.

As that vast aspiration and its waves accumulated beyond time and space, the White-Robed Great Being dharani went beyond mere letters to form a powerful "energy field of healing and protection (morphogenetic field)."

## Three-step talisman reading: new creation, firm territory, a brilliant start

Rather than carrying everything alone, it signifies the obstacles and blessings arising in all events where association is formed by mutual agreement, as in a business partnership.

Since it signifies all problems and solutions arising not only between spouses but between business partners, your top task is finding a trustworthy partner to lead a new project together and coordinating the risks in that relationship.

🛡️ Step 2: Sul-day talisman (술일부 戌日符): a sturdy mentality and firm defense of territory

You need to solidify your substance with the energy of yang earth (양토 陽土). Apply to investing and business the stable mindset of basically preferring harmony to competition and achievement to conquest.

But defend your assets thoroughly with the fierceness of being a pacifist who turns very aggressive when your territory is invaded.

It's a time when your strength of being sturdy and keeping your center well stands out, but bear in mind that you easily suffer from overthinking and an excessive sense of responsibility, and resolutely stop taking on others' unnecessary burdens.

🚪 Step 3: Hakdo Huiseon talisman (학도희선부 學道希仙符): a brilliant start and beginner's risk

It represents learning and mastering something new, going down a new field or path. It means starting anything for the very first time, so you receive the blessing of "the image of many heavenly gods rejoicing and protecting you," and it means there are helpers around you, so move forward boldly.

But you mustn't forget that, having the innocence of not yet knowing anything, there are dangers you haven't seen. It may be an obvious danger you can't see because you don't know it properly, so don't neglect thorough study of the market!
