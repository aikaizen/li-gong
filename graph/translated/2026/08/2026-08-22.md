## Wishing through water: *bam!* and the water-wheel contemplation
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2091141412301808054) (quotes [the Aug 22 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2091138292003852353))

Water is everywhere. So interacting with the original spirit of water means you can make wishes through the medium of water. The method is to say *bam!*, then savor the stillness and speak your wish. The *bam!* of *bam-yanggaeng* (chestnut jelly)! In tantra this is called the water-wheel contemplation (수륜관 水輪觀).

*(Translator's note: "밤양갱" is the title of a popular 2024 Korean song; 밤 (*bam*) is also a seed syllable, VAM, associated with water.)*

## Sensing the water as it boils
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2090997225581322253) (quotes [the Aug 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088796089462018329))

Interacting with the original spirit of water means sensing the process of water boiling.

When water boils, at first it releases a lot of cold qi. There's a point when it has released all the cold qi and starts breathing out heat.

I learned that this is the point when the original spirit of water expresses itself.

## Garuda: the bird that destroys the three poisons, and the shakti it awakens
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2090938061937168861)

Garuda is the bird that destroys the three poisons of greed, anger, and ignorance, symbolized by snakes. The image of Garuda eating snakes [shows that he] removed poison and ignorance (무명 無明) and won spiritual freedom.

In Indian myth, Garuda fought the snakes and, even with amrita, which promises eternal absolute power and immortality, in his grasp, did not covet a single drop.

Moved by the nobility of Garuda, who transcended desire with the single thought of saving his mother, the god Vishnu granted him grace and made him an immortal being.

Garuda himself, destroying the snakes of the three poisons, is masculine, destructive yang (陽) energy,

but the essence of the energy that wakes inside the spine through the Garuda mantra is "feminine energy (Shakti)."

In the 1st chakra (prostate, cervix), the sensual shakti energy of the red bodhicitta lies sleeping.

Under the pressure of the Garuda mantra, the red flame (feminine / red bodhicitta) shoots up the spine to the brow point and melts the white dew (masculine / white bodhicitta).

When the melted dew pours back down the spine, the brain and whole nervous system are engulfed in overwhelming pleasure (Bliss) that transcends orgasm, and realizing empty wisdom (Emptiness) at this moment is tantric yoga.

The being who appears then as the red bodhicitta is the dakini.
