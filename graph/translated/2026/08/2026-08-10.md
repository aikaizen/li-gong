## Uga Benzaiten: the water goddess and the snake god combined
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086781270378643660) (quotes [the Aug 10 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086618065656312281))

What was fun about today's divination was that water and a snake came up together. Uga Benzaiten (우가변재천 宇賀弁財天), in whom the water god and the snake god are combined.

A river goddess who's also a goddess of wealth, and who also has a femme fatale character.

India's great life-nurturing "river (Benzaiten / Sarasvati)" and Japan's "snake of the earth (the god Uga)," which takes in that water and gives forth grain and wealth, are essentially the same energy dealing with abundance.

As the simple goddess of wisdom (弁才天) absorbed the snake's primal life force and greed, her character was completely upgraded into an absolute goddess of capital (弁財天) who draws enormous wealth.

And Gustav Klimt's *Water Serpents*.

*(Translator's note: Benzaiten is written two ways in Japanese: 弁才天 "talent" and 弁財天 "wealth.")*

## Anima and animus: the masculine and feminine within me
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086631098147357004)

[Anima and animus: the masculine and feminine within me]

In early fetal development (until about week 6), every human exists in a bisexual state, holding both the "Wolffian duct," which will become male genitals (vas deferens, epididymis), and the "Müllerian duct," which will become female genitals (uterus, vagina).

In males, the Wolffian duct develops and the Müllerian duct regresses, remaining, as was pointed out earlier, as the empty "prostatic utricle (the male uterus)" in the middle of the prostate.

In females, the Müllerian duct develops into the uterus and vagina, and the Wolffian duct, which would have become the male organs, regresses. But this male seed doesn't disappear completely. Remarkably, it remains hidden for life as fine ducts along both sides of the cervix and the vaginal wall.

In medical terminology this is said to be called "Gartner's duct."

That is, traces of anima and animus remain biologically within our bodies.

## Three-step talisman reading: adversity, aim, explosive action

Begin to unfold the spiritual power itself working behind individuals and groups, and boldly cast off old administrative bonds. If you break through this weighty process, you'll finally meet, in life, all events in which you become head of a certain group or must act as the group's representative. On the basis of the insights and trading philosophy you've meticulously honed, stand tall as the overwhelming leader of the huge new market that's opening!

🧭 Step 2: Sogu-ryeong Samjeong talisman & Su-day talisman: cool-headed targeting and compressing knowledge

The Sogu-ryeong Samjeong talisman signifies the direction to move forward and represents goals. It's a stage of moving toward a new goal with relevant knowledge. But since only basic preparations are done, you must painfully face the fact that depth is lacking.

To make up for this, actively use the energy of the Su-day talisman, the energy that corresponds to water (水) among the five phases and symbolizes "wealth." This period signifies knowledge and information and governs resources and life force, so you should store core energy with deep breathing like spinal bellows and focus on meticulous planning and study.

🔥 Step 3: Sa-day talisman (사일부 巳日符): hushed wildness, and explosive power of execution

Now it's time to execute without hesitation with the energy that corresponds to yin fire (음화 陰火). Outwardly quiet and gentle, firmly maintain your inner strong pride and tenacity and excellent mental strength.

Within the existing organization, respond flexibly without friction, but when entering a position, show the fierce power of execution of looking weak yet being strong, and being soft yet explosive.
