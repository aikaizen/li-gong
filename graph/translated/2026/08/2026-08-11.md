## Three-step talisman reading: aim, break through, make it real
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086978830498218103)

🧭 Step 1: Sogu-ryeong Samjeong talisman: cool-headed targeting and accumulating depth

The first step of a leap is setting a clear compass. Guryeong and Samjeong mean the Big Dipper and the Three Terraces stars respectively. This signifies the direction to move forward and represents goals. It's a stage of moving toward a new goal with relevant knowledge.

But premature conceit is forbidden. Right now only basic preparations are done. So coolly face the lack of depth, and solidify the substance of thorough study and planning before betting in earnest.

🐅 Step 2: In-day talisman (인일부 寅日符): lone-wolf leadership and unstoppable breakthrough

It's time to turn off the noise around you and push ahead with the energy of yang wood (양목 陽木). Exert the overwhelming energy of having a boss's temperament, being very upright and strong, being progressive, and having strong leadership and responsibility.

In the face of asset-market volatility or old meddling from those around you, the attitude of disliking restraint or interference and not listening well to others actually becomes an excellent defense mechanism blocking outside noise.

Pioneer the path you designed without hesitation, with unshakable steadfastness.

🛡️ Step 3: Metal-day talisman (금일부 金日符): concrete materialization and firm control

It's the harvest season for hardening the ambitions in your head into real power and wealth. This tablet corresponds to metal (金) among the five phases and symbolizes "power." It means autumn among the four seasons, the energy of all things converging and being sorted.

Make full use of its traits: condensing scattered assets and plans firmly, having the power to give the formless form, and governing analysis, research, and matters related to public authority. It's the perfect timing to materialize your ideas and trading strategies into a firm system no one can defy!

## Mantra prayer and perfect penetration through the ear faculty
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086943443683807606)

Perfect penetration through the ear faculty: hearing the sound arising in the space 10 cm outside and above the ears.

You come to hear the sound of that space tearing (in my case it's represented as the sound of thunder or clouds).

It's the sound of the tide that Odysseus hears lying on his raft.
