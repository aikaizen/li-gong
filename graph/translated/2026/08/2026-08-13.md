## Three-step talisman reading: firm defense, solid core, cleansing the environment
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2087700473147248804)

🛡️ Step 1: Sul-day talisman (술일부 戌日符): a sturdy mentality and firm defense of territory

It's time to guard your assets with the energy of yang earth (양토 陽土). Keep the stable mindset of basically preferring harmony to competition and achievement to conquest. But defend your profits thoroughly with the fierceness of being a pacifist who turns very aggressive when your territory is invaded.

Use your strength of being sturdy and keeping your center well, and stick to mechanical trading principles like trailing stops to the end. But don't forget that you easily suffer from overthinking and an excessive sense of responsibility, and resolutely cut off herd-following trades that shoulder excessive market risk alone.

⛰️ Step 2: Mi-day talisman (미일부 未日符): an unshakable core and technical armament

You need to firmly solidify your substance with the energy of yin earth (음토 陰土). Outwardly you may seem to lack individuality and self-assertion, but inside sits a powerful core: very strong stubbornness and clear standards. In particular, since you dislike situations beyond your control, a conservative tendency not to change readily can deepen. But you have the definite strength of learning skills quickly and teaching well, so nimbly absorb new trading knowledge, like automated system trading tools or pyramiding entry techniques, and build a solid foundation for defending your assets.

🌍 Step 3: Yanghae Tohwang-sinsal Geumgi talisman: facing environmental limits and cleansing the system

You need to check the external environment blocking asset expansion. Tohwang-sinsal means the surrounding environment a person belongs to. In investing, among the environment and surrounding resources you're in, there are things you can't fully take and use.

That is, you must objectively face that the environment you currently belong to doesn't suit your situation and is uncomfortable.

Rather than staying trapped in variables you can't control, keep in mind that now is a stage of growth through cleansing and resolving all problems coming from the three powers of heaven, earth, and humanity, and coolly rebalance your positions.

## Crypto national bank charters and the lock-in to T-Bills
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2087667361935728940) (quotes [a post by another account](https://x.com/i/status/2087543022644842658))

The aim is to legally complete a structural source of demand for US Treasuries (especially short-term Treasuries, T-Bills).

Crypto firms that obtain national bank status can't hold customer deposits or the reserves of the stablecoins they issue in risky assets.

The direction is to strictly limit, by law, assets to effectively only T-Bills and repos backed by T-Bills.

In exchange for giving crypto firms the indulgence of being "legitimate banks," the liquidity they gather from around the world is locked in at the source as buying of US Treasuries.
