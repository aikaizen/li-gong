## Three-step talisman reading: environment, core, breaking old bonds
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2087330878011969867)

🌍 Step 1: Yanghae Tohwang-sinsal Geumgi talisman: facing environmental limits and cleansing the system

You need to check the external environment blocking asset expansion. Tohwang-sinsal means the surrounding environment a person belongs to.

In investing, among the environment and surrounding resources you're in, there are things you can't fully take and use.

As now, when you're considering migrating to a new platform this coming autumn, you must face that the environment doesn't suit your situation and is uncomfortable.

Rather than staying trapped in variables you can't control, keep in mind that now is a stage of growth through cleansing and resolving all problems coming from the three powers of heaven, earth, and humanity, and objectively reorganize your positions.

⛰️ Step 2: Mi-day talisman (미일부 未日符): an unshakable core and technical armament

You need to firmly solidify your substance with the energy of yin earth (음토 陰土).
Outwardly you may seem to lack individuality and self-assertion.
In particular, since you dislike situations beyond your control, a conservative attitude of not changing readily easily appears.

But inside sits a powerful core: very strong stubbornness and clear standards.

When running your account or preparing a new digital-ecosystem plan, make full use of your strength of learning skills quickly and teaching well, and nimbly absorb new trading techniques and knowledge.

⚔️ Step 3: Soje Gochae Chodo Joheon talisman: breaking chronic old bonds

It's time to pull out the obstacle hidden deepest, for the leap. This corresponds to problems so old they've become chronic.

This old inertia is expressed as *gudok* (고독 蠱毒, gu poison), and it causes life's many hardships and adversities.

In particular, it can stay not only in people's hearts but also in time and space, settle in particular places, and arise in work situations.

Just as you empty turbid qi from deep in the body with spinal bellows breathing, only by fundamentally cutting off the unconscious trading habits or old professional inertia that have bound you for a long time will your suppressed wealth luck burst out powerfully.
