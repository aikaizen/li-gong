## Kriya Yoga raises qi to the bindu, not the crown
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089673120143839664) (quotes [the Aug 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089528568829317537))

When raising qi with the Kriya Yoga mantra, you raise it not to Baihui (the crown) but to the Brahma aperture (범혈). This is called the bindu and is considered to hold moon energy.

Raising qi often causes it to rush to the head (상기), and if Baihui opens, life energy escapes outward;

Kriya Yoga is safe because it uses the bindu (Brahma aperture) rather than Baihui.

## The six central syllables of the Kriya mantra
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089562656474898442) (quotes [the Aug 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089329057133461786))

The Kriya Yoga mantra is

Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya.

Of it, Bha, Ga, Ba, Te, Va, Su,
that is, "Bha-Ga-Ba-Te-Va-Su,"
are the core central six of the 12 syllables.

The vibration of these six syllables
resonates with "Bhagavan Tat Tvam Asi"
("you are that consciousness").

Om (1st chakra)
Na (2nd chakra)
