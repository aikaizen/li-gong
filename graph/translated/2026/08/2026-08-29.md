## Tantra is the resultant vehicle: empowerment from your future self
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093640250635788409) (quotes [the Aug 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093633441187430436))

Empowerment is essential for tantric practice. Usually a teacher regarded as equal to the Buddha performs it for the disciple. But how easy is it to have such a bond?

Tantra is not the causal vehicle (인과승 因果乘), "creating causes to produce results," but the resultant vehicle (과승 果乘), "taking the already completed result as the present path."

That is, your own self who has become the future Dharma body…

## Subject and object must first be fully divided
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093633441187430436) (quotes [the Aug 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093577065161478156))

For subject and object to become one, subject and object must first be thoroughly divided. Only when the gap grows that large can union finally be reached. That is, it's hard to experience emptiness through methods like "get comfortable easily" or "relax." In this respect tantra differs from today's consumerist healing culture. Afflictions becoming emptiness…

## Seeing one's nature is seeing the indestructible bright drop
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093577065161478156) (quotes [the Aug 28 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093167419342954791))

Seeing one's nature (견성) means seeing the indestructible bright drop.

You see the indestructible bright drop in the heart chakra where the red and white bodhicitta have merged. The eye that sees then is the eye of the Dharma body.

Then a field of view arises from the indestructible bright drop as well. This too is the eye of the Dharma body.

The moment you realize the two gazes are the same gaze, I see the bright drop and the bright drop…
