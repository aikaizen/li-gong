## The broken dakini archetype and the Dark Mother
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094308199612694648) (quotes [the Aug 31 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094278172099637438))

For women who have difficulty in relationships with the opposite sex, the archetype of Vajrayogini, the principal deity of the dakinis, is often broken.

In Carl Jung's analytical psychology there's "the Dark Mother" (or the devouring mother).

In analytical psychology, the "Dark Mother" is the other side of the loving mother who conceives and nurtures life.

## Attraction without karma is becoming wholly passive
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2094278172099637438) (quotes [the Aug 25 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2092012483754242192))

Attraction that creates no karma is becoming thoroughly passive. In Tibetan tantra this state is called becoming a dakini: becoming a goddess body and simply receiving the blessing of the Dharma body. In that process the clear light, the bright light in which the karmic winds stop, appears, and mother and child unite (모자합일). What happens simply happens.
