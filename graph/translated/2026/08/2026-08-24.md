## The reverse-drawing breath (역취법)
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2091802270325448967) (quotes [the Aug 21 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2090703749534884308))

There's a breathing method that used to be passed down as secret.

It was made public through Esoteric Qigong..
It's called the reverse-drawing method (역취법).

This breathing method isn't one of those ignorant (?) methods of forcibly lengthening or holding the breath,
or forcibly circulating qi.

It's a very elegant method:

outwardly,
on the in-breath, you breathe in so the air goes down
but the qi comes up.

On the out-breath, you lower the risen qi a little,

on the in-breath you raise that qi up again,

and in this way you raise it to the first cervical vertebra (the level of the earlobes).

If you keep breathing with the reverse-drawing method like this,
the breath becomes subtle and lung breathing decreases and stops.

The breath should become less and stop naturally this way;
you must not use the method of forcibly holding the breath to extend the time.
