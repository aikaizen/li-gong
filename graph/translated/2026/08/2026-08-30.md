## Akshobhya's dakini empowerment

Akshobhya Buddha's dakini empowerment

Empowerment is activating the Buddha-nature seed in the alaya consciousness. To receive empowerment from Akshobhya Buddha, your future self, first make yourself into a dakini.

1. Transformation into a dakini through the seed KHAM

Symbol of Sparśavajrā (촉금강녀, the touch vajra goddess): the "KHAM" seed syllable shown in the image signifies Sparśavajrā.

## Love in the tomb: natural liberation in the bardo
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093834092781715470) (quotes [the Aug 28 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093167419342954791))

Love in the tomb.

Tantra holds that natural liberation is possible. That is, at death, over 49 days, the force of karma is cut seven times, and a moment comes when the clear light, the light of Buddha-nature, shines. If you follow the light at that moment you are liberated; if fear keeps you from following it, you cycle through rebirth again. If you don't miss that chance, even without practice, natural liberation…

## Strong mental power means a firmly seated bright drop
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093750227144704325) (quotes [the Aug 29 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093633441187430436))

Having strong mental power means the indestructible bright drop is firmly seated. In the absolute sense everyone has the indestructible bright drop, but there is always a relative side.

In a state where the indestructible bright drop is firmly compressed in the heart chakra, those who haven't reached that state are merely relative.
