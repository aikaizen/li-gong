Nimbly absorbing new business models and technologies with excellent mental absorption and imitation is the key to multiplying wealth.

It's time to shoot out explosively with the energy that corresponds to wood (木) among the five phases and symbolizes "honor." This means spring among the four seasons, the energy of all things beginning anew.

Don't hesitate any longer to launch a new project or make the independent career shift you've long prepared. Now is the first beginning, with drive, and the optimal timing that governs events of building and raising anew. Stand tall as a leader in a new market with bold decisions and steadfastness!
