## The Vaishravana dharani from the Lotus Sutra
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2085155732555722883) (quotes [the Aug 5 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2085012773709467773))

Vaishravana, the Buddha of the north, was originally a being who guarded the treasures in the earth. A being who guards treasures not yet revealed to the world and repels evil things.

"Ari Nari Nonari Anaro Nari Gunari"

The mantra of Vaishravana (the Heavenly King Who Hears Much, 다문천왕) from the "Dharani" chapter (陀羅尼品) of the *Lotus Sutra* (法華經).

The mantric power of this incantation carrying Vaishravana's protection looks simple on the surface, but within its wave is a fierce dance that cuts away evil energy.

Meaning of the mantra:

- *Ari Nari*: the image of dancing on high with a weapon, warning [evil beings] not to approach.

- *Nonari*: the majesty of dancing in the forest and proclaiming to bad beings.

- *Anaro*: unshakable wholeness.

- *Nari (nadi)*: the pulse of endless life force flowing in the heart.

- *Gunari*: a special guardian who surely protects us.

The greatest purpose of this mantra is to repel and protect so that you meet no worries whatsoever within a radius of a hundred yojanas (a distance so vast an arrow could fly it a hundred times).

It becomes the biggest, most dependable umbrella, keeping outside forces or evil maras (bad news, envy, fraud, etc.) that would take away wealth and abundance from even daring to approach the bounds of my life.

From the viewpoint of vocal qigong, this mantra, through the repeated strikes of its consonants, gives the nervous system a powerful "boundary alarm" and "vitality" at the same time.

"N" (*nari*, *nonari*): spinal vibration and the rising of the sympathetic nerves:

Pronouncing "n" vibrates the spine gently from the coccyx up and stimulates the sympathetic nerves. This relaxes the body while sharply raising the antennas of the senses. Rather than making the amygdala, the part of the brain that detects threats, tremble with anxiety, it helps norepinephrine release so you face the situation clearly and coolly, like a general taking the battlefield.

"R" (*aro*, *nari*, *gunari*): the turning of the microcosmic orbit and energy circulation. Pronouncing "r" strongly circulates (turns) the qi that rose up the spine. It speeds up stalled blood flow and cerebrospinal fluid, cleanly washes the brain's frontal lobe, stiffened by stress (cortisol), and sends the pulse of powerful life force round the whole body.

From the viewpoint of analytical psychology, reciting this mantra is an active meditation that summons the archetypes of the "Warrior" and the "Protector" from the depths of the unconscious.

"Ari Nari Nonari Anaro Nari Gunari"

*(Translator's note: the source names 다문천왕 (Vaishravana, "Hears Much"); the Lotus Sutra dharani is usually given as "a-ni na-ni nu-na-ni a-na-ro na-ri ku-na-ri.")*
