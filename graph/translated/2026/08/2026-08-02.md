The healing of the White-Robed Woman (백의녀 白衣女): in records such as the *Samguk Yusa*, with depictions of Silla-era monks and ordinary people, there are countless stories of someone gravely ill and at death's door to whom a "woman in white (White-Robed Avalokiteshvara)" appeared in a dream, stroked their forehead or the afflicted area with a cool, soft hand or poured clear water over them, and on waking the illness was healed as if washed away.

What's characteristic is that, the moment the touch lands, one feels extreme coolness and peace, accompanied by the bodily feedback of stiffened energy channels opening.

## The key phrase of the White-Robed dharani: ga-rya-ba-da
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2083808691686367569) (quotes [the Aug 1 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2083425207805784180))

The key phrase of the White-Robed Avalokiteshvara dharani

"Garyabada (g-r-b-d)"

Ga (g): opens the brain, centered on Okchim (the occiput)
Rya (r): sends the qi down the spine and turns it
Ba (b): draws out the kidneys' toxins, then
Da (d): stimulates the chest, connecting

At the end it strongly resonates the chest (heart and lungs), balancing the left and right organs, stimulating the vagus nerve, and binding the scattered qi back together at the body's center (the base of the chest) to connect it.
