## Pronouncing the mantra that releases zodiac karma
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089277759172542483) (quotes [the Aug 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089241119691288773))

The mantra for dissolving karmic consciousness from the influence of the stars is

Om Namo Bhagava Tebaso Devaya,

12 syllables.

When saying "Bhagava," pronounce the last "ba" close to "pa" to raise light before your eyes. The pronunciation stimulates the upper back of the head.

When saying "Tebaso," pronounce "Te" clearly while stimulating the medulla and thalamus.

Om Namo
Bhagapa (top back of the head; light appears in the eyes)

Te (medulla and thalamus / center of the head)
Baso
Devaya

## The twelve zodiac locks on the spine
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089241119691288773) (quotes [the Aug 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089143824945979681))

All the residue of karma we've made through countless past lives is firmly stored, according to the arrangement of the constellations in the sky, in the form of "energy knots (granthi)" in these 12 centers in and around the spine.

Ordinary people, whenever the gravitational and radiant energy of the planets emitted by the constellations (the twelve zodiac signs) touches the spine's 12 centers, unconsciously have their emotions churn, past karmic retribution manifest, and are dragged around by fate. That is, the twelve zodiac chakras become 12 locks that keep me in the bonds of samsara.

So when the sun or constellations move along the twelve zodiac signs, the karma stored in that body-constellation is stimulated and the corresponding matter occurs.

For example, the universe's two great energy sources at present, the Sun and Jupiter, are both gathered in Gemini. Gemini matches the 5th, cervical center (ruled by Mercury).

It's a period when the energy of information gathering, intellectual curiosity, and communication is strongly stimulated. As energy crowds into the neck center, the gateway to the brain, thoughts may multiply during meditation, or various spiritual insights and ideas may pour out.

On the other hand, the karma of thought chasing thought is easily triggered.

Sensitive people are probably feeling stimulation around the 7th cervical vertebra these days.

At this time, you purify that area by applying the liberation mantra "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" to the Dazhui point (대추혈).

## The chakras, the five elements, and returning the five qi to the origin
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089193320472027477) (quotes [the Aug 17 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089159579217301950))

The chakras each govern the five elements that make up the material world (earth, water, fire, wind, space), and they bind our consciousness to the body and the material world. (In the East these are called the five phases, 오행 五行.)

So if you're negatively bound in your saju, you fall into that phase. That is, you fall into karmic obstruction.

In Seondo (선도, Korean Daoist practice), one does "the five qi returning to the origin" (오기조원 五氣朝元),

and this corresponds to untying the chakras' granthi (energy knots). Purifying karma isn't something I do.

I only create the environment, and purification of karma begins with the flame of shakti.

## The granthi of the lower chakras
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089159579217301950) (quotes [a post by another account](https://x.com/i/status/2089144528951476314))

In the lower chakras, the rough karma of past and present lives and worldly desires are firmly clumped in the form of granthi (energy knots).

Of the twelve earthly branches, Chuk (축 丑) corresponds to the tailbone and the 1st chakra.

It's filled with the basic needs of reproduction and survival, and cold qi.

In Kriya Yoga, the energy of these chakras is broken through with the hot qi of shakti and special breathing.

## Purifying the samskaras through the Kutastha
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089143824945979681) (quotes [the Aug 16 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2088979187587834042))

In the seven chakras in our brain and spine, the habits of thought, emotion, and action from countless past and present lives, that is, "seeds of karma (samskara)," lie sleeping layer upon layer in the form of subtle energy.

So when you purify the brain and spinal nerve chakras with spiritual fire, karmic consciousness rises up and is purified.

At that point the bindu opens and white light billows before the eyes; this is called "Hamsa" (or Hong-Sau). It also means the "swan," symbolizing spiritual discernment.

When the bindu opens and consciousness concentrates inward, the practitioner sees the spiritual eye (Spiritual Eye, Kutastha) through the brow.

Through this Kutastha the chakras are opened. That is, the chakras don't exist as entities in our spine but as areas of bodily response to the Kutastha.

So rather than using the body to activate the spinal chakras, you activate the chakras using the Kutastha, the third eye.

That is, in a way where "seeing" red
activates the muladhara chakra.
