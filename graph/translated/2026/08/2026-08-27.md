## Earth qi gone turbid: the Five Yellow Killing and *burim*
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2092767639055851596) (quotes [the Aug 26 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2092531701067190731))

People with a lot of earth qi,
whether their saju has a lot of earth,
or they feel something is stagnant and can't flow..

that is called the Five Yellow Killing (오황살 五黃殺).

Originally earth qi is the qi of the center, so it is clear qi,
but when the qi isn't purified and is piled up in karmic force,
it becomes turbid qi. That is called the Five Yellow Killing.

*Burim*
