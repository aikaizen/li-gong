## Lovers' love-hate as energy: the partner as dakini
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093211372565979276) (quotes [the Aug 28 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2093167419342954791))

Tantra doesn't see love-hate between lovers as good and evil.
It sees it as energy.

The partner who tests the tantric practitioner's ego
and shakes them to their emotional rock bottom
is called a dakini (Dakini, goddess of wisdom).

When you fight fiercely with your lover, or conversely feel a maddening attraction, the sympathetic nervous system (right channel, Pingala) surges as if in a seizure.

## Using poison as medicine (이독제독 以毒制毒)

Using poison as medicine (이독제독, 以毒制毒)

Tantra does not deny desire.

From the exoteric (현교) view, greed, anger, ignorance, pride, and doubt (탐진치만의 貪瞋癡慢疑) are defined as the five poisons (오독 五毒) to be discarded, but tantra doesn't suppress this fierce energy; instead it transforms (전변 轉變) it into the five wisdoms (오지 五智).
