## How to make a wish while drinking coffee or tea

How to make a wish while drinking coffee or tea:

There's a saying, "the highest good is like water" (상선약수 上善若水). It means the best thing is like water, because water is everywhere, and when the original spirit of water wakes, it gives life force and grants our wishes.

1) While boiling the tea water, feel its qi.

Feel the qi of the water between your thumb and index finger.

When joy fills your chest, the original spirit has woken and begun to interact with you.

2) While listening to the sound of the water boiling, catch the moment when the water's cold qi has left and its yang qi feels sufficient.

3) Pour the water into the cup.

At this point the water is finely split, so it brews the coffee or tea better.

4) Breathe in the aroma through your nose.
→ activates the sympathetic nervous system

5) Drink through your mouth.
→ activates the parasympathetic nervous system

6) Make the sound "*bam*" (밤) and feel the stillness that stays on your lips.

Then speak what you wish for.

## Seeing cause and effect through *chokgi*
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2091258576543351097) (quotes [the Aug 11 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2086994611344712191))

Knowing how to see cause and effect means using conditions based on *chokgi* (촉기).

Take saju as an example: there's a lot of talk about the unfavorable element, the joyful element, the useful element (기신, 희신, 용신), and so on, but that's not what matters.

Good and bad fortune aren't fixed. The question is how you use them.

To use them you have to see cause and effect. You have to activate *chokgi* about why these things are happening.

Things that happen in the phenomenal world might be a tree branch I broke without thinking.

It's not that you shouldn't break branches, but that what's called cause and effect isn't the kind of causality positivism or materialism talks about.

It's like the butterfly effect. To catch that cause and effect with *chokgi*, you need to be able to see the situation from the center.

The center is the spot 1 cm above the solar plexus and 5 cm inward. It's a comfortable place. Put down your thoughts, ask about the problem from that place, and see how the center feels.

If comfort is felt and maintained, it's good cause and effect; if discomfort is felt, it's not good cause and effect.

If you don't stop at simply good or bad, but reflect again with "What is good about it?" or "What is this?", exact guidance and scenes about that cause and effect will arise from the center.

You mustn't stop at knowing how to see cause and effect; you must grasp why it's good or why it's bad and have "the capacity to use it well."
