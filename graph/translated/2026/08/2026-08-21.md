## Raising qi on the in-breath with the reverse-drawing method
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2090703749534884308) (quotes [the Aug 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2089528568829317537))

In Kriya Yoga,
qi is raised on the in-breath.

That's done through the reverse-drawing method (역취법). That is, the air coming in from outside goes down while the qi goes up.

Raise it a little on the in-breath, let it go down on the out-breath,
on the next in-breath raise it higher than before, let it go down on the out-breath..
and raise it to the medulla. Safely.

It's a very important breathing method. ^^

Done this way, at some point a moment comes when lung breathing disappears.

## Three-step talisman reading: change, vigilance, precision
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2090620351772598690)

🌊 Step 1: Suryuk-haengjang talisman (수륙행장부): environmental change and wise negotiation

This tablet represents changing environments and moving to new places, such as changing jobs or moving house.

If you're planning a job change or a move to a new department, boldly ride the flow, which governs all moves, voluntary or not.

Be careful that it also signifies all sorts of dangers that arise in the process of changing environments and conditions, but this tablet governs the strength and wisdom to get through all sorts of troubles that arise while carrying out matters without suffering major harm.

Receive the energy that signifies all events related to job changes, moves, and even salary negotiations, and confidently put your value on the negotiating table.

🦅 Step 2: Myeoljo-yoseo-goesa-eol talisman: thoroughly smashing spaced-out laziness

In personal relationships, this tablet signifies annoying, lazy situations and all problems arising from laziness.

When a downtrend signal appears while trading or a job opportunity comes, beware of every situation where, whether an event has happened or hasn't yet but is fully foreseeable, you fail to come to your senses and waste time vacantly.

Bear in mind painfully that if you don't sharpen your senses with the reverse-drawing breath, various harms will occur, or by doing nothing even in fully foreseeable situations the harm will grow even larger.

💎 Step 3: Yu-day talisman (유일부): sharp calculation and precise control

You must manage your assets with the cold, sharp energy that corresponds to yin metal (음금 陰金).

Apply its characteristics, delicate and fastidious, good at seeing details and focusing on fine points, to the maximum for your trade entry points and trailing-stop settings.

Using the powerful weapon of excellent planning and calculation ability, you can flawlessly design a new digital archiving or festival-ecosystem proposal.

Use as your engine the competitive drive of hating to lose to others and having strong pride, apply your tendency toward a strong need for control to a thorough risk-management system, and build your own solid fortress of wealth!

Attraction, image training, and the Kutastha

Many people do image training for attraction. Image training seems to be a way of bypassing the conscious mind to build rapport with the subconscious and teach it habits and goals.

So if some wish is realized, that's what's called attraction at the level of the individual ego.

And things that have come to pass are "shown" and "experienced."

I'm simply grateful.

That's why it's grace and action-through-non-action.
