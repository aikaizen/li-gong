## Pouring water along the wall of the cup
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2119249204170379751) (quotes [the Nov 8 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2119244247182557468))

To protect the tender leaves of an old tea in which forty years are condensed from physical shock, and to keep its fragrance and thick texture (the body of the liquor, 미탕감) from scattering into the air, it is best to pour the water along the wall of the cup or gaiwan.

The '87 White Needle Golden Lotus is made of needle-thin, tender buds (palace grade, 궁정료), and over forty years its tissue has become extremely soft. If you strike the boiling water hard and straight down into the middle of the leaves, the tender cell walls burst and fine powder (tea dust) floats up, clouding the clear ruby liquor and possibly mixing in a muddy taste.

If you pour the water in a circle along the wall, it swirls gently like a vortex inside the pot. Then the heavy polysaccharides and pectin of the leaves already lying at the bottom (from the middle infusions on) and the bright aromatic compounds of the new leaves placed on top mix evenly without shock, building a three-dimensional layering.

## Bing-Xu day: '87 White Needle Golden Lotus, and the One Thing rising and falling

I'm drinking a Pu'er that suits today, a flower blooming in the earth: the '87 White Needle Golden Lotus (백침금련 白針金蓮).

Over nearly forty years the fermented smell typical of ripe tea has vanished completely, and, true to its name, a clear, deep lotus fragrance (연향 蓮香) rises, along with the deep medicinal aroma (약향 藥香) and ginseng aroma (삼향 蔘香) of an old herbal pharmacy.

In the '87 White Needle Golden Lotus, the rich amino acids drawn from young buds combine with water-soluble pectin, giving an ultimate softness that wraps the mouth thick and smooth (활 滑), like well-simmered pine-nut porridge or a rich bone broth.

With the fermented smell entirely gone after nearly forty years, the deep forest-floor scent after rain (진향 陳香) mingles with the profound medicinal aroma of ginseng or orchid root, settling even the mind into calm.

The '87 White Needle Golden Lotus has an "embracing energy": the moment you drink it, it warms you inside as if a soft, heavy stove were lit in the solar plexus and lower belly (dantian), and it slowly melts the back (the Huatuo Jiaji points, 화타협척혈 華佗夾脊穴).

It seems to release the stagnant acetylcholine of the abdomen and widen the nerve endings; each segment of the sternum comes alive, and it lets energy flow into the microtubules in the fascia of the breastbone.

On the out-breath, the One Thing (한물건) arises in the Yellow Court between the lower dantian below the navel and Mingmen; on the in-breath that One Thing rises to Zhongji (중극 中極) in front of T6, in front of Lingtai, and on the out-breath it goes back down to the Yellow Court.

Today's day branch, Xu earth (戌; palace 6 Qian, the Gate of Heaven), faces Chen earth (辰; palace 4 Xun, the Door of Earth) head-on and creates the Chen-Xu clash.
