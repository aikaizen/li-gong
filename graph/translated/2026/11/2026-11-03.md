## Kidney fire: the fire within water
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117581896452571384) (quotes [the Nov 3 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117502269226967363))

In Eastern internal alchemy, the Former Heaven fire that blooms deep inside the kidneys, the organ that governs water, is called "kidney fire" (신화 腎火) or "the fire within water" (수중지화 水中之火).

At first glance this idea of "fire inside water" sounds like a contradiction. But looked at through the lens of modern renal physiology and neuroendocrinology, it is an insight that precisely captures the combination of three things: the mitochondrial metabolism of the kidneys, which produce more electricity and heat per unit of weight than anything else in the body; the bioelectricity of the nerve plexus behind the peritoneum; and the endocrine hormone axis that governs the bone marrow and the vitality of the whole body.

The kidneys are not a passive strainer that merely filters urine. After the heart (and higher even than the brain), they are the body's "ultra-high-output power plant," with overwhelmingly high mitochondrial density and oxygen consumption per unit of weight.

The two kidneys filter about 180 liters of plasma (water) a day and then reabsorb 99% of that water and electrolytes (sodium, potassium, calcium and so on) back into the blood. In this process, the ultra-dense mitochondria inside the tubule cells synthesize and burn enormous amounts of ATP. By the laws of thermodynamics, a considerable amount of energy is released as deep metabolic heat (thermal energy) as ATP is made and broken down.

Under chronic stress or sympathetic tension, the renal blood vessels constrict, and the inside of the kidneys (especially the medulla) is left in a chronic low-oxygen state.

But when, in stillness, you gently regulate the pressure of the abdominal cavity with slack in the lower belly and ultra-fine breathing, renal blood flow opens and the dense mitochondria of the tubules are all supplied with oxygen and switch on at once. Then, in the middle of kidney tissue through which 180 liters of fluid (water) circulate, explosive ATP bioelectricity and deep metabolic heat (fire) bloom up. This is the felt reality of the "fire within water" (kidney fire) that a practitioner senses deep inside the lower back.

## Why a cup of Pu'er before and after dantian breathing helps
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117568545739444364) (quotes [the Nov 3 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117502269226967363))

The cold in the abdomen that comes from over-activation of the parasympathetic nerves arises when acetylcholine stagnates at the nerve endings.

Well-aged old tea (노차 老茶) or genuine Pu'er has an outstanding synergy, both neurobiochemically and in terms of practice, for releasing the yin-fire (cold stagnation) caused by narrowing of the vagal nerve endings in the lower dantian and excess build-up of acetylcholine.

The key macromolecules produced as Pu'er post-ferments over the years, theabrownins, gallic acid and oxidized catechins, stimulate the gut microbiota and promote nitric oxide (NO) secretion from the endothelial cells of the intestinal lining.

Nitric oxide immediately relaxes stiffened intestinal smooth muscle and micro-vessels, widening the space of the vagal nerve-ending mesh that had physically narrowed.

At the same time, as warm blood circulates, the acidification of the tissue (lowered pH) is relieved, creating the best conditions for the enzyme that breaks down acetylcholine (AChE), which had been stuck in substrate inhibition, to break down and reset acetylcholine again.

In addition, L-theanine, an amino acid abundant in Pu'er, crosses the blood-brain barrier (BBB), raises levels of the inhibitory neurotransmitter GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid) in the brain, and induces alpha and theta waves.

This gently calms the runaway upper medulla (the dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus), which had been pouring out acetylcholine, over-excited by stress or forced breathing.

## The danger of practice built only on relaxation and accumulating qi
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117502269226967363)

In ordinary dantian breathing (단전호흡 丹田呼吸) the lower dantian is treated as a vague pouch of energy. But if you forcibly push energy into the lower belly to accumulate qi (축기 蓄氣), the parasympathetic nervous system becomes abnormally over-activated.

In that state, if the mind has not been trained, and in daily life emotions such as anger, stress or refusal flare up, or past trauma (karmic imprints, 업식 業識) lodged in the large intestine is touched, then the over-concentrated cholinergic parasympathetic energy cannot produce an electric spark. Instead it abruptly undergoes a phase transition into a "pushing force" (yin qi, 음기 陰氣).

As a result, all the Later Heaven qi you had gathered in the lower belly suddenly turns into a huge mass of cold, freezing the organs and sinking down to settle in the kidneys, the bladder and the Yongquan point (용천혈 湧泉穴). This is the yin-fire phenomenon (음화 陰火), the "fire entering, demons possessing" (주화입마 走火入魔) that breathing practitioners so often suffer.
