## The character 炁: fire inside nothingness
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118202700626288779)

The character 炁 (qi; 既 → 無 + 灬) is not the ordinary 氣 (qi) of breathing. It is a character used specifically in Daoism to mean the Former Heaven original qi (선천의 원기; 先天眞一之炁, the true one qi of Former Heaven) that awakens in the state of no-thought (무념 無念), when thinking has been cut off.

The very shape of the character, "fire" (灬) placed beneath "nothing" (無), perfectly depicts the state of holding the flame of Ding fire (정화 丁火) inside that empty nothingness.

While we write the Later Heaven energy we get by eating rice (米) and breathing air (气) through the nose as 氣, we distinguish the Former Heaven energy that ignites by itself in absolute stillness, where thought has been completely cut off, by writing it 炁.

The site of Zhonghuang (중황 中黃; the thalamus and third ventricle), midway between Mixin (미심 眉心, between the eyebrows) and Yuzhen (옥침 玉枕, the back of the head), is the "absolutely empty space" (the place of no-thought, 無念處) where even the sensation of breathing is cut off completely and all deliberation and discrimination (thought) vanish.

If thought and intention remain (having-thought, 유념 有念), only the coarse Later Heaven fire-qi of the sympathetic nerves blazes (ordinary fire, 범화 凡火, not samadhi fire), and the Former Heaven energy never awakens.

In other words, the upper "無" signifies the zero-point state in which the thalamus and hypothalamus, the brain's center for integrating the senses, are completely emptied.

If Bing fire (병화 丙火) is the sun-fire blazing fiercely outward (sympathetic overdrive, anger, lust), Ding fire is the candle, the moonlight, the seed-fire in the brazier: cool and wrapped in quiet darkness (yin, 無) on the outside, yet shining unextinguished at its core.

In Guseon's (구선스님) system, after passing through the kidney fire of the Yellow Court (the fire within water) and being absorbed into the bones (the marrow), the "One Thing" (한 물건) that settles densely in the empty place of no-thought of Zhonghuang in the head, and the "clear, transparent form of quiescent extinction" (적멸상 寂滅相; the vitality of being alive) that hangs just beneath it (the hypothalamus), are exactly this "Ding fire inside nothingness."

## Gui-Wei day: closing the vault of the Jia-Xu decade
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118161084561694999) (quotes [the Nov 4 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117866396935950353))

November 5, a Gui-Wei day (계미일 癸未日), is the tenth and final day that brings the grand finale of the second decade, the Jia-Xu decade (甲戌旬), which has run for the past ten days.

Gui-Wei is the last, tenth runner of the ten stem-branches of the Jia-Xu decade (Jia-Xu to Gui-Wei). It is the day when all the work and energy laid out during the Jia-Xu decade are finally settled, put into the storehouse by the blessing of the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden, and the door is locked.

And today, the last day of the Jia-Xu decade, the vacuum suction of its void, Shen and You (신·유 申·酉), reaches its peak; then tomorrow morning, as the Jia-Shen day (갑신일 甲申日) dawns, Shen, which had been void, appears directly as the commander (filled, leaving the void).

In other words, it is the decisive inflection point at which the potential of "Shen-You metal qi" (금기 金氣), compressed in a vacuum (void) for the past ten days, is unsealed in an instant and erupts into reality (leaving the void, 출공 出空).

So powerful metal qi is set off, and events arise in which Shen and You are stirred, such as the Yin-Shen clash (인신충 寅申沖) or the Mao-You clash (묘유충 卯酉沖).

In the five phases, metal means fruition and harvest, and cold reason. In market liquidity and money flows too, it becomes important to withdraw vague expectations and to settle accounts by clear profit-and-loss criteria, or to check your cash weighting and margin of safety.

This afternoon and evening (compressing and refining the void of You metal):

Lake-Fire, Revolution (택화혁 澤火革 ䷰; right hand with only the index finger lifted: Ding-You palace 7 Dui → Jia-Wu palace 9 Li)
Place awareness in the vacuum chamber of palace 7 Dui on the left side of the abdomen (the Ding-You Jade Maiden), gather clear sweet saliva (metal qi) in the mouth, and compress the complicated matters in your head into cool, clear conclusions.

Tonight to tomorrow morning (Shen metal leaving the void, and welcoming the life-palace Ding-Hai): Heaven-Fire, Fellowship (천화동인 天火同人 ䷌; right hand with all fingers touching: Ding-Hai palace 6 Qian → Jia-Shen palace 2 Kun)

First open wide palace 6 Qian in the lower left abdomen (the life-palace, the Ding-Hai Jade Maiden), the protagonist of tomorrow (the Jia-Shen day) and the day after (the Ji-Hai month, Start of Winter); then gently embrace the sharp command of palace 2 Kun in the upper right abdomen (the Jia-Shen General, Hu Wenzhang), which leaps out of the void, and stand it up as the central axis of your body.
