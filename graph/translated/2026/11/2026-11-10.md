## The backlash of a carelessly awakened kundalini
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2120025950444572813) (quotes [the Nov 10 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2119964953700659661))

The Yellow Court (황정 黃庭) and Mingmen (명문 命門), in front of and behind the lumbar spine, are the powder magazine of the Former Heaven original qi, condensed since the days of the fertilized egg.

If you stimulate only the lower dantian and forcibly detonate this spark while the upper channels and the center are not yet established, serious side effects follow.

The unconscious runs wild and you are tormented by visions and voices; the energy suddenly over-expands the nerves and microtubules, and you suffer terrible pain, as if snakes or worms were wriggling and crawling under the skin. In severe cases the heat is swallowed by the cold of the lower body and turns into an icy yin-fire (음화 陰火; wind of the joints, 역절풍 歷節風) that strikes the organs.

Why Laogong (노궁 勞宮) and Jueyin (궐음 厥陰)?

The key to handling this wild energy without side effects is Laogong in the middle of the palm and its root, the Hand Jueyin Pericardium meridian (수궐음심포경 手厥陰心包經). If the sovereign fire (군화 君火) of the heart is the body guarding the throne, the minister fire (상화 相火) of the pericardium meridian is the soft lamp that receives the order and attunes things on site.

"Jueyin" in particular is the code of transition, yin at its extreme giving birth to yang (음극생양 陰極生陽), sprouting new yang qi at the far end of deep yin.

The ignition method that soaks from top to bottom

Instead of lighting the fire from below, you must open Laogong in the palm and let the soft warmth (Later Heaven qi) you gather fall drop by drop from the top of the spine down to Mingmen.

When the precise spark of Jueyin first melts the cold of the spinal cord, widens the channel and fills the Yellow Court, the Former Heaven original qi ripens not into an explosive that tears the nerves but into a gentle ball of fire that brings life back.
