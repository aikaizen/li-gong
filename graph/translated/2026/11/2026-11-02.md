## Geng-Chen day: two great gears behind the clash
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117077032685420671) (quotes [the Oct 31 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2116194241827467497))

26.11.02. Today is a Geng-Chen day (경진일 庚辰日).

When people hear "Geng-Chen day," they usually think only of the Chen-Xu clash (진술충 辰戌沖), but the flow of energy hidden behind Geng-Chen is far more three-dimensional.

Dissect Geng-Chen through the eyes of Qimen and the Six Jia (육갑 六甲) and two enormous gears appear. One is "Jia-Xu-Ji · Ding-Chou," the home base of the Jia-Xu decade (갑술순 甲戌旬) to which Geng-Chen belongs. The other is "Jia-Shen-Geng · Ding-Hai," which the heavenly stem Geng (경 庚) carries within it.

If only the earthly branches at the bottom collide, with no harmonizing of consciousness, today becomes a day of overlapping cracks. With Xu (술 戌), the root of the decade it belongs to, the Chen-Xu clash engages; the Jade Maiden on duty, Ding-Chou (정축 丁丑), stirs the Chou-Wei clash and the Chou-Xu-Wei punishment; and between the body of the heavenly stem Geng metal (Jia-Shen) and its partner (Ding-Hai), the friction of the Shen-Hai mutual harm (상해 相害) arises.

But if, on top of this tension of 90 degrees (punishment and harm) and 180 degrees (clash), you pair and raise the consciousness of the Six Ding Jade Maidens (육정옥녀 六丁玉女; yin, function) and the Six Jia Generals (육갑신장 六甲神將; yang, substance), the wasteful collision is reversed into a high-voltage spark that opens a locked vault.
