## '97 Yiwu wild-arbor green cake: the year of the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2119012499810849050)

I'm drinking the '97 Yiwu Lü Da Shu green cake, made in 1997, a Ding-Chou year (정축년 丁丑年). By the logic of the stem-branches and Qimen, '97 is the year of the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden (정축옥녀 丁丑玉女). Like a soft glow (Ding) warming the dark winter earth (Chou), it was a truly exquisite space-time for the birth of a gentle, graceful Yiwu (이무 易武) tea, "the queen of Pu'er."

The charm of this tea is "bitter, then sweet," that is, the returning sweetness (회감 回甘). As soon as the firm bitterness typical of wild big-tree tea brushes the mouth, the sticky sweetness of pectin and polysaccharides fermented over thirty years surges up from the root of the tongue.

It is the state of sweetness within bitterness (고중유감 苦中有甘), where sweetness coexists inside the bitter.

When drinking this precious tea, take a sip from a small cup, roll it around in the mouth until it mixes well with saliva (jade liquid, 옥액 玉液), and swallow slowly. Only then does the water not merely pass through as urine but become true fluids (진액 津液) that moisten the five organs and six bowels. The sweetness the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden waited thirty years to give fills the center of the body quietly.

## The Ding-You Jade Maiden and the breath of the chest
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118925298481947128) (quotes [the Nov 6 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118561408929702048))

Today is the first day of the Ji-Hai month (Start of Winter, 입동 立冬) and a day related to the Ding-You Jade Maiden (정유옥녀 丁酉玉女).

The Ding-You Jade Maiden has the image of "true metal refined by fire" (화련진금 火煉眞金; the purest gold and gems refined out by fire), where the starlight of heaven (Ding fire) meets the pure gem of earth (You metal).

The Ding-You Jade Maiden's three sacred titles, "Tongxuan Daode Zhenjun" (통현도덕진군 通玄道德眞君), "Zang Wengong (臧文公), Shumi (叔靡) / Renjing (仁敬)" and "Weishen (威神), Congkui (從魁)," all contain the secret of internal alchemy (내단 內丹): "through deep breathing of the chest (Danzhong and the lungs), draw in the profound mysterious qi (현기 玄氣) of the universe and preserve the vitality and the immortal elixir (단 丹) within you" (Ding-You preserving life, 정유보생 丁酉保生).

Danzhong (단중 膻中) and the lungs, which the Ding-You Jade Maiden influences, receive the clear breath of heaven (heavenly qi, 천기 天氣), govern the qi of the whole body (the lungs govern qi, 폐주기 肺主氣), and are the organs that hold the po soul (백 魄), the body's actual life-support system (autonomic nerves, immunity, bodily sensation).
