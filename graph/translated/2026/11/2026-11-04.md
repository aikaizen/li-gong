## Trump's chart on the Ren-Wu day: the paradox of wood feeding fire

Looking at the November 4 Ren-Wu day through flat Four Pillars astrology (명리학 命理學), it is easy to misjudge: "Ren water (wealth / water) has come into a hot, dry chart in the heavenly stem, so won't it cool the heat?"

1. The axis of time

The structure of the natal chart: Trump's chart has the year pillar Bing-Xu (병술 丙戌, the fire storehouse) and the day pillar Ji-Wei (기미 己未, hot dry earth) as its pillars, so the chart itself is already a blazing furnace.

The intervention of the Ren-Wu day (Jia-Xu decade): Today's Ren-Wu belongs to the Jia-Xu decade (甲戌旬). That is, the background commander of today's space-time is Jia-Xu-Ji (갑술기 甲戌己), and Trump's year branch is also Xu (戌) while his day stem and month stem are Ji (己).

What happens: The moment the day's earthly branch Wu fire (午) comes in, it combines with the Xu of Trump's year branch and the Xu of today's decade to create the Wu-Xu half fire combination, and at the same time forms the Wu-Wei combination into fire with his day branch Wei (未).

The side effect for a hot, dry chart: In a hot, dry chart that badly needs water qi and metal qi, the lower belly (palace 6 Qian, Xu earth), the solar plexus (palace 9 Li, Wu fire) and the dantian (palace 2 Kun, Wei earth) are all linked only by flames. If the cushioning mechanism of the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden (palace 8 Gen; damp earth opening the storehouse), "yin first, yang after," does not work to cool this, the enormous fire comes bursting out not as drive but as extreme self-righteousness, explosions of anger, scandal and clouded judgment (화다토초 火多土焦: the fire is so strong the earth is scorched).

2. The axis of space

The axis of space (Jia-Chen-Ren, the body of the stem Ren water): wood generating fire (목생화 木生火), fanned by the Ding-Ren combination into wood.

The optical illusion on the surface: through flat astrology it is easy to misjudge: "Ren water has come into a hot, dry chart in the heavenly stem, so won't it cool the heat?"

The deep decoding of Qimen and the Six Jia:
Today's Ren water has its root sitting on Wu fire, so it is water about to evaporate like steam (water in its place of extinction, 절지 絶地).
What is more, the home base of the stem Ren is Jia-Chen-Ren (palace 4 Xun), and when this Ren water meets its 90-degree partner, the Jade Maiden (Ding fire: the hidden Ding fire inside the Wei earth of Trump's day branch and the Xu earth of his year branch), instead of sprinkling water it degenerates through the Ding-Ren combination into wood and turns into wood (fuel)!

What happens: Even the single stream of water that came in to cool the heat combines with Ding fire and becomes firewood, and that firewood is thrown back into the day's Wu-Xu fire configuration, feeding the flames: the "paradox of wood generating fire." It is a structure in which, while trying to grab wealth or public support (Ren water, the wealth star), one easily ruins the game through one's own impatience and overheated words and deeds (excess Wu fire).

In a space-time like today's Ren-Wu day, when the heat of fire and earth soars to the extreme, the Ding-Chou (丁丑) and Ding-You (丁酉) Jade Maidens act as the "coolant and the mold (casting form)" that keeps the overheated furnace from exploding and condenses its heat into tangible results (gold, assets).

The Ding-You Jade Maiden (You) and the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden (Chou) are a team that pull strongly toward each other in the You-Chou half metal combination.

Especially when fire covers everything, as on a Ren-Wu day, only when a "double cooling and refining line" runs, Ding-Chou cooling the heat from below as damp earth and supplying the raw material of metal, and Ding-You taking it and extracting high-purity gold and water qi, can one fully take hold of that enormous fire as one's own power and wealth.
