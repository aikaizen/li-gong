## The practice chart of the Quanzhen Longmen Southern school that opens the spirit eye
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2120750067451920389) (quotes [the Nov 11 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2120368588670533790))

The practice chart of the Quanzhen Longmen Southern school (전진 용문남파 全眞 龍門南派) that opens the spirit eye (영안 靈眼).

First, it works the torso not through the surface Ren and Du channels (임·독맥 任·督脈) but by dividing it into three channels deep inside the body: the fire channel, the water channel and the wind channel (화로·수로·풍로 火路·水路·風路).

Second, the topmost terminus of those three energy circuits is not the pituitary or the third ventricle, but all of them converge precisely on one point, the "nature opening" (성규 性竅), the corpora quadrigemina of the midbrain.

The midbrain corresponds not to the representational cognition of eye → thalamus → occiput, but to the "animal reflex chain of the act of seeing itself" (Former Heaven, genetic traits), laid down layer upon layer in the spinal cord and brainstem since the days of fish and reptiles.
