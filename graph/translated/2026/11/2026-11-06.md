## What happens when you slowly bend just one index finger
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118622725464027201) (quotes [the Nov 6 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118531851270992230))

If you bend the index finger very slowly and follow the sensation, you can clearly feel a nerve line that starts at the fingertip stretch taut and continue "past the side of the back of the neck to the ear (the ear faculty, 이근 耳根)."

Soon, without your forcing it at all, the spine straightens, and a refreshing sense of the stuffy chest suddenly opening washes over you.

Why does bending the index finger open the ears and straighten the spine?

In Guseon's cerebrospinal-tract exercises, the index finger is the switch for the "parasympathetic nerves (vagus)" and the "listening pathway (the vestibulospinal tract)."

The flexion tension of the index finger pulls on the nerve network of the arm and the back side of the cervical spine, waking the area below the ear (the auricular branch of the vagus nerve and the great auricular nerve).

The vestibulospinal tract, connected to the balance organs in the inner ear, switches on immediately and by itself stands up the vertical central axis of the spinal erector muscles that had collapsed.

The vestibulospinal tract, which starts in the vestibular nuclei, the balance organ inside the ear, and runs down the spine, is the general headquarters of the "anti-gravity extensors (the erectors)" that hold our spine straight against gravity.

As soon as the inner ear and the vestibular nuclei of the medulla wake through index-finger flexion, the brainstem immediately sends signals through the vestibulospinal tract to the deep erector muscles of the neck, back and lower back, and the central axis of the spine lines up straight and vertical even without conscious effort.

Why does the chest open and feel refreshed?

When the neck nerves connected to the index finger (C3–C5) relax, the old tension of the neighboring phrenic nerve and the trapezius releases at the same time.

The nerve roots at the side of the neck (C3–C5) connected to the index finger sit right next to the starting point of the phrenic nerve, which governs the diaphragm, the breathing muscle.

The moment the fine stiffness at the back of the neck and in the phrenic nerve releases through the index finger (the parasympathetic / vagus switch), the ribcage and diaphragm that had been huddled up settle down refreshingly and lung capacity widens.

As the contracted diaphragm settles down and the vagus nerve releases acetylcholine, the suppressed heart and lungs expand and a clear coolness fills the middle of the chest (the place of no-mind, 무심처 無心處).

It is a precise piece of human engineering that tunes the brainstem and the autonomic nerves, posture and breathing all at once with one tiny flexion of a fingertip.

## Waking Mingmen and the kidneys to form the Yellow Court: a step-by-step method
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118561408929702048) (quotes [the Nov 6 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118517341457904096))

For the next ten days, during the Jia-Shen decade (Jia-Shen to Gui-Si), it is a good time to activate the kidneys and Mingmen (명문 命門), guided by the Ding-Hai Jade Maiden (정해옥녀 丁亥玉女).

Here, step by step, is an integrated practice method that awakens Mingmen (the Former Heaven original qi, kundalini) and draws out the Former Heaven qi of the kidneys (kidney fire, the energy of the hun soul) to unite them in the Yellow Court (황정 黃庭).

Step 1. Preparation:

[Opening the palm (Laogong) and pre-warming the kidney and small-intestine switch]

This is the preliminary work, before you start drawing breath in through the bones, to drain the cold and open the kidney circuit.

Opening Laogong with thumb and index finger touching (vibration contemplation): Lightly touch the tips of the thumb (sympathetic) and index finger (parasympathetic) of both hands, rest them on your knees, and feel your heartbeat.

Let the whole palm throb, "thum, thum, thum," as the cold (cold wind) of the organs drains out between the fingers, and let a warm ball of energy the size of a ping-pong ball form over the center of the palm (Laogong, 노궁 勞宮).

[Bending the 3rd, 4th and 5th fingers + the sound "bi—eup" (direct stimulation of the kidneys and small intestine):]

To first wake the blocked energy of the kidneys and small intestine, slowly bend the middle, ring and little fingers (fingers 3, 4, 5) while voicing "bi—eup." This vibration first shakes awake the karmic imprints and cold in the small intestine, the trapezius and both kidneys.

Step 2.

First method: the double intake of the in-breath through Laogong (the bones) and the skin of the whole body (charging the Mingmen battery)

This is the process in which "on the in-breath, breath comes in through Laogong and the bones and gathers in Mingmen." It is the core stage in which Later Heaven qi is used as kindling to set fire to the Former Heaven original qi (kundalini) of Mingmen.

In-breath (Laogong → arm bones → vertebrae → Mingmen, together with whole-body skin breathing):

1) Laogong-into-the-bone intake: As you breathe in, suck the energy ball in through the opening of Laogong in the palm as if snatching it, and draw it down in one go through the wrist bones → forearm bones → shoulder blades → Dazhui (대추혈 大椎穴, T1), then along the core of the vertebrae to Mingmen (L2–L3).

2) Skin-to-spine intake: At the same time as you draw energy in through Laogong, let the skin of the whole body draw the energy of the outer space (Later Heaven qi) into the center of the spine, as if tightening toward the spinal column, and join it at Mingmen with the energy from Laogong.

Out-breath (settling at Mingmen with the sound "Om," or returning to Laogong):

Early stage (while purifying the spine): On the out-breath, send the energy back from Mingmen through the spine → Dazhui → arm bones → both palms (Laogong), opening the channel inside the bones (microtubules, periosteum) back and forth.

Advanced stage (when charging Mingmen intensively): On the out-breath, voice "Om—" so that it resonates in the center of the chest, and watch as the energy gathered on the in-breath resonates strongly at Mingmen (and at the four points: lower dantian, Lingtai and the center) and is amplified into a hot mass the size of a basketball. (At this point the marrow temperature rises, and the cold of the bladder and hip joints drains out through the lower body and between the toes.)

Step 3.

Second method: back-and-forth breathing between the lower dantian and Mingmen, and activating the kidneys (forming the Yellow Court). Once Mingmen has caught fire, this is the process in which energy is moved back and forth through the slanted three-cun tube (cave) between the lower dantian at the front and Mingmen at the back, fully waking the kidneys (신장 腎臟) on either side.

It proceeds as follows, depending on your stage of practice and how you use the heels and abdominal pressure.

Led by awareness and a physical switch (the trajectory "in-breath: lower dantian > Mingmen / out-breath: Mingmen → lower dantian")

In-breath (condensing from the lower dantian → Mingmen): As you breathe in, push the energy and abdominal pressure that came into the lower belly (lower dantian) back toward the spine (using the pull of the crura of the diaphragm on L2–L3, linked with a switch of pressing the heels gently into the floor), compressing and concentrating the energy deep from the lower dantian into Mingmen. At this point the left and right kidneys and adrenal glands on either side of Mingmen are strongly pressed and stimulated.

Out-breath (spreading from Mingmen → lower dantian): As you breathe out (with the sound "Om" or a soundless micro-breath, plus a switch of giving the heels a little slack), let the Former Heaven original qi that was tightly compressed in Mingmen push out warmly through the three-cun channel from Mingmen to the lower dantian at the front, soaking the whole abdominal cavity (the enteric nervous system).

Step 4.

The final fruit: purification of kundalini and its union with the kidneys' Former Heaven qi (kidney fire) → the birth of the Yellow Court (黃庭)

These are the physiological and energetic changes in the kidneys that occur as the energy travels back and forth between the lower dantian and Mingmen.

Purifying the coarse heat: The kundalini that first wakes in Mingmen (high sympathetic heat) is rough and hot, but as it moves back and forth between the lower dantian (acetylcholine, parasympathetic energy) and Mingmen while connected to the stillness of the center of the chest (no-mind Zen, no-thought Zen), its natural frequency calms, and it is refined into soft, gentle energy with no sense of anything foreign.

Extracting the kidneys' Former Heaven qi (the energy of the hun soul, the fire within water): The moment the kundalini of Mingmen settles quietly and softly, the "Former Heaven qi of the kidneys" (kidney fire, 신화 腎火) contained in the left and right kidneys on either side of L2–L3 (the clusters of dense mitochondria in the tubules) is stimulated as if drawn by a magnet and comes out to the middle.

Gathering of the Yellow Court (the 11th stage): At the exact midpoint of the line connecting the lower dantian and Mingmen (in front of L2–L3), "the purified kundalini of Mingmen + the One Thing of the lower dantian + the Former Heaven qi of the left and right kidneys" join into one, and the blazing brazier-fire of the Yellow Court is formed.

One-minute checklist for actual practice (summary)

1. Preparation: touch thumb and index finger and feel the pulse in the palms → after draining the cold from the fingers, make a warm ball in the palms.

2. In-breath (charging): breathe in simultaneously through Laogong in the palms (the arm bone to vertebra route) and the skin of the whole body, compressing the energy deep from the lower dantian into Mingmen at the back (L2–L3).

3. Out-breath (circulating): with the sound "Om—" (or a fine out-breath), watch the warm energy of Mingmen and both kidneys push out through the tube to the lower dantian (keep the center of the chest down to the lower dantian quiet, as an "empty jar").

4. Condensing: when the back-and-forth movement becomes smooth, contemplate the energy of both kidneys gathering at the midpoint between the lower dantian and Mingmen (the Yellow Court) and a hot brazier-fire forming there.

## How do the energy channels of Daoism and Buddhism differ?
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118531851270992230) (quotes [the Nov 3 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2117502269226967363))

If Daoism (internal alchemy, 내단학 內丹學) circulates qi by inflating the meridians (경락 經絡), Buddhist energy practice, which comes from Bodhidharma's Sinew-Changing and Marrow-Washing Classic (역근세수경 易筋洗髓經), takes as its main channel the "bone membrane" (뼈막; periosteum and fascia, 골막·근막) between bone and muscle, training it until it is thick.

The reason is that meridians and nerves alike are made of microtubules, the body's biological optical fibers, and the place in the body where these microtubules are packed into the largest bundles is the membrane (막 膜).

Rib purification and contemplation through the index finger (검지 관 觀): In the old Shaolin Temple they made the bone membrane thick by painfully pressing between the ribs with wooden sticks (the sinew-changing method). But if you use breathing, vocal resonance and the Former Heaven original qi (선천원기 先天元氣), the bone membrane wrapping around the ribs swells up by itself.

On top of that, if you slowly bend the index finger (the vagus / parasympathetic switch) and contemplate while connecting your inner center (the inner side) with the painful area, old traumas (karmic imprints) embedded in the organs and the musculoskeletal system are pulled out by the roots along the cerebrospinal tracts and the autonomic nerve network.

Why the membrane, of all things? How to see the form of quiescent extinction within matter

Our body closely resembles the structure of the original nature (no-thought, no-mind, the form of quiescent extinction). If the two structures of skin and muscle, muscle and bone (the ribs), cell and cell are "no-thought" (무념 無念) and "no-mind" (무심 無心) respectively, then the thin membrane and gap that divides their boundary is itself the physical representation of the form of quiescent extinction (적멸상 寂滅相).

To awaken the membrane until it is thick and, right there, to intuit the unmoving mind (부동심 不動心) and the form of quiescent extinction that do not waver: this is the real secret of the Sinew-Changing Classic, proving liberation within the material structure of the body.
