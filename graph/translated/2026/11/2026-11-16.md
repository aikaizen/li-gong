## Feeling the pulse between thumb and index finger: the self that is born and dies
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122236981744996478) (quotes [the Nov 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2121536288356798663))

Another place to feel the heartbeat is the fingers.

Feel the heart's pulse in the space where thumb and index finger touch. If you stay mindful of the pulse like this, before you know it you become still.

Our brain cannot process much information at once
and tends to pay attention to wherever it has allocated a lot.

The fingers, especially the index finger, are exactly such a part.
So if you join the thumb and index finger and are mindful of that spot,
the brain becomes still while busy processing that information.

In the heart's thumping systole the pupils move,
and inner information rises to the brain and forms the self.
In the diastole of the heart and pulse the pupils stop,
and outer information is taken in rather than inner.

Between thumb and index finger
you come to feel the self being born and dying away,
that is, the self as process.

In addition, just by joining the two fingers,
the thymus in the chest is activated,
the spring of joy, the source of a child's delight,
which is said to atrophy in adulthood.

Stillness is not made by force, by straining, irritably;
when you build the environment physiologically like this, it settles in by itself.
