## The method for opening the bright nature of the chest
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122705308565008802) (quotes [the Nov 14 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2121536288356798663))

The method for opening the bright nature of the chest. The energy of Mingmen (명문 命門) comes to the chest, the still center. Since energy gathers where it finds stability, the chest must be still.

The energy gathered this way travels on the in-breath along the breastbone to Lingtai (영대 靈臺) at T5.

The energy that went to Lingtai goes on the out-breath in a straight line from Lingtai to the center.

In this way you let the energy of the front and back of the body communicate and purify the karmic imprints (업식 業識) in the breastbone.

When Lingtai is activated there is pain.

## How the bright nature falls into ignorance
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122549418721476779) (quotes [a post by another account](https://x.com/i/status/2122209587147501767))

The bright nature falls into ignorance (무명 無明) within the utterly biological basic motive of pleasure and displeasure. And so one loses the original nature. This reminds me of Antonio Damasio's discussion.

*(Translator's note: this quotes a Japanese post by another account on the Buddhist view of samsara as the persistence of the conditions that produce suffering: ignorance, craving, clinging.)*
