## Zhongji: the switch for the fourth dhyāna
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2126079015685627940) (quotes [the Nov 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122855871235674340))

In traditional Buddhism the fourth dhyāna is the stage of absolute peace (purity of mindfulness through equanimity, 사념청정 捨念淸淨) in which the in-breath and out-breath stop (breath abiding, 식주 息住) and even pleasure and pain are entirely cut off. Physiologically, in terms of practice, Zhongji (중극 中極; the ventral face of the spinal cord at T5) sits at the switch that creates this state.
