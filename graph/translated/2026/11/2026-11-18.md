## A practice the Dragon Spirit is said to have taught: rib purification
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2123057544998568036)

Usually, when emotion surges, people say, "Breathe in deeply and send it down to the lower belly." But if you force the breath down while the chest (the middle dantian 중단전 中丹田 and the ribs) and the organs (장부 臟腑) have not been purified, side effects arise.

The passion lodged in the chest (heat, sympathetic arousal) is pressed hard from top to bottom without ever being resolved, so instead the belly gets bloated, or the pressure rebounds and shoots up into the head: the rising-qi (상기 上氣) phenomenon.

As a result, the emotional wave that arose in the front of the chest easily gets trapped as it is in the intercostal nerves and rib fascia on both sides, and stiffens into a dull ache.

Rather than sending it down, try breastbone purification.

Instead of stamping emotion down, you open a horizontal rotating circuit that crosses the cylinder of the ribcage from the front (the center, 중심) to the back (Lingtai 영대 靈臺), and refine the energy.

The congestion, the density of passion, lodged in the front of the chest (the center) rides the in-breath as the ribcage widens, travels along the outer edges of the ribs on both sides, and is scattered to Lingtai at the back (around the 5th thoracic vertebra).

Because this spreads emotional energy that was clumped in one spot and about to explode across the broad surface of the left and right ribs, and drains it out through the spinal line at the back, the chest opens up refreshingly at once, without suppression.

On the out-breath, the qi gathered at Lingtai at the back (around T5, where sympathetic ganglia are dense and passion is stored) is drawn back into the center through the straight midline passage, together with the out-breath (and the sounding of "Om").

As this round trip repeats, the emotional turbid qi (탁기 濁氣) wedged in the ribs and the coarse heat of the sympathetic system are washed out, and a firm stillness that outside stimuli cannot shake (the iron wall of no-mind → breaking through → the arising of heart-fire) settles in the center.

When the front-to-back rotation of the ribcage (rib purification) is complete in this way, the ground of the chest is emptied like the void and a warm heart-fire (심화 心火) arises. Then, without any straining to send it down, that soft energy flows down by itself, like the law of gravity, to the lower belly (the lower dantian 하단전 下丹田, the Yellow Court 황정 黃庭).

## Making the passage between the lower dantian and Mingmen
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122923788211986645) (quotes [the Nov 18 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122855871235674340))

On the in-breath, raise the energy gathered in the lower dantian below the navel to Mingmen at L2–L3; on the out-breath, send the energy out from Mingmen to the lower dantian. In this way you make a passage along which the dragon (the energy) comes and goes between Mingmen and the lower dantian.

As the energy comes and goes like this, it stimulates the kidneys, kidney-fire energy comes out of the kidneys, and, uniting with the Former Heaven qi of Mingmen, it forms the Yellow Court (황정 黃庭), where kundalini becomes active. It is the place in front of Mingmen, also called the true-qi point (진기혈 眞氣穴).

## [Korean Meditation] A practice that goes beyond meditation to change the structure of the body
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2122855871235674340)

Many meditation methods stop at watching thoughts or the breath. But Korea's Guseon (구선스님) unravels practice not as a concept but as a thorough "engineering of bioenergy and the purification of the skeleton."

This is because of his insight that emotions and karmic imprints (업식 業識), suppressed for a long time, are physically engraved beyond the organs (장기 臟器) into the bones, the spine and even the tailbone. Follow the core roadmap of "The method of contemplation (관 觀) using qi (기 氣)," printed in the appendix of his book, and a clear map is drawn of how the unmoving mind and the Yellow Court (황정 黃庭), the inner brazier, are established.

1. The great principle of practice: the organs (장부 臟腑) must be cleared first before the bones open

The five-stage flow of the whole practice that Guseon presents is as follows: Om (OM) practice → purification of the organs → formation of the center → deliverance of karmic imprints (purification of the bones) → progress of the center.

There is an order here that must be kept. The turbid energy of the organs (yin qi) is discharged outward through the meridians; if you force practices that open the bones while the organs are not yet purified, the yin qi of the organs flows back into the bones and causes terrible pain. So the first button is to loosen the five organs and six bowels with the vibration of a low "Om" and to establish a still "center" in the chest ground (가슴바탕; 1 cm above the solar plexus and 5 cm into the chest), free of any wavering of emotion.

2. Opening the five hearts (오심 五心) and purifying the spine: making the body one "empty tube"

When the chest ground has become still and you can watch the outer world and your inner center at the same time (separation of consciousness), the purification of the bones finally begins. The order is vertebrae → ribs → tailbone → skull.

The first gates to open in order to bore through the spine are the five hearts (오심 五心): the two palms (Laogong, 노궁), the two soles (Yongquan, 용천) and the crown (Baihui, 백회).

The palms (Laogong) and Mingmen: feel the opening in the middle of the palm through the vibration of the fingertips and palm, gather the energy like a ping-pong ball, and on the in-breath draw it along the arm bones, shoulders and Dazhui to Mingmen at the back (L2–L3). (Here the thumb is linked directly to the vibration of the lungs, and the index finger to that of the large intestine.)

The soles (Yongquan) and Mingmen: lying down with the body relaxed, push the hot heat of Mingmen down to the soles on the out-breath, and when a pulsing vibration forms in the soles, draw it back up to Mingmen on the in-breath.

Baihui and Mingmen: on the in-breath, draw the vibration of the crown straight down the exact middle of the spine to Mingmen.

Once all five gates are open, you no longer put your will on individual points. Treating the whole spine as one hollow tube, with each in-breath you let the energy of the whole body be drawn in through the spine to Mingmen and hang there like a ball.

3. Rib purification and the birth of heart-fire (심화 心火): tearing down the iron wall of the mind

When energy has condensed in Mingmen like a ball and the stillness of the chest ground is stable enough to overpower that energy, the Former Heaven qi of Mingmen flows through Lingtai at the back (around T5) into the center of the chest.

At this point the chest fills with energy and you feel a tautness as if a solid "iron wall" (철벽 鐵壁; a wall to face, 면벽 面壁) had been raised. Whatever outer situation comes, you simply keep watching this wall in the chest in silence and wait for it to crack.

Now add the breath.

In-breath: with intention, divide the energy of the chest into the ribs on both sides and send it to Lingtai at the back. With a ringing thrill, the area around Lingtai tightens.

Out-breath: from Lingtai as the base, voice "Om" toward the middle dantian and draw the energy of Lingtai into the middle of the chest.

As you repeat this, at some moment the solid iron wall bursts open and the chest ground is emptied like open sky. And in that empty place a soft, warm energy, heart-fire (심화 心火), blooms and seeps down into the lower belly like a spring breeze. This is the moment the five stages of the progress of the chest ground (stillness → penetrating equanimity → like an iron wall → bursting open → the birth of heart-fire) are complete.

4. Purifying the tailbone and building the Yellow Court (황정 黃庭): the furnace that melts all afflictions

Guseon sees the tailbone as the place where the primal survival instinct, the will to fight and sexual desire left in human beings are stored, along with the karmic imprints that overflow from the spine. The final energy center built to purify the turbid qi of this tailbone is exactly the Yellow Court.

The key to building the Yellow Court lies in the "lower-dantian Om" and the "union of heart-fire and Former Heaven qi."

The position of the tongue tip: lightly touch the tip of the tongue to the inside of the lower gums and voice "Om" in a low tone so that the lower belly rings, making the lower dantian and Mingmen at the back resonate.

The crossing of in-breath and out-breath: when hot Former Heaven qi hangs in Mingmen, draw that heat to the lower dantian on the in-breath and heat it like a ball of fire, and on the out-breath send it back to Mingmen with the lower-dantian "Om."

The place where it "catches" with a click: as you repeat sending the warm heart-fire that came down from the chest to Mingmen, a point appears where the kidneys pull the energy and it catches with a "tak—."

This is exactly the Yellow Court, the furnace of life force in which four energies fuse into one: Later Heaven qi (the breath) + Former Heaven qi (Mingmen) + heart-fire (the chest) + kidney fire (the kidneys).

5. Life after the Yellow Court: a brazier on which snowflakes melt

Once the Yellow Court is established, the landscape of practice changes completely. Even when all kinds of emotional states rise, anger, jealousy, affection, fear, they are no more than a single snowflake falling in front of a blazing brazier (the Yellow Court). They melt without a trace before they even touch it.

Guseon says that at this stage there is no need even to work hard at "Om" practice separately; you only need to make it resonate with the ringing of the lower dantian about once every thirty minutes, and, even while walking through daily life, gaze gently at the Yellow Court alone as if holding a baby.

In the end, practice is not an austerity of suppressing thought. It is the work of boring through the blocked paths of the five hearts and the spine, the ribs and the tailbone, and lighting one warm brazier that never goes out in the very middle of your body.
