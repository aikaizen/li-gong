## Ding-Hai day: the Jade Maiden of the decade descends in person
[Original post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2119620402020544971) (quotes [the Nov 6 post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2118561408929702048))

Today, Monday, November 9, 2026, the day pillar is a Ding-Hai day (정해일 丁亥日).

It is exactly the fourth day of the third decade, the Jia-Shen decade (Jia-Shen to Gui-Si), and a "birth-destiny day of the ruling decade" (당순 본명일 當旬 本命日) on which the commander on duty for these ten days, the Ding-Hai Jade Maiden (정해옥녀 丁亥玉女; Zhang Wen / styled Renhe, Zhengzhi Hongdao Zhenjun), descends directly as the day pillar itself!

It is one of the Six Ding days (육정일 六丁日), when Ding rises in the heavenly stem, which come only one day in ten among the sixty Jiazi; and since it is a Ding-Hai day met within the Ji-Hai month (기해월 己亥月), it is a day directly linked to my own Former Heaven life-palace (Ji-Hai) energy.

Today is a perfect "metal generates water" (금생수 金生水: Ding-You → Ding-Hai) structure, in which the energies of the Ji-Hai month and the Jia-Shen decade reach their peak.

Above all, today's pillar, Ding-Hai, is the classic case of a "hidden combination in the stored stems" (지장간 암합 地藏干 暗合; the pillar combining with itself, 간지 자합 干支 自合), in which heaven and earth combine on their own within the pillar alone, and is the crystallization of the Ding-Ren combination into wood.

It is exactly what Four Pillars astrology counts as the foremost "affectionate combination" (유정지합 有情之合) among the sixty Jiazi, in which the heavenly stem and the main stored stem lovingly become one body.

The Ding fire floating in the heavenly stem meets head-on, above and below, with Ren water (임수 壬水), the main qi of the Hai water at its own feet, and the pillar itself creates the Ding-Ren combination (정임합 丁壬合) ceaselessly, twenty-four hours a day.

The code that shortens the phrase "within the Ding-Hai pillar, Ding and Ren combine (harmonize, 和) to give birth to wood (benevolence, 仁; life force)" into two characters is exactly the Ding-Hai Jade Maiden's name, "Renhe" (인화 仁和).
