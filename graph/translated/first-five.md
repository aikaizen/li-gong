# First five teachings, translated

These are the five most recent teaching posts by 마리지천 摩利支天 ([@z9_s8_y_9](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9)), newest first. Dates are in Korea time. Key terms keep the original Korean or Hanja in parentheses.

**Hand-seal key used throughout (신기문 身奇門):** each hand forms a trigram by touching fingers to the thumb. Index, middle, and ring finger stand for the bottom, middle, and top line. A finger touching the thumb is a solid (yang) line, and a lifted finger is a broken (yin) line. So Qian ☰ is all three fingers on the thumb, Li ☲ is index and ring on with the middle lifted, and Gen ☶ is the index alone on the thumb. Two hands together make a hexagram: the right hand gives the lower trigram and the left hand the upper.

## 4. Why Ding-Chou can be auspicious or ominous, and the hand-seal sequence
[2026-10-30 · post](https://x.com/z9_s8_y_9/status/2116016581625221141)

Today, a Ding-Chou day, is one of the seven great White Tiger days (백호살) among the sixty cycle days. Its energy is condensed to the extreme. Maybe that's why the market is in such chaos.

In Body-Qimen, the Jade Maiden (옥녀 玉女) carries the energy of Ding-fire. She gently melts the Geng-metal energy that attacks Jia-wood (갑목 甲木).

So depending on the earthly branch it meets, Ding-fire can create an auspicious formation (길격 吉格), or an ominous one (흉격 凶格) in which nothing works.

The mechanism in one sentence: "When Ding-fire (丁火), the moving starlight, enters a particular direction (branch) of the nine palaces, it meets the heavenly stem already lying at the bottom of that direction, the hidden stem (지장간 地藏干), and automatically sparks an auspicious formation (a chemical spark)."

Everything depends on the bond Ding forms with the palace's owner (its stem) at the moment it settles into that palace (branch).

In Body-Qimen this is done with hand seals (수인 手印):

- **Left hand holds Former-Heaven Li ☲** = switch on the heavenly Ding-fire (丁火 · Jade Maiden).
- **Right hand holds one of the six Later-Heaven palaces** (Zhen, Xun, Kun, Dui, Qian, Gen) = call up the hidden stem concealed in that palace's branch (Mao, Si, Wei, You, Hai, Chou).
- **The two hands interlock** = Ding plus the hidden stem combine, and Qimen Dunjia's six great auspicious formations activate inside your own body.

**Why is it sometimes auspicious and sometimes ominous?**

Looking only at the stems, Ding + Xin (丁+辛), Ding + Gui (丁+癸), or Ding-fire entering the Gen palace (Chou-earth), which is called "striking punishment" (격형 擊刑), are classed in space-time Qimen Dunjia as difficult or ominous.

Ding-Chou is the image of Ding-fire, the starlight and candle flame of heaven, set on top of Chou-earth, the frozen winter mud of the ground and the storehouse of metal.

You *can* use the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden as a great auspicious formation: "lighting a lamp to open a dark treasure vault." But if your body's condition (its fire timing, 화후 火候) or the order in which you use the energies is off, Ding-Chou turns into one of the hardest ominous formations in Qimen, destiny reading (명리), and neidan alike.

**1) Fire generates earth, but not every earth welcomes fire.**

- *The nature of Chou-earth:* the Chou month, which holds Minor Cold and Great Cold, is the coldest, darkest stretch of deep winter: frozen, waterlogged mud (습토 濕土).
- *The flame suffocates (회화 晦火):* if the basic yang-qi of the lower belly (lower dantian; Qian-metal ☰) has not been warmed up at all, and you rashly take only the Ding-Chou seal (left Li ☲ + right Gen ☶), the little candle (Ding-fire) is crushed by the damp of the cold mud. It loses its light, smokes, and goes out.
- *Ding-fire enters the tomb (입묘 入墓):* by the twelve life stages, Chou is Ding-fire's tomb (묘지 墓地), where its energy goes into the grave. Go in unprepared and instead of opening the vault, the starlight of your own awareness (the Jade Maiden) gets trapped in a cold tomb. You fall into lethargy, low mood, or deep exhaustion (a drained battery).

**2) Open the belly of the character Chou (its hidden stems)** and three things lie coiled there: Gui-water (癸水, cold rain), Xin-metal (辛金, a sharp gem or needle), and Ji-earth (己土, cloud or damp soil).

If Ding enters Chou with no protective shield, two Qimen ominous formations go off inside the hidden stems at once:

1. **Inner "Red Bird plunges into the river" (주작투강 朱雀投江, Ding + Gui):** the cold Gui-water hidden in Chou-earth rises and smothers Ding-fire's spark (water overcomes fire). In neidan practice this is when cold water (한수 寒水) in the lower belly puts out the faint warmth at the solar plexus. The belly goes cold, with diarrhea or indigestion.
2. **Inner "fire and metal at war" (화금상전 火金相戰, Ding + Xin):** if Ding touches the Xin-metal inside Chou-earth with weak, clumsy fire, it does not refine the gem. It only coats it in soot, and they clash in sparks (fire overcoming metal, backfiring). Qimen reads this as an ominous formation of getting tangled in disputes with a criminal (Xin-metal).

Ding-Chou is one of the seven great White Tiger days, its energy condensed to the extreme. When this high-pressure energy is well gathered in through neidan, it becomes explosive drive and the power to open the treasury (재물 개고 開庫). When control is lost, it shows up as side effects of qi and blood flowing backward in the body: rising blood pressure, sudden anger, muscle cramps, or nerve pain.

In the end, Ding-Chou turns ominous for one reason only: "you shoved a bare little candle (Ding-fire) into a cold, murky storehouse (Chou-earth)."

That is exactly why, when I gave today's Ding-Chou seal sequence earlier, I stressed that you must not go straight to *Bi* (山火賁 ䷕: Ding-Chou). You must first lay down *Great Taming* (山天大畜 ䷙) and then draw it out afterward with *Xian* (澤山咸 ䷞)!

- **Pre-warming (prevents damp earth smothering fire and the Red Bird plunging):** *Great Taming* ䷙, right hand Gen ☶ / left hand Qian ☰. Close all the fingers of the left hand and first shoot the pure yang of the sun (Qian ☰) into palace 8, Gen. The frozen mud and cold water (Gui) of Chou-earth dry out warmly, making a warm hearth where the Jade Maiden's fire will never go out.
- **Feeding in the Jade Maiden (refining the vault):** *Bi* ䷕, right hand Gen ☶ / left hand Li ☲. With the lower belly warm, lift only the left middle finger to switch on Ding-fire (Li ☲). The ominous formations (striking punishment, entering the tomb) vanish. Only the vault inside Chou-earth opens, safely, and the great auspicious formation "Jade Maiden in hidden treasure · opening the Chou-Wei storehouse" (옥녀암장 · 축미개고) starts working.

Put only fire (Li ☲) into palace 8 (Gen) with an empty lower belly and you get "striking punishment," and the energy breaks. But first seal palace 8 shut and fill it with energy, using *Great Taming* ䷙ or *Decrease* (山澤損 ䷨) as we learned before, then switch to *Bi* ䷕. It transforms completely into an auspicious formation: melting the mountain's vault (Chou-earth, the storehouse of metal) with Ding's fire and digging out the gold.

## 5. Ding-Chou day: the three-step golden sequence

From the view of Qimen and neidan, though, it is the day when the heavenly starlight, Ding-fire (丁火 · Jade Maiden), comes down in person into Chou-earth (丑土 · palace 8, Gen), the dark, cold winter ground and the great storehouse (묘고 墓庫) of metal (wealth and fruition). She melts the vault door and refines the gems.

In space-time Qimen Dunjia, if Ding enters palace 8 (Chou-earth) unguarded, the light gets trapped in cold damp earth and the energy breaks: the Six Wonders Striking Punishment formation (육기격형 六奇擊刑).

So to take today's Ding-Chou wave fully into your body as the great auspicious formation "Jade Maiden in hidden treasure · opening the Chou-Wei storehouse," and not as an ominous one (stuffiness, depletion), you must use the three-step seal of "store qi first, refine after" (선축기 후제련 先蓄氣 後製煉). It always fills palace 8 with pure-yang energy *before* putting in fire (Li ☲).

**The three-step golden sequence for the Ding-Chou day, to hold often throughout the day** (the last step is kept private)

During the day or over tea, place your awareness on the lower right belly (palace 8, Gen) and below the navel (palace 5, center), then touch your fingers in this order:

**Step 1: Guard against striking punishment and fill the vault.** *Great Taming* (山天大畜 ䷙)
- *Seals:* right hand Gen (☶: index alone on the thumb) + left hand Qian (☰: index, middle and ring all on).
- *Action:* so the Jade Maiden's little candle (Ding-fire) is not put out by Chou-earth's cold, first fill palace 8 (right-hand Gen ☶) completely with the pure-yang heavenly metal of the left hand (Qian ☰). This warms the lower belly and makes it heavy and solid ("heaven within the mountain," 천재산중 天在山中).

**Step 2: Synchronize with today's protagonist, the Ding-Chou Jade Maiden.** *Bi* (山火賁 ䷕)

- *Action:* while the right hand keeps holding palace 8 (Chou-earth), change only the left hand into Li ☲ (Ding-fire) by flicking off the one middle finger, the control.
- *Resonance point:* at this moment the seal inside your body locks onto the same frequency as today's day pillar, Ding-Chou (left hand Ding-fire 丁 + right hand Chou-earth 丑)! Between the lower right belly (palace 8, Gen) and below the solar plexus (palace 9, Li), a soft, warm starlight (*Bi* ䷕) lights up, and the vault inside Chou-earth (the Xin-metal gems) begins to shine brilliantly.
